001:014 and makest men as the fishes of the sea, as the creeping things, that have no ruler over them?

002:009 ¶ Woe to him that getteth an evil gain for his house, that he may set his nest on high, that he may be delivered from the hand of evil!

002:011 For the stone shall cry out of the wall, and the beam out of the timber shall answer it.
002:012 ¶ Woe to him that buildeth a town with blood, and establisheth a city by iniquity!
002:013 Behold, is it not of Jehovah of hosts that the peoples labor for the fire, and the nations weary themselves for vanity?

002:017 For the violence done to Lebanon shall cover thee, and the destruction of the beasts, which made them afraid; because of men’s blood, and for the violence done to the land, to the city and to all that dwell therein.
002:018 ¶ What profiteth the graven image, that the maker thereof hath graven it; the molten image, even the teacher of lies, that he that fashioneth its form trusteth therein, to make dumb idols?
002:019 Woe unto him that saith to the wood, Awake; to the dumb stone, Arise! Shall this teach? Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver, and there is no breath at all in the midst of it.
