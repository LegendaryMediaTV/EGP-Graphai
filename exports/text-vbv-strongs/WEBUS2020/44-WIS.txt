001:001 ¶ °{The Wisdom of Solomon is recognized as Deuterocanonical Scripture by the Roman Catholic, Greek Orthodox, and Russian Orthodox Churches.}Love righteousness, all you who are judges of the earth.␤Think of the Lord°{Gr. in goodness.} with a good mind.␤Seek him in singleness of heart,␤
001:002 because he is found by those who don’t put him to the test,␤and is manifested to those who trust him.␤
001:003 for crooked thoughts separate from God.␤His Power convicts when it is tested,␤and exposes the foolish;␤
001:004 because wisdom will not enter into a soul that devises evil,␤nor dwell in a body that is enslaved by sin.␤
001:005 For a holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit,␤and will depart from thoughts that are without understanding,␤and will be ashamed when unrighteousness has come in.
001:006 ¶ For°{Some authorities read the spirit of wisdom is loving to man.} wisdom is a spirit who loves man,␤and she will not hold a°{Or, reviler} blasphemer guiltless for his lips,␤because God is witness of his inmost self,␤and is a true overseer of his heart,␤and a hearer of his tongue.␤
001:007 Because the spirit of the Lord has filled°{Gr. the inhabited earth.} the world,␤and that which holds all things together knows what is said.␤
001:008 Therefore no one who utters unrighteous things will be unseen;␤neither will Justice, when it convicts, pass him by.␤
001:009 For in his counsels the ungodly will be searched out,␤and the sound of his words will come to the Lord to bring his lawless deeds to conviction;␤
001:010 because a jealous ear listens to all things,␤and the noise of murmurings is not hidden.␤
001:011 Beware then of unprofitable murmuring,␤and keep your tongue from slander;␤because no secret utterance will go on its way void,␤and a lying mouth destroys a soul.␤
001:012 Don’t court death in the error of your life.␤Don’t draw destruction upon yourselves by the works of your hands;␤
001:013 because God didn’t make death,␤neither does he delight when the living perish.␤
001:014 For he created all things that they might have being.␤The generative powers of the world are wholesome,␤and there is no poison of destruction in them,␤nor has Hades°{Or, a royal house} royal dominion upon earth;␤
001:015 for righteousness is immortal,␤
001:016 but ungodly men by their hands and their words summon death;␤deeming him a friend they°{Or, were consumed with love of him} pined away.␤They made a covenant with him,␤because they are worthy to belong with him.
002:001 ¶ For they said°{Or, among} within themselves, with unsound reasoning,␤“Our life is short and sorrowful.␤There is no healing when a man comes to his end,␤and no one was ever known who°{Or, returned out of Hades} was released from Hades.␤
002:002 Because we were born by mere chance,␤and hereafter we will be as though we had never been,␤because the breath in our nostrils is smoke,␤and reason is a spark kindled by the beating of our heart,␤
002:003 which being extinguished, the body will be turned into ashes,␤and the spirit will be dispersed as thin air.␤
002:004 Our name will be forgotten in time.␤No one will remember our works.␤Our life will pass away as the traces of a cloud,␤and will be scattered as is a mist,␤when it is chased by the rays of the sun,␤and°{Gr. weighed down.} overcome by its heat.␤
002:005 For our allotted time is the passing of a shadow,␤and our end doesn’t retreat,␤because it is securely sealed, and no one°{Or, comes again} turns it back.
002:006 ¶ “Come therefore and let’s enjoy the good things that exist.␤Let’s use the creation earnestly as in our youth.␤
002:007 Let’s fill ourselves with costly wine and perfumes,␤and let no spring flower pass us by.␤
002:008 Let’s crown ourselves with rosebuds before they wither.␤
002:009 Let none of us go without his share in our proud revelry.␤Let’s leave tokens of mirth everywhere,␤because this is our portion, and this is our lot.␤
002:010 Let’s oppress the righteous poor.␤Let’s not spare the widow,␤nor regard the gray hair of the old man.␤
002:011 But let our strength be a law of righteousness;␤for that which is weak is proven useless.␤
002:012 But let’s lie in wait for the righteous man,␤because he annoys us,␤is contrary to our works,␤reproaches us with sins against the law,␤and charges us with sins against our training.␤
002:013 He professes to have knowledge of God,␤and calls himself a child of the Lord.␤
002:014 He became to us a reproof of our thoughts.␤
002:015 He is grievous to us even to look at,␤because his life is unlike other men’s,␤and his paths are strange.␤
002:016 We were regarded by him as something worthless,␤and he abstains from our ways as from uncleanness.␤He calls the latter end of the righteous happy.␤He boasts that God is his father.␤
002:017 Let’s see if his words are true.␤Let’s test what will happen at the end of his life.␤
002:018 For if the righteous man is God’s son, he will uphold him,␤and he will deliver him out of the hand of his adversaries.␤
002:019 Let’s test him with insult and torture,␤that we may find out how gentle he is,␤and test his patience.␤
002:020 Let’s condemn him to a shameful death,␤for he will be protected, according to his words.”
002:021 ¶ Thus they reasoned, and they were led astray;␤for their wickedness blinded them,␤
002:022 and they didn’t know the mysteries of God,␤neither did they hope for wages of holiness,␤nor did they discern that there is a prize for blameless souls.␤
002:023 Because God created man for incorruption,␤and made him an image of his own everlastingness;␤
002:024 but death entered into the world by the envy of the devil,␤and those who belong to him experience it.
003:001 ¶ But the souls of the righteous are in the hand of God,␤and no torment will touch them.␤
003:002 In the eyes of the foolish they seemed to have died.␤Their departure was considered a disaster,␤
003:003 and their travel away from us ruin,␤but they are in peace.␤
003:004 For even if in the sight of men they are punished,␤their hope is full of immortality.␤
003:005 Having borne a little chastening, they will receive great good;␤because God tested them, and found them worthy of himself.␤
003:006 He tested them like gold in the furnace,␤and he accepted them as a whole burnt offering.␤
003:007 In the time of their visitation they will shine.␤They will run back and forth like sparks among stubble.␤
003:008 They will judge nations and have dominion over peoples.␤The Lord will reign over them forever.␤
003:009 Those who trust him will understand truth.␤The faithful will live with him in love,␤because grace and mercy are with his chosen ones.
003:010 ¶ But the ungodly will be punished even as their reasoning deserves,␤those who neglected righteousness and revolted from the Lord;␤
003:011 for he who despises wisdom and discipline is miserable.␤Their hope is void and their toils unprofitable.␤Their works are useless.␤
003:012 Their wives are foolish and their children are wicked.␤
003:013 Their descendants are cursed.␤For the barren woman who is undefiled is happy,␤she who has not conceived in transgression.␤She will have fruit when God examines souls.␤
003:014 So is the eunuch which has done no lawless deed with his hands,␤nor imagined wicked things against the Lord;␤for a precious gift will be given to him for his faithfulness,␤and a delightful inheritance in the Lord’s sanctuary.␤
003:015 For good labors have fruit of great renown.␤The root of understanding can’t fail.␤
003:016 But children of adulterers will not come to maturity.␤The seed of an unlawful union will vanish away.␤
003:017 For if they live long, they will not be esteemed,␤and in the end, their old age will be without honor.␤
003:018 If they die young, they will have no hope,␤nor consolation in the day of judgment.␤
003:019 For the end of an unrighteous generation is always grievous.
004:001 ¶ It is better to be childless with virtue,␤for immortality is in the memory of virtue,␤because it is recognized both before God and before men.␤
004:002 When it is present, people imitate it.␤They long after it when it has departed.␤Throughout all time it marches, crowned in triumph,␤victorious in the competition for the prizes that are undefiled.␤
004:003 But the multiplying brood of the ungodly will be of no profit,␤and their illegitimate offshoots won’t take deep root,␤nor will they establish a sure hold.␤
004:004 For even if they grow branches and flourish for a season,␤standing unsure, they will be shaken by the wind.␤They will be uprooted by the violence of winds.␤
004:005 Their branches will be broken off before they come to maturity.␤Their fruit will be useless,␤never ripe to eat, and fit for nothing.␤
004:006 For unlawfully conceived children are witnesses of wickedness␤against parents when they are investigated.
004:007 ¶ But a righteous man, even if he dies before his time, will be at rest.␤
004:008 For honorable old age is not that which stands in length of time,␤nor is its measure given by number of years,␤
004:009 but understanding is gray hair to men,␤and an unspotted life is ripe old age.
004:010 ¶ Being found well-pleasing to God, someone was loved.␤While living among sinners he was transported.␤
004:011 He was caught away, lest evil should change his understanding,␤or guile deceive his soul.␤
004:012 For the fascination of wickedness obscures the things which are good,␤and the whirl of desire perverts an innocent mind.␤
004:013 Being made perfect quickly,␤he filled a long time;␤
004:014 for his soul was pleasing to the Lord.␤Therefore he hurried out of the midst of wickedness.␤
004:015 But the peoples saw and didn’t understand,␤not considering this, that grace and mercy are with his chosen,␤and that he visits his holy ones;␤
004:016 but a righteous man who is dead will condemn the ungodly who are living,␤and youth who is quickly perfected will condemn the many years of an unrighteous man’s old age.␤
004:017 For the ungodly will see a wise man’s end,␤and won’t understand what the Lord planned for him,␤and why he safely kept him.␤
004:018 They will see, and they will despise;␤but the Lord will laugh them to scorn.␤After this, they will become a dishonored carcass␤and a reproach among the dead forever;␤
004:019 because he will dash them speechless to the ground,␤and will shake them from the foundations.␤They will lie utterly waste.␤They will be in anguish␤and their memory will perish.
004:020 ¶ They will come with coward fear when their sins are counted.␤Their lawless deeds will convict them to their face.
005:001 ¶ Then the righteous man will stand in great boldness␤before the face of those who afflicted him,␤and those who make his labors of no account.␤
005:002 When they see him, they will be troubled with terrible fear,␤and will be amazed at the marvel of salvation.␤
005:003 They will speak among themselves repenting,␤and for distress of spirit they will groan,␤“This was he whom we used to hold in derision,␤as a parable of reproach.␤
005:004 We fools considered his life madness,␤and his end without honor.␤
005:005 How was he counted among sons of God?␤How is his lot among saints?␤
005:006 Truly we went astray from the way of truth.␤The light of righteousness didn’t shine for us.␤The sun didn’t rise for us.␤
005:007 We°{See Proverbs 14:14} took our fill of the paths of lawlessness and destruction.␤We traveled through trackless deserts,␤but we didn’t know the Lord’s way.␤
005:008 What did our arrogance profit us?␤What good have riches and boasting brought us?
005:009 ¶ Those things all passed away as a shadow,␤like a rumor that runs by,␤
005:010 like a ship passing through the billowy water,␤which, when it has gone by, there is no trace to be found,␤no pathway of its keel in the waves.␤
005:011 Or it is like when a bird flies through the air,␤no evidence of its passage is found,␤but the light wind, lashed with the stroke of its pinions,␤and torn apart with the violent rush of the moving wings, is passed through.␤Afterwards no sign of its coming remains.␤
005:012 Or it is like when an arrow is shot at a mark,␤the air it divided closes up again immediately,␤so that men don’t know where it passed through.␤
005:013 So we also, as soon as we were born, ceased to be;␤and we had no sign of virtue to show,␤but we were utterly consumed in our wickedness.”␤
005:014 Because the hope of the ungodly man is like chaff carried by the wind,␤and°{Gr. like foam chased to thinness: or, as thin foam chased.} as°{Most Greek authorities read hoar frost: some authorities, perhaps rightly, a spider’s web.} foam vanishing before a tempest;␤and is scattered like smoke by the wind,␤and passes by as the remembrance of a guest who stays just a day.
005:015 ¶ But the righteous live forever.␤Their reward is in the Lord,␤and the care for them with the Most High.␤
005:016 Therefore they will receive the crown of royal dignity␤and the diadem of beauty from the Lord’s hand,␤because he will cover them with his right hand,␤and he will shield them with his arm.␤
005:017 He will take his zeal as complete armor,␤and will make the whole creation his weapons to punish his enemies:␤
005:018 He will put on righteousness as a breastplate,␤and will wear impartial judgment as a helmet.␤
005:019 He will take holiness as an invincible shield.␤
005:020 He will sharpen stern wrath for a sword.␤The universe will go with him to fight against his frenzied foes.␤
005:021 Shafts of lightning will fly with true aim.␤They will leap to the mark from the clouds, as from a well-drawn bow.␤
005:022 Hailstones full of wrath will be hurled as from a catapult.␤The water of the sea will be angered against them.␤Rivers will sternly overwhelm them.␤
005:023 A mighty wind will encounter them.␤It will winnow them away like a tempest.␤So lawlessness will make all the land desolate.␤Their evil-doing will overturn the thrones of princes.
006:001 ¶ Hear therefore, you kings, and understand.␤Learn, you judges of the ends of the earth.␤
006:002 Give ear, you rulers who have dominion over many people,␤and make your boast°{Or, in the multitudes of your nations} in multitudes of nations,␤
006:003 because your dominion was given to you from the Lord,␤and your sovereignty from the Most High.␤He will search out your works,␤and will inquire about your plans,␤
006:004 because being officers of his kingdom, you didn’t judge rightly,␤nor did you keep the law,␤nor did you walk according to God’s counsel.␤
006:005 He will come upon you awfully and swiftly,␤because a stern judgment comes on those who are in high places.␤
006:006 For the man of low estate may be pardoned in mercy,␤but mighty men will be mightily tested.␤
006:007 For the Sovereign Lord of all will not be impressed with anyone,␤neither will he show deference to greatness;␤because it is he who made both small and great,␤and cares about them all;␤
006:008 but the scrutiny that comes upon the powerful is strict.␤
006:009 Therefore, my words are to you, O princes,␤that you may learn wisdom and not fall away.␤
006:010 For those who have kept the things that are holy in holiness will be made holy.␤Those who have been taught them will find what to say in defense.␤
006:011 Therefore set your desire on my words.␤Long for them, and you princes will be instructed.
006:012 ¶ Wisdom is radiant and doesn’t fade away;␤and is easily seen by those who love her,␤and found by those who seek her.␤
006:013 She anticipates those who desire her, making herself known.␤
006:014 He who rises up early to seek her won’t have difficulty,␤for he will find her sitting at his gates.␤
006:015 For to think upon her is perfection of understanding,␤and he who watches for her will quickly be free from care;␤
006:016 because she herself goes around, seeking those who are worthy of her,␤and in their paths she appears to them graciously,␤and in every purpose she meets them.␤
006:017 For her true beginning is desire for instruction;␤and desire for instruction is love.␤
006:018 And love is observance of her laws.␤To give heed to her laws confirms immortality.␤
006:019 Immortality brings closeness to God.␤
006:020 So then desire for wisdom promotes to a kingdom.
006:021 ¶ If therefore you delight in thrones and sceptres, you princes of peoples,␤honor wisdom, that you may reign forever.␤
006:022 But what wisdom is, and how she came into being, I will declare.␤I won’t hide mysteries from you;␤but I will explore from her first beginning,␤bring the knowledge of her into clear light,␤and I will not pass by the truth.␤
006:023 Indeed, I won’t travel with consuming envy,␤because envy will have no fellowship with wisdom.␤
006:024 But a multitude of wise men is salvation to the world,␤and an understanding king is stability for his people.␤
006:025 Therefore be instructed by my words, and you will profit.
007:001 ¶ I myself am also°{Many authorities read a mortal man.} mortal, like everyone else,␤and am a descendant of one formed first and born of the earth.␤
007:002 I molded into flesh in the time of ten months in my mother’s womb,␤being compacted in blood from the seed of man and pleasure of marriage.␤
007:003 I also, when I was born, drew in the common air,␤and fell upon the kindred earth,␤uttering, like all, for my first voice, the same cry.␤
007:004 I was nursed with care in swaddling clothes.␤
007:005 For no king had a different beginning,␤
007:006 but all men have one entrance into life, and a common departure.
007:007 ¶ For this cause I prayed, and understanding was given to me.␤I asked, and a spirit of wisdom came to me.␤
007:008 I preferred her before sceptres and thrones.␤I considered riches nothing in comparison to her.␤
007:009 Neither did I liken to her any priceless gem,␤because all gold in her presence is a little sand,␤and silver will be considered as clay before her.␤
007:010 I loved her more than health and beauty,␤and I chose to have her rather than light,␤because her bright shining is never laid to sleep.␤
007:011 All good things came to me with her,␤and innumerable riches are in her hands.␤
007:012 And I rejoiced over them all because wisdom leads them;␤although I didn’t know that she was their mother.␤
007:013 As I learned without guile, I impart without grudging.␤I don’t hide her riches.␤
007:014 For she is a treasure for men that doesn’t fail,␤and those who use it obtain friendship with God,␤commended by the gifts which they present through discipline.
007:015 ¶ But may God grant that I may speak his judgment,␤and to conceive thoughts worthy of what has been given me;␤because he is one who guides even wisdom and who corrects the wise.␤
007:016 For both we and our words are in his hand,␤with all understanding and skill in various crafts.␤
007:017 For he himself gave me an unerring knowledge of the things that are,␤to know the structure of the universe and the operation of the elements;␤
007:018 the beginning, end, and middle of times;␤the alternations of the solstices and the changes of seasons;␤
007:019 the circuits of years and the positions of stars;␤
007:020 the natures of living creatures and the raging of wild beasts;␤the violence of°{Or, spirits} winds and the thoughts of men;␤the diversities of plants and the virtues of roots.␤
007:021 All things that are either secret or manifest I learned,␤
007:022 for wisdom, that is the architect of all things, taught me. ¶ For there is in her a spirit that is quick to understand, holy,␤unique, manifold, subtle, freely moving, clear in utterance, unpolluted,␤distinct, invulnerable, loving what is good, keen, unhindered,␤
007:023 beneficent, loving toward man, steadfast, sure, free from care, all-powerful, all-surveying,␤and penetrating through all spirits that are quick to understand, pure, most subtle.␤
007:024 For wisdom is more mobile than any motion.␤Yes, she pervades and penetrates all things by reason of her purity.␤
007:025 For she is a breath of the power of God,␤and a pure emanation of the glory of the Almighty.␤Therefore nothing defiled can find entrance into her.␤
007:026 For she is a reflection of everlasting light,␤an unspotted mirror of the working of God,␤and an image of his goodness.␤
007:027 Although she is one, she has power to do all things.␤Remaining in herself, she renews all things.␤From generation to generation passing into holy souls,␤she makes friends of God and prophets.␤
007:028 For God loves nothing as much as one who dwells with wisdom.␤
007:029 For she is fairer than the sun,␤and above all the constellations of the stars.␤She is better than light.␤
007:030 For daylight yields to night,␤but evil does not prevail against wisdom.
008:001 ¶ But she reaches from one end to the other with full strength,␤and orders all things well.
008:002 ¶ I loved her and sought her from my youth.␤I sought to take her for my bride.␤I became enamoured by her beauty.␤
008:003 She glorifies her noble birth by living with God.␤The Sovereign Lord of all loves her.␤
008:004 For she is initiated into the knowledge of God,␤and she chooses his works.␤
008:005 But if riches are a desired possession in life,␤what is richer than wisdom, which makes all things?␤

008:007 If a man loves righteousness,␤the fruits of wisdom’s labor°{Gr. her labors} are virtues,␤for she teaches soberness, understanding, righteousness, and courage.␤There is nothing in life more profitable for people than these.␤
008:008 And if anyone longs for wide experience,␤she knows the things of old, and infers the things to come.␤She understands subtleties of speeches and interpretations of dark sayings.␤She foresees signs and wonders, and the issues of seasons and times.
008:009 ¶ Therefore I determined to take her to live with me,␤knowing that she is one who would give me good counsel,␤and encourage me in cares and grief.␤
008:010 Because of her, I will have glory among multitudes,␤and honor in the sight of elders, though I am young.␤
008:011 I will be found keen when I give judgment.␤I will be admired in the presence of rulers.␤
008:012 When I am silent, they will wait for me.␤When I open my lips, they will heed what I say.␤If I continue speaking, they will put their hands on their mouths.␤
008:013 Because of her, I will have immortality,␤and leave behind an eternal memory to those who come after me.␤
008:014 I will govern peoples.␤Nations will be subjected to me.␤

008:016 When I come into my house, I will find rest with her.␤For conversation with her has no bitterness,␤and living with her has no pain, but gladness and joy.␤

008:019 Now I was a clever child, and received a good soul.␤
