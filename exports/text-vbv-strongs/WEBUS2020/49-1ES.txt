001:004 He said, “You no longer need to carry it on your shoulders. Now therefore serve the Lord your God, and minister to his people Israel, and prepare yourselves by your fathers’ houses and kindred,
001:005 according to the writing of King David of Israel, and according to the magnificence of Solomon his son. Stand in the holy place according to the divisions of your Levite families who minister in the presence of your kindred the descendants of Israel.
001:006 Offer the Passover in order, prepare the sacrifices for your kindred, and keep the Passover according to the Lord’s commandment, which was given to Moses.
001:007 ¶ To the people which were present, Josias gave thirty thousand lambs and kids, and three thousand calves. These things were given from the king’s possessions, as he promised, to the people and to the priests and Levites.
001:008 Helkias, Zacharias, and °{Jehiel, 2 Chronicles 35:8.}Esyelus, the rulers of the temple, gave to the priests for the Passover two thousand six hundred sheep, and three hundred calves.
001:009 Jeconias, Samaias, Nathanael his brother, Sabias, Ochielus, and Joram, captains over thousands, gave to the Levites for the Passover five thousand sheep and seven hundred calves.
001:010 ¶ When these things were done, the priests and Levites, having the unleavened bread, stood in proper order according to the kindred,
001:011 and according to the several divisions by fathers’ houses, before the people, to offer to the Lord as it is written in the book of Moses. They did this in the morning.
001:012 They roasted the Passover lamb with fire, as required. They boiled the sacrifices in the brazen vessels and caldrons with a pleasing smell,
001:013 and set them before all the people. Afterward they prepared for themselves and for their kindred the priests, the sons of Aaron.
001:014 For the priests offered the fat until night. The Levites prepared for themselves and for their kindred the priests, the sons of Aaron.
001:015 The holy singers also, the sons of Asaph, were in their order, according to the appointment of David: Asaph, Zacharias, and Eddinus, who represented the king.
001:016 Moreover the gatekeepers were at every gate. No one needed to depart from his daily duties, for their kindred the Levites prepared for them.
001:017 ¶ So the things that belonged to the Lord’s sacrifices were accomplished in that day, in holding the Passover,
001:018 and offering sacrifices on the altar of the Lord, according to the commandment of King Josias.
001:019 So the children of Israel which were present at that time held the Passover and the feast of unleavened bread seven days.
001:020 Such a Passover had not been held in Israel since the time of the prophet Samuel.
001:021 Indeed, none of the kings of Israel held such a Passover as Josias with the priests, the Levites, and the Jews, held with all Israel that were present in their dwelling place at Jerusalem.
001:022 This Passover was held in the eighteenth year of the reign of Josias.
001:023 The works of Josias were upright before his Lord with a heart full of godliness.
001:024 Moreover the things that came to pass in his days have been written in times past, concerning those who sinned and did wickedly against the Lord more than any other people or kingdom, and how they grieved him °{Or, sensibly Judges 16:17.}exceedingly, so that the Lord’s words were confirmed against Israel.
001:025 ¶ °{2 Chronicles 35:20} Now after all these acts of Josias, it came to pass that Pharaoh the king of Egypt came to make war at Carchemish on the Euphrates; and Josias went out against him.
001:026 But the king of Egypt sent to him, saying, “What do I have to do with you, O king of Judea?
001:027 I wasn’t sent out from the Lord God against you, for my war is against the Euphrates. Now the Lord is with me, yes, the Lord is with me hastening me forward. Depart from me, and don’t be against the Lord.”
001:028 ¶ However, Josias didn’t turn back to his chariot, but tried to fight with him, not regarding the words of the prophet Jeremy from the Lord’s mouth,
001:029 but joined battle with him in the plain of Megiddo, and the commanders came down against King Josias.
001:030 Then the king said to his servants, “Carry me away out of the battle, for I am very weak!” Immediately his servants carried him away out of the army.
001:031 Then he got into his second chariot. After he was brought back to Jerusalem he died, and was buried in the tomb of his ancestors.
001:032 All Judea mourned for Josias. Jeremy the prophet lamented for Josias, and the chief men with the women made lamentation for him to this day. This was given out for an ordinance to be done continually in all the nation of Israel.
001:033 These things are written in the book of the histories of the kings of Judea, and every one of the acts that Josias did, and his glory, and his understanding in the law of the Lord, and the things that he had done before, and the things now told, are reported in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.

002:017 ¶ “To King Artaxerxes our Lord, from your servants, Rathumus the recorder, Samellius the scribe, and the rest of their council, and the judges who are in Coelesyria and Phoenicia:
002:018 Let it now be known to our lord the king, that the Jews that have come up from you to us, having come to Jerusalem, are building that rebellious and wicked city, and are repairing its marketplaces and walls, and are laying the foundation of a temple.

So the building of the temple in Jerusalem ceased until the second year of the reign of King Darius of the Persians.
003:001 ¶ Now King Darius made a great feast for all his subjects, for all who were born in his house, for all the princes of Media and of Persia,
003:002 and for all the local governors and captains and governors who were under him, from India to Ethiopia, in the one hundred twenty seven provinces.
003:003 They ate and drank, and when they were satisfied went home. Then King Darius went into his bedchamber and slept, but awakened out of his sleep.
003:004 ¶ Then the three young men of the bodyguard, who guarded the king, spoke one to another:
003:005 “Let every one of us state what one thing is strongest. King Darius will give him whose statement seems wiser than the others great gifts and great honors in token of victory.
003:006 He shall be clothed in purple, drink from gold cups, sleep on a gold bed, and have a chariot with bridles of gold, a fine linen turban, and a chain around his neck.
003:007 He shall sit next to Darius because of his wisdom, and shall be called cousin of Darius.”
003:008 ¶ Then they each wrote his sentence, sealed them, and laid them under King Darius’ pillow,
003:009 and said, “When the king wakes up, someone will give him the writing. Whoever the king and the three princes of Persia judge that his sentence is the wisest, to him shall the victory be given, as it is written.”
003:010 The first wrote, “Wine is the strongest.”
003:011 The second wrote, “The king is strongest.”
003:012 The third wrote, “Women are strongest, but above all things Truth is the victor.”
003:013 ¶ Now when the king woke up, they took the writing and gave it to him, so he read it.
003:014 Sending out, he called all the princes of Persia and of Media, the local governors, the captains, the governors, and the chief officers
003:015 and sat himself down in the royal seat of judgment; and the writing was read before them.
003:016 He said, “Call the young men, and they shall explain their own sentences. So they were called and came in.
003:017 They said to them, “Explain what you have written.” ¶ Then the first, who had spoken of the strength of wine, began
003:018 and said this: “O sirs, how exceedingly strong wine is! It causes all men who drink it to go astray.
003:019 It makes the mind of the king and of the fatherless child to be the same, likewise of the bondman and of the freeman, of the poor man and of the rich.
003:020 It also turns every thought into cheer and mirth, so that a man remembers neither sorrow nor debt.
003:021 It makes every heart rich, so that a man remembers neither king nor local governor. It makes people say things in large amounts.
003:022 When they are in their cups, they forget their love both to friends and kindred, and before long draw their swords.
003:023 But when they awake from their wine, they don’t remember what they have done.
003:024 O sirs, isn’t wine the strongest, seeing that it forces people to do this?” And when he had said this, he stopped speaking.
004:001 ¶ Then the second, who had spoken of the strength of the king, began to say,
004:002 “O sirs, don’t men excel in strength who rule over the sea and land, and all things in them?
004:003 But yet the king is stronger. He is their lord and has dominion over them. In whatever he commands them, they obey him.
004:004 If he tells them to make war against each other, they do it. If he sends them out against the enemies, they go, and conquer mountains, walls, and towers.
004:005 They kill and are killed, and don’t disobey the king’s commandment. If they win the victory, they bring everything to the king—all the plunder and everything else.
004:006 Likewise for those who are not soldiers, and don’t have anything to do with wars, but farm, when they have reaped again that which they had sown, they bring some to the king and compel one another to pay tribute to the king.
004:007 He is just one man! If he commands people to kill, they kill. If he commands them to spare, they spare.
004:008 If he commands them to strike, they strike. If he commands them to make desolate, they make desolate. If he commands to build, they build.
004:009 If he commands them to cut down, they cut down. If he commands them to plant, they plant.
004:010 So all his people and his armies obey him. Furthermore, he lies down, he eats and drinks, and takes his rest;
004:011 and these keep watch around him. None of them may depart and do his own business. They don’t disobey him in anything.
004:012 O sirs, how could the king not be the strongest, seeing that he is obeyed like this?” Then he stopped talking.
004:013 ¶ Then the third, who had spoken of women, and of truth, (this was Zorobabel) began to speak:
004:014 “O sirs, isn’t the king great, and men are many, and isn’t wine strong? Who is it then who rules them, or has the lordship over them? Aren’t they women?
004:015 Women have given birth to the king and all the people who rule over sea and land.
004:016 They came from women. Women nourished up those who planted the vineyards, from where the wine comes.
004:017 Women also make garments for men. These bring glory to men. Without women, men can’t exist.
004:018 Yes, and if men have gathered together gold and silver and any other beautiful thing, and see a woman who is lovely in appearance and beauty,
004:019 they let all those things go and gape at her, and with open mouth stare at her. They all have more desire for her than for gold, or silver, or any other beautiful thing.
004:020 A man leaves his own father who brought him up, leaves his own country, and joins with his wife.
004:021 With his wife he ends his days, with no thought for his father, mother, or country.
004:022 By this also you must know that women have dominion over you. Don’t you labor and toil, and bring it all to give to women?
004:023 Yes, a man takes his sword and goes out to travel, to rob, to steal, and to sail on the sea and on rivers.
004:024 He sees a lion and walks in the darkness. When he has stolen, plundered, and robbed, he brings it to the woman he loves.
004:025 Therefore a man loves his wife better than father or mother.
004:026 Yes, there are many who have lost their minds for women, and become slaves for their sakes.
004:027 Many also have perished, have stumbled, and sinned, for women.
004:028 Now don’t you believe me? Isn’t the king great in his power? Don’t all regions fear to touch him?
004:029 Yet I saw him and Apame the king’s concubine, the daughter of the illustrious Barticus, sitting at the right hand of the king,
004:030 and taking the crown from the king’s head, and setting it upon her own head. Yes, she struck the king with her left hand.
004:031 At this, the king gaped and gazed at her with open mouth. If she smiles at him, he laughs. But if she takes any displeasure at him, he flatters her, that she might be reconciled to him again.
004:032 O sirs, how can it not be that women are strong, seeing they do this?”
004:033 ¶ Then the king and the nobles looked at one another. So he began to speak concerning truth.
004:034 “O sirs, aren’t women strong? The earth is great. The sky is high. The sun is swift in its course, for it circles around the sky, and returns on its course again in one day.
004:035 Isn’t he who makes these things great? Therefore the truth is great, and stronger than all things.
004:036 All the earth calls upon truth, and the sky blesses truth. All works shake and tremble, but with truth there is no unrighteous thing.
004:037 Wine is unrighteous. The king is unrighteous. Women are unrighteous. All the children of men are unrighteous, and all their works are unrighteous. There is no truth in them. They shall also perish in their unrighteousness.
004:038 But truth remains, and is strong forever. Truth lives and conquers forevermore.
004:039 With truth there is no partiality toward persons or rewards, but truth does the things that are just, instead of any unrighteous or wicked things. All men approve truth’s works.
004:040 In truth’s judgment is not any unrighteousness. Truth is the strength, the kingdom, the power, and the majesty of all ages. Blessed be the God of truth!”
004:041 ¶ With that, he stopped speaking. Then all the people shouted and said, “Great is truth, and strong above all things!”
