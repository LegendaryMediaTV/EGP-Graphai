001:001 ¶ °{The book of Baruch is recognized as Deuterocanonical Scripture by the Roman Catholic, Greek Orthodox, and Russian Orthodox Churches. In some Bibles, Baruch chapter 6 is listed as a separate book called The Letter of Jeremiah, reflecting its separation from Baruch in some copies of the Greek Septuagint.}These are the words of the book which Baruch the son of Nerias, the son of Maaseas, the son of Sedekias, the son of Asadias, the son of Helkias, wrote in Babylon,
001:002 in the fifth year, in the seventh day of the month, at the time when the Chaldeans took Jerusalem and burned it with fire.
001:003 Baruch read the words of this book in the hearing of Jechonias the son of Joakim king of Judah, and in the hearing of all the people who came to hear the book,
001:004 and in the hearing of the mighty men, and of the kings’ sons, and in the hearing of the elders, and in the hearing of all the people, from the least to the greatest, even of all those who lived at Babylon by the river Sud.
001:005 Then they wept, fasted,°{Another reading is, and vowed vows.} and prayed before the Lord.
001:006 They also made a collection of money according to every man’s ability;
001:007 and they sent it to Jerusalem to Joakim the high priest, the son of Helkias, the son of Salom, and to the priests and to all the people who were found with him at Jerusalem,
001:008 at the same time when he took the vessels of the house of the Lord, that had been carried out of the temple, to return them into the land of Judah, the tenth day of Sivan—silver vessels which Sedekias the son of Josias king of Judah had made,
001:009 after Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon had carried away Jechonias, the princes, the captives, the mighty men, and the people of the land from Jerusalem, and brought them to Babylon.
001:010 ¶ And they said: Behold, we have sent you money; therefore buy with the money burnt offerings, sin offerings, and incense, and prepare an oblation, and offer upon the altar of the Lord our God;
001:011 and pray for the life of Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon, and for the life of Baltasar his son, that their days may be°{See Deuteronomy 11:21} as the days of heaven above the earth.
001:012 The Lord will give us strength and light to our eyes. We will live under the shadow of Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon and under the shadow of Baltasar his son, and we shall serve them many days, and find favor in their sight.
001:013 Pray for us also to the Lord our God, for we have sinned against the Lord our God. To this day the wrath of the Lord and his indignation is not turned from us.
001:014 You shall read this book which we have sent to you, to make confession in the house of the Lord upon the day of the feast and on the days of the solemn assembly.
001:015 ¶ You shall say: To the Lord our God belongs righteousness, but to us confusion of face, as at this day—to the men of Judah, to the inhabitants of Jerusalem,
001:016 to our kings, to our princes, to our priests, to our prophets, and to our fathers,
001:017 because we have sinned before the Lord.
001:018 We have disobeyed him and have not listened to the voice of the Lord our God, to walk in the commandments of the Lord that he has set before us.
001:019 Since the day that the Lord brought our fathers out of the land of Egypt to this present day, we have been disobedient to the Lord our God, and we have been negligent in not listening to his voice.
001:020 Therefore the plagues have clung to us, along with the curse which the Lord declared through Moses his servant in the day that he brought our fathers out of the land of Egypt to give us a land that flows with milk and honey, as at this day.
001:021 Nevertheless we didn’t listen to the voice of the Lord our God, according to all the words of the prophets whom he sent to us,
001:022 but we each walked in the imagination of his own wicked heart, to serve strange gods and to do what is evil in the sight of the Lord our God.
002:001 ¶ Therefore the Lord has made good his word which he pronounced against us, and against our judges who judged Israel, and against our kings, and against our princes, and against the men of Israel and Judah,
002:002 to bring upon us great plagues such as never happened before under the whole heaven,°{Another reading is, even as he has done.} as it came to pass in Jerusalem, according to the things that are written in the law of Moses,
002:003 that we should each eat the flesh of our own son, and each eat the flesh of our own daughter.
002:004 Moreover he has given them to be in subjection to all the kingdoms that are around us, to be a reproach and a desolation among all the people around us, where the Lord has scattered them.
002:005 Thus they were cast down and not exalted, because we sinned against the Lord our God in not listening to his voice.
002:006 To the Lord our God belongs righteousness, but to us and to our fathers confusion of face, as at this day.
002:007 All these plagues have come upon us which the Lord has pronounced against us.
002:008 Yet have we not entreated the favor of the Lord by everyone turning from the thoughts of his wicked heart.
002:009 Therefore the Lord has kept watch over the plagues. The Lord has brought them upon us, for the Lord is righteous in all his works which he has commanded us.
002:010 Yet we have not listened to his voice, to walk in the commandments of the Lord that he has set before us.
002:011 ¶ And now, O Lord, you God of Israel who have brought your people out of the land of Egypt with a mighty hand, with signs, with wonders, with great power, and with a high arm, and have gotten yourself a name, as at this day:
002:012 O Lord our God, we have sinned. We have been ungodly. We have done wrong in all your ordinances.
002:013 Let your wrath turn from us, for we are but a few left among the heathen where you have scattered us.
002:014 Hear our prayer, O Lord, and our petition, and deliver us for your own sake. Give us favor in the sight of those who have led us away captive,
002:015 that all the earth may know that you are the Lord our God, because Israel and his posterity is called by your name.
002:016 O Lord, look down from your holy house and consider us. Incline your ear, O Lord, and hear.
002:017 Open your eyes, and see; for the dead that are in Hades, whose breath is taken from their bodies, will give to the Lord neither glory nor righteousness;
002:018 but the soul who is greatly vexed, who goes stooping and feeble, and the eyes that fail, and the hungry soul, will declare your glory and righteousness, O Lord.
002:019 ¶ For we do not present our supplication before you, O Lord our God, for the righteousness of our fathers and of our kings.
002:020 For you have sent your wrath and your indignation upon us, as you have spoken by your servants the prophets, saying,
002:021 “The Lord says, ‘Bow your shoulders to serve the king of Babylon, and remain in the land that I gave to your fathers.
002:022 But if you won’t hear the voice of the Lord to serve the king of Babylon,
002:023 I will cause to cease out of the cities of Judah and from the region near Jerusalem the voice of mirth, the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride. The whole land will be desolate without inhabitant.’”
002:024 But we wouldn’t listen to your voice, to serve the king of Babylon. Therefore you have made good your words that you spoke by your servants the prophets, that the bones of our kings and the bones of our fathers would be taken out of their places.
002:025 Behold, they are cast out to the heat by day and to the frost by night. They died in great miseries by famine, by sword, and by°{See Jeremiah 32:36} pestilence.
002:026 You have made the house that is called by your name as it is today because of the wickedness of the house of Israel and the house of Judah.
002:027 ¶ Yet, O Lord our God, you have dealt with us after all your kindness and according to all your great mercy,
002:028 as you spoke by your servant Moses in the day when you commanded him to write your law in the presence of the children of Israel, saying,
002:029 “If you won’t hear my voice, surely this very great multitude will be turned into a small number among the nations where I will scatter them.
002:030 For I know that they will not hear me, because they are a stiff-necked people; but in the land of their captivity they will take it to heart,
002:031 and will know that I am the Lord their God. I will give them a heart and ears to hear.
002:032 Then they will praise me in the land of their captivity, and think about my name,
002:033 and will return from their stiff neck and from their wicked deeds; for they will remember the way of their fathers who sinned before the Lord.
002:034 I will bring them again into the land which I promised to their fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, and they will rule over it. I will increase them, and they won’t be diminished.
002:035 And I will make an everlasting covenant with them to be their God, and they will be my people. I will no more remove my people Israel out of the land that I have given them.”
003:001 ¶ O Lord Almighty, you God of Israel, the soul in anguish and the troubled spirit cries to you.
003:002 Hear, O Lord, and have mercy; for you are a merciful God. Yes, have mercy upon us, because we have sinned before you.
003:003 For you are enthroned forever, and we keep perishing.
003:004 O Lord Almighty, you God of Israel, hear now the prayer of the dead Israelites, and of the children of those who were sinners before you, who didn’t listen to the voice of you their God; because of this, these plagues cling to us.
003:005 Don’t remember the iniquities of our fathers, but remember your power and your name at this time.
003:006 For you are the Lord our God, and we will praise you, O Lord.
003:007 For this cause, you have put your fear in our hearts, to the intent that we should call upon your name. We will praise you in our captivity, for we have called to mind all the iniquity of our fathers who sinned before you.
003:008 Behold, we are yet this day in our captivity where you have scattered us, for a reproach and a curse, and to be subject to penalty according to all the iniquities of our fathers who departed from the Lord our God.
003:009 ¶ Hear, O Israel, the commandments of life! Give ear to understand wisdom!
003:010 How is it, O Israel, that you are in your enemies’ land, that you have become old in a strange country, that you are defiled with the dead,
003:011 that you are counted with those who are in Hades?
003:012 You have forsaken the fountain of wisdom.
003:013 If you had walked in the way of God, you would have dwelled in peace forever.
003:014 Learn where there is wisdom, where there is strength, and where there is understanding, that you may also know where there is length of days and life, where there is the light of the eyes and peace.
003:015 Who has found out her place? Who has come into her treasuries?
003:016 Where are the princes of the heathen, and those who ruled the beasts that are on the earth,
003:017 those who had their pastime with the fowls of the air, and those who hoarded up silver and gold, in which people trust, and of their getting there is no end?
003:018 For those who diligently sought silver, and were so anxious, and whose works are past finding out,
003:019 they have vanished and gone down to Hades, and others have come up in their place.
003:020 ¶ Younger men have seen the light and lived upon the earth, but they haven’t known the way of knowledge,
003:021 nor understood its paths. Their children haven’t embraced it. They are far off from their way.
003:022 It has not been heard of in Canaan, neither has it been seen in Teman.
003:023 The sons also of Agar who seek understanding, which are in the land, the merchants of Merran and Teman, and the authors of fables, and the searchers out of understanding—none of these have known the way of wisdom or remembered her paths.
003:024 ¶ O Israel, how great is the house of God! How large is the place of his possession!
003:025 It is great and has no end. It is high and unmeasurable.
003:026 Giants were born that were famous of old, great of stature, and expert in war.
003:027 God didn’t choose these, nor did he give the way of knowledge to them,
003:028 so they perished, because they had no wisdom. They perished through their own foolishness.
003:029 Who has gone up into heaven, taken her, and brought her down from the clouds?
003:030 Who has gone over the sea, found her, and will bring her for choice gold?
003:031 There is no one who knows her way, nor any who comprehend her path.
003:032 But he that knows all things knows her, he found her out with his understanding. He who prepared the earth for all time has filled it with four-footed beasts.
003:033 It is he who sends forth the light, and it goes. He called it, and it obeyed him with fear.
003:034 The stars shone in their watches, and were glad. When he called them, they said, “Here we are.” They shone with gladness to him who made them.
003:035 This is our God. No other can be compared to him.
003:036 He has found out all the way of knowledge, and has given it to Jacob his servant and to Israel who is loved by him.
003:037 Afterward she appeared upon earth, and lived with men.
004:001 ¶ This is the book of God’s commandments and the law that endures forever. All those who hold it fast will live, but those who leave it will die.
004:002 Turn, O Jacob, and take hold of it. Walk toward the shining of its light.
004:003 Don’t give your glory to another, nor the things that are to your advantage to a foreign nation.
004:004 O Israel, we are happy; for the things that are pleasing to God are made known to us.
004:005 ¶ Be of good cheer, my people, the memorial of Israel.
004:006 You were not sold to the nations for destruction, but because you moved God to wrath, you were delivered to your adversaries.
004:007 For you provoked him who made you by sacrificing to demons and not to God.
004:008 You forgot the everlasting God who brought you up. You also grieved Jerusalem, who nursed you.
004:009 For she saw the wrath that came upon you from God, and said, “Listen, you who dwell near Zion; for God has brought upon me great mourning.
004:010 For I have seen the captivity of my sons and daughters, which the Everlasting has brought upon them.
004:011 For with joy I nourished them, but sent them away with weeping and mourning.
004:012 Let no man rejoice over me, a widow and forsaken by many. For the sins of my children, I am left desolate, because they turned away from the law of God
004:013 and had no regard for his statutes. They didn’t walk in the ways of God’s commandments or tread in the paths of discipline in his righteousness.
004:014 Let those who dwell near Zion come and remember the captivity of my sons and daughters, which the Everlasting has brought upon them.
004:015 For he has brought a nation upon them from afar, a shameless nation with a strange language, who didn’t respect old men or pity children.
004:016 They have carried away the dear beloved sons of the widow, and left her who was alone desolate of her daughters.”
004:017 ¶ But I—how can I help you?
004:018 For he who brought these calamities upon you will deliver you from the hand of your enemies.
004:019 Go your way, O my children. Go your way, for I am left desolate.
004:020 I have put off the garment of peace, and put on the sackcloth of my petition. I will cry to the Everlasting as long as I live.
004:021 ¶ Take courage, my children. Cry to God, and he will deliver you from the power and hand of the enemies.
004:022 For I have trusted in the Everlasting, that he will save you; and joy has come to me from the Holy One, because of the mercy that will soon come to you from your Everlasting Savior.
004:023 For I sent you out with mourning and weeping, but God will give you to me again with joy and gladness forever.
004:024 For as now those who dwell near Zion have seen your captivity, so they will shortly see your salvation from our God which will come upon you with great glory and brightness of the Everlasting.
004:025 My children, suffer patiently the wrath that has come upon you from God, for your enemy has persecuted you; but shortly you will see his destruction and will tread upon their necks.
004:026 My delicate ones have traveled rough roads. They were taken away like a flock carried off by enemies.
004:027 ¶ Take courage, my children, and cry to God; for you will be remembered by him who has brought this upon you.
004:028 For as it was your decision to go astray from God, return and seek him ten times more.
004:029 For he who brought these calamities upon you will bring you everlasting joy again with your salvation.
004:030 Take courage, O Jerusalem, for he who called you by name will comfort you.
004:031 Miserable are those who afflicted you and rejoiced at your fall.
004:032 Miserable are the cities which your children served. Miserable is she who received your sons.
004:033 For as she rejoiced at your fall and was glad of your ruin, so she will be grieved at her own desolation.
004:034 And I will take away her pride in her great multitude and her boasting will be turned into mourning.
004:035 For fire will come upon her from the Everlasting for many days; and she will be inhabited by demons for a long time.
004:036 ¶ O Jerusalem, look around you toward the east, and behold the joy that comes to you from God.
004:037 Behold, your sons come, whom you sent away. They come gathered together from the east to the west at the word of the Holy One, rejoicing in the glory of God.
005:001 ¶ Take off the garment of your mourning and affliction, O Jerusalem, and put on forever the beauty of the glory from God.

006:001 [[The Letter of Jeremy (Jeremiah)]] ¶ A copy of a letter that Jeremy sent to those who were to be led captives into Babylon by the king of the Babylonians, to give them the message that God commanded him.
006:002 ¶ Because of the sins which you have committed before God, you will be led away captives to Babylon by Nabuchodonosor king of the Babylonians.
006:003 So when you come to Babylon, you will remain there many years, and for a long season, even for seven generations. After that, I will bring you out peacefully from there.
006:004 But now you will see in Babylon gods of silver, gold, and wood carried on shoulders, which cause the nations to fear.
006:005 Beware therefore that you in no way become like these foreigners. Don’t let fear take hold of you because of them when you see the multitude before them and behind them, worshiping them.
006:006 But say in your hearts, “O Lord, we must worship you.”
006:007 For my angel is with you, and I myself care for your souls.
006:008 For their tongue is polished by the workman, and they themselves are overlaid with gold and with silver; yet they are only fake, and can’t speak.
006:009 And taking gold, as if it were for a virgin who loves to be happy, they make crowns for the heads of their gods.
006:010 Sometimes also the priests take gold and silver from their gods, and spend it on themselves.
006:011 They will even give some of it to the common prostitutes. They dress them like men with garments, even the gods of silver, gods of gold, and gods of wood.
006:012 Yet these gods can’t save themselves from rust and moths, even though they are covered with purple garments.
006:013 They wipe their faces because of the dust of the temple, which is thick upon them.
006:014 And he who can’t put to death one who offends against him holds a sceptre, as though he were judge of a country.
006:015 He has also a dagger in his right hand, and an axe, but can’t deliver himself from war and robbers.
006:016 By this they are known not to be gods. Therefore don’t fear them.
006:017 For like a vessel that a man uses is worth nothing when it is broken, even so it is with their gods. When they are set up in the temples, their eyes are full of dust through the feet of those who come in.
006:018 As the courts are secured on every side upon him who offends the king, as being committed to suffer death, even so the priests secure their temples with doors, with locks, and bars, lest they be carried off by robbers.
006:019 They light candles for them, yes, more than for themselves, even though they can’t see one.
006:020 They are like one of the beams of the temple. Men say their hearts are eaten out when things creeping out of the earth devour both them and their clothing. They don’t feel it
006:021 when their faces are blackened through the smoke that comes out of the temple.
006:022 Bats, swallows, and birds land on their bodies and heads. So do the cats.
006:023 By this you may know that they are no gods. Therefore don’t fear them.
006:024 Notwithstanding the gold with which they are covered to make them beautiful, unless someone wipes off the tarnish, they won’t shine; for they didn’t even feel it when they were molten.
006:025 Things in which there is no breath are bought at any cost.
006:026 Having no feet, they are carried upon shoulders. By this, they declare to men that they are worth nothing.
006:027 Those who serve them are also ashamed, for if they fall to the ground at any time, they can’t rise up again by themselves. If they are bowed down, they can’t make themselves straight; but the offerings are set before them, as if they were dead men.
006:028 And the things that are sacrificed to them, their priests sell and spend. In like manner, their wives also lay up part of it in salt; but to the poor and to the impotent they give none of it.
006:029 The menstruous woman and the woman in childbed touch their sacrifices, knowing therefore by these things that they are no gods. Don’t fear them.
006:030 For how can they be called gods? Because women set food before the gods of silver, gold, and wood.
006:031 And in their temples the priests sit on seats, having their clothes torn and their heads and beards shaven, and nothing on their heads.
006:032 They roar and cry before their gods, as men do at the feast when one is dead.
006:033 The priests also take off garments from them and clothe their wives and children with them.
006:034 Whether it is evil or good what one does to them, they are not able to repay it. They can’t set up a king or put him down.
006:035 In like manner, they can neither give riches nor money. Though a man make a vow to them and doesn’t keep it, they will never exact it.
006:036 They can save no man from death. They can’t deliver the weak from the mighty.
006:037 They can’t restore a blind man to his sight, or deliver anyone who is in distress.
006:038 They can show no mercy to the widow, or do good to the fatherless.
006:039 They are like the stones that are cut out of the mountain, these gods of wood that are overlaid with gold and with silver. Those who minister to them will be confounded.
006:040 ¶ How could a man then think or say that they are gods, when even the Chaldeans themselves dishonor them?
006:041 If they shall see one mute who can’t speak, they bring him and ask him to call upon Bel, as though he were able to understand.
006:042 Yet they can’t perceive this themselves, and forsake them; for they have no understanding.
006:043 The women also with cords around them sit in the ways, burning bran for incense; but if any of them, drawn by someone who passes by, lies with him, she reproaches her fellow, that she was not thought as worthy as herself and her cord wasn’t broken.
006:044 Whatever is done among them is false. How could a man then think or say that they are gods?
006:045 They are fashioned by carpenters and goldsmiths. They can be nothing else than what the workmen make them to be.
006:046 And they themselves who fashioned them can never continue long. How then should the things that are fashioned by them?
006:047 For they have left lies and reproaches to those who come after.
006:048 For when there comes any war or plague upon them, the priests consult with themselves, where they may be hidden with them.
006:049 How then can’t men understand that they are no gods, which can’t save themselves from war or from plague?
006:050 For seeing they are only wood and overlaid with gold and silver, it will be known hereafter that they are false.
006:051 It will be manifest to all nations and kings that they are no gods, but the works of men’s hands, and that there is no work of God in them.
006:052 Who then may not know that they are not gods?
006:053 ¶ For they can’t set up a king in a land or give rain to men.
006:054 They can’t judge their own cause, or redress a wrong, being unable; for they are like crows between heaven and earth.
006:055 For even when fire falls upon the house of gods of wood overlaid with gold or with silver, their priests will flee away, and escape, but they themselves will be burned apart like beams.
006:056 Moreover they can’t withstand any king or enemies. How could a man then admit or think that they are gods?
006:057 Those gods of wood overlaid with silver or with gold aren’t able to escape from thieves or robbers.
006:058 The gold, silver, and garments with which they are clothed—those who are strong will take from them, and go away with them. They won’t be able to help themselves.
006:059 Therefore it is better to be a king who shows his manhood, or else a vessel in a house profitable for whatever the owner needs, than such false gods—or even a door in a house, to keep the things safe that are in it, than such false gods; or better to be a pillar of wood in a palace than such false gods.
006:060 ¶ For sun, moon, and stars, being bright and sent to do their jobs, are obedient.
006:061 Likewise also the lightning when it flashes is beautiful to see. In the same way, the wind also blows in every country.
006:062 And when God commands the clouds to go over the whole world, they do as they are told.
006:063 And the fire sent from above to consume mountains and woods does as it is commanded; but these are to be compared to them neither in show nor power.
006:064 Therefore a man shouldn’t think or say that they are gods, seeing they aren’t able to judge causes or to do good to men.
006:065 Knowing therefore that they are no gods, don’t fear them.
006:066 For they can neither curse nor bless kings.
006:067 They can’t show signs in the heavens among the nations, or shine as the sun, or give light as the moon.
006:068 The beasts are better than they, for they can find shelter and help themselves.
006:069 In no way then is it manifest to us that they are gods. Therefore don’t fear them.
006:070 For as a scarecrow in a garden of cucumbers that keeps nothing, so are their gods of wood overlaid with gold and silver.
006:071 Likewise also their gods of wood overlaid with gold and with silver are like a white thorn in an orchard that every bird sits upon. They are also like a dead body that is thrown out into the dark.
006:072 You will know them to be no gods by the bright purple that rots upon them. They themselves will be consumed afterwards, and will be a reproach in the country.
006:073 Better therefore is the just man who has no idols; for he will be far from reproach.
