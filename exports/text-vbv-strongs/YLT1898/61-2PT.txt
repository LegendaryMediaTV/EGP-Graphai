The first who expressly mentions it, (which he does as S.S.), is Origen (A.D. 182–254), while he states that some doubted its canonicity. Clement of Rome (A.D. 108), Hermas (A.D. 108), Justin-Martyr (A.D. 140), Athenagorus (A.D. 178), Irenaeus (A.D. 140–200), &c. appear to have several allusions to certain phrases that appear in it. Exhortations, ch. ii. False Teachers, ch. iii. Scoffers.} °{Chapter I. may be divided into five parts; v. 1, 2 address and salutation; v. 3, 4 divine gifts and promises; v. 5–11 encouragement to abound in graces; v. 12–15 reasons for reminding them; v. 16–21 of the truthfulness of the apostolic and scripture testimony to Christ.} Simeon Peter°{i.e. a “rock,” a firm, unbending man.}, a servant°{Lit., bondsman} and an apostle°{Lit., one sent forth} of Jesus Christ°{i.e. the “anointed saviour.”}, to those who did obtain a like precious faith with us in the righteousness of our God and Saviour Jesus Christ:
001:002 Grace°{Lit., what causes leaping for joy} to you, and peace be multiplied in the acknowledgement of God and of Jesus our Lord!
001:003 As all things to us His divine power (the things pertaining unto life and piety) hath given, through the acknowledgement of him who did call us through glory and worthiness°{Or, pleasingness},
001:004 through which to us the most great and precious promises have been given, that through these ye may become partakers of a divine nature, having escaped from the corruption in the world in desires.
001:005 And this same also—all diligence having brought in besides, superadd in your faith the worthiness, and in the worthiness the knowledge,
001:006 and in the knowledge the temperance°{Lit., inward-strength}, and in the temperance the endurance°{Lit., remaining under}, and in the endurance the piety,
001:007 and in the piety the brotherly kindness, and in the brotherly kindness the love;
001:008 for these things being to you and abounding, do make you neither inert nor unfruitful in regard to the acknowledging of our Lord Jesus Christ,
001:009 for he with whom these things are not present is blind, dim-sighted°{Lit., shutting the eyes}, having become forgetful of°{Lit., accepted a hiding of} the cleansing of his old sins;
001:010 wherefore, the rather, brethren, be diligent to make stedfast your calling and choice°{Or, election}, for these things doing, ye may never stumble,
001:011 for so, richly shall be superadded to you the entrance into the age-during reign of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.
001:012 ¶ Wherefore, I will not be careless always to remind you concerning these things, though, having known them, and having been established in the present truth,
001:013 and I think right, so long as I am in this tabernacle, to stir you up in reminding you,
001:014 having known that soon is the laying aside of my tabernacle, even as also our Lord Jesus Christ did shew to me,
001:015 and I will be diligent that also at every time ye have, after my outgoing°{Gr. exodus}, power to make to yourselves the remembrance of these things.
001:016 For, skilfully devised fables not having followed out, we did make known to you the power and presence of our Lord Jesus Christ, but eye-witnesses having become of his majesty—
001:017 for having received from God the Father honour and glory, such a voice being borne to him by the excellent glory: “This is My Son—the beloved, in whom I was well pleased;”
001:018 and this voice we—we did hear, out of heaven borne, being with him in the holy mount.
001:019 And we have more firm the prophetic word, to which we do well giving heed, as to a lamp shining in a dark°{Or, squalid} place, till day may dawn, and a morning star may arise—in your hearts;
001:020 this first knowing, that no prophecy of the Writing doth come of private exposition°{Or, one’s (i.e. a prophet’s) own solution (or “loosing out” of probable events)},
001:021 for not by will of man did ever prophecy come, but by the Holy Spirit borne on holy men of God spake.
002:001 °{Chapter II. may be divided into four parts; v. 1–3 prediction of false teachers; v. 4–8 God’s conduct towards sinful messengers, the old world, the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah; v. 9–16 and those following Balaam; v. 17–22 their awful corruption and depravity.} [[False Teachers]] ¶ And there did come also false prophets among the people, as also among you there shall be false teachers, who shall bring in besides destructive sects, and the Master°{Lit., Despot} who bought them denying, bringing to themselves quick destruction,
002:002 and many shall follow out their destructive ways, because of whom the way of the truth shall be evil spoken of,
002:003 and in covetousness, with moulded words, of you they shall make merchandise, whose judgment of old is not idle, and their destruction°{Lit., loosing away} doth not slumber.
002:004 For if God messengers who sinned°{Lit., “missed” the mark} did not spare, but with chains of thick gloom, having cast them down to Tartarus, did deliver them to judgment, having been reserved,
002:005 and the old world did not spare, but the eighth person, Noah, of righteousness a preacher°{Or, herald}, did keep, a flood on the world of the impious having brought,
002:006 and the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah having turned to ashes, with an overthrow did condemn, an example to those about to be impious having set them;
002:007 and righteous Lot, worn down by the conduct in lasciviousness of the impious°{Lit., the unsettled}, He did rescue,
002:008 for in seeing and hearing, the righteous man, dwelling among them, day by day the righteous soul with unlawful works was harassing°{Or, oppressing, violating, forcing}.
002:009 The Lord hath known to rescue pious ones out of temptation, and unrighteous ones to a day of judgment, being punished, to keep,
002:010 and chiefly those going behind the flesh in desire of uncleanness, and lordship despising; presumptuous, self-complacent, dignities they are not afraid to speak evil of,
002:011 whereas messengers, in strength and power being greater, do not bear against them before the Lord an evil speaking judgment;
002:012 ¶ and these, as irrational natural beasts, made to be caught and destroyed—in what things they are ignorant of, speaking evil—in their destruction shall be destroyed,
002:013 about to receive a reward of unrighteousness, pleasures counting the luxury in the day, spots and blemishes, luxuriating in their deceits, feasting with you,
002:014 having eyes full of adultery, and unable to cease from sin, enticing unstable souls, having an heart exercised°{Lit., made naked} in covetousnesses, children of a curse,
002:015 having forsaken a right way, they did go astray, having followed in the way of Balaam the son of Bosor, who a reward of unrighteousness did love,
002:016 and had a rebuke of his own iniquity°{Or, going aside from law}—a dumb ass°{Lit., one under yoke}, in man’s voice having spoken, did forbid the madness of the prophet.
002:017 These are wells without water, and clouds by a tempest driven, to whom the thick gloom of the darkness to the age hath been kept;
002:018 for overswellings of vanity speaking, they do entice in desires of the flesh—lasciviousnesses, those who had truly°{Or, scarcely} escaped from those conducting themselves in error,
002:019 liberty to them promising, themselves being servants of the corruption, for by whom any one hath been overcome, to this one also he hath been brought to servitude,
002:020 for, if having escaped from the pollutions of the world, in the acknowledging of the Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, and by these again being entangled, they have been overcome, become to them hath the last things worse than the first,
002:021 for it were better to them not to have acknowledged the way of the righteousness, than having acknowledged it, to turn back from the holy command delivered to them,
002:022 and happened to them hath that of the true similitude; “A dog did turn back upon his own vomit,” and, “A sow having bathed herself—to rolling in mire°{Or, “food.”}.”
003:001 °{Chapter III. may be divided into five parts; v. 1, 2 exhortation to be mindful of former sayings; v. 3–7 prediction of coming scoffers and their folly; v. 8–10 certainty of the coming of the day of the Lord; v. 11–16 and what we ought to be and to do; v. 17, 18 final warning, exhortation and doxology.} ¶ This, now, beloved, a second letter to you I write, in both which I stir up your pure mind in reminding you,
003:002 to be mindful of the sayings said before by the holy prophets, and of the command of us the apostles of the Lord and Saviour,
003:003 this first knowing, that there shall come in the latter end of the days°{of the Jewish dispensation, as always,} scoffers°{Lit., those acting inwardly as boys}, according to their own desires going on,
003:004 and saying, “Where is the promise of his presence? for since the fathers did fall asleep, all things so remain from the beginning of the creation;”
003:005 for this is unobserved by them willingly, that the heavens were of old, and the earth out of water and through water standing together by the word of God,
003:006 through which the then world°{Lit., “order” of things}, by water having been deluged, was destroyed;
003:007 and the present heavens and the earth, by the same word are treasured°{Lit., set for to-morrow}, for fire being kept to a day of judgment and destruction of the impious men.
003:008 ¶ And this one thing let not be unobserved by you, beloved, that one day with the Lord is as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day;
003:009 the Lord is not slow°{as being under a “burden.”} in regard to the promise, as certain count slowness, but is longsuffering to us, not counselling any to be lost but all to pass on to reformation,
003:010 and it will come—the day of the Lord—as a thief in the night, in which the heavens with a rushing noise will pass away, and the elements°{Lit., steppings (i.e. first principles)} with burning heat be dissolved, and earth and the works in it shall be burnt up.
003:011 All these, then, being dissolved, what kind of persons doth it behove you to be in holy behaviours and pious acts?
003:012 waiting for and hasting to the presence of the day of God, by which the heavens, being on fire, shall be dissolved, and the elements with burning heat shall melt;
003:013 and for new heavens and a new earth according to His promise we do wait, in which righteousness doth dwell;
003:014 ¶ wherefore, beloved, these things waiting for, be diligent, spotless and unblameable, by Him to be found in peace,
003:015 and the long-suffering of our Lord count ye salvation°{i.e. a time of it.}, according as also our beloved brother Paul—according to the wisdom given to him—did write to you,
003:016 as also in all the epistles, speaking in them concerning these things, among which things are certain hard to be understood, which the untaught and unstable do wrest, as also the other Writings, unto their own destruction.
003:017 ¶ Ye, then, beloved, knowing before, take heed, lest, together with the error of the impious being led away, ye may fall from your own stedfastness,
003:018 and increase ye in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ; to him is the glory both now, and to the day of the age! Amen.
