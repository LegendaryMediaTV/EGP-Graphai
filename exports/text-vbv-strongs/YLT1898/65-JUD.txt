001:001 ¶ °{JUDE—or rather JUDAS—the writer of this Letter was probably that one of the twelve apostles referred to in John 14. 22, and elsewhere called Lebbeus and Thaddeus (lit. one with a heart, i.e. a bold man). His brother James, to whom he refers in the opening of his Letter, was doubtless the one who spoke at the Jerusalem Council. This Letter is quoted or referred to by Clement of Alexandria (A.D. 194), Tertullian (A.D. 200), Origen (A.D. 230), Eusebius (A.D. 320), Jerome (A.D. 392), Ephraim Syrus (A.D. 370). It was evidently written a little before the destruction of Jerusalem.␤This Letter may be divided into eight parts; v. 1, 2 address and salutation; v. 3–7 exhortation to contend for the faith, against certain impious men, from God’s past dealings; v. 8–11 their presumption and carnality; v. 12, 13 their dangerous character and end; v. 14, 15 like those to whom Enoch prophesied; v. 16–19 and they themselves spoken of beforehand by the apostles; v. 20–23 call to build themselves up, to wait for the kindness of Christ, and to help others; v. 24, 25 ascription of praise to God.} Judas, of Jesus Christ°{an “anointed saviour.”} a servant°{Lit., a bondsman}, and brother°{Lit., one from the same womb.} of James°{Gr. Jacobus, same as Heb. Jacob, “one who takes by the heel,” i.e. supplants; he is called the Less or Little (Mark 15. 40; Luke 6. 16; Jas. 1. 1), probably because of his stature.}, to those sanctified°{Textual note: set-apart (lit. “not of the earth,” but many MSS. read, “beloved,”)} in God the Father, and in Jesus Christ kept—called°{same as Heb. Judah, “praise, confession.”},
001:002 kindness to you, and peace°{Lit., what brings into unity.}, and love, be multiplied!
001:003 ¶ Beloved, all diligence using to write to you concerning the common salvation, I had necessity to write to you, exhorting to agonize°{Lit., agonize-openly} for the faith once delivered to the saints°{those “out of the earth.”},
001:004 for there did come in unobserved°{Lit., along-side} certain men, long ago having been written°{Lit., publicly described} beforehand to this judgment, impious, the grace of our God perverting to lasciviousness, and our only Master°{the only despot,}, God, and Lord—Jesus Christ—denying,
001:005 ¶ and to remind°{Lit., remind you somewhat or a little} you I intend, you knowing once this, that the Lord, a people out of the land of Egypt having saved, again those who did not believe did destroy°{Lit., loosed away};
001:006 messengers also, those who did not keep their own principality, but did leave their proper dwelling, to a judgment of a great day, in bonds everlasting, under darkness He hath kept,
001:007 as Sodom and Gomorrah, and the cities around them, in like manner to these, having given themselves to whoredom, and gone after other flesh, have been set before—an example, of fire age-during, justice suffering.
001:008 ¶ In like manner, nevertheless, those dreaming°{Lit., putting themselves in a sleep} also the flesh indeed do defile, and lordship they put away, and dignities°{Lit., glories} they speak evil of,
001:009 yet Michael°{who is like God?}, the chief messenger, when, with the devil contending, he was disputing about the body of Moses°{the Jewish church, comp. the “body of Christ,”}, did not dare to bring up an evil-speaking judgment, but said, “The Lord rebuke°{Lit., put a weight upon thee!} thee!”
001:010 and these, as many things indeed as they have not known, they speak evil of; and as many things as naturally (as the irrational beasts) they understand, in these they are corrupted;
001:011 woe to them! because in the way of Cain they did go on, and to the deceit of Balaam for reward they did rush, and in the gainsaying of Korah they did perish°{Lit., loosed themselves away}.
001:012 These are in your love-feasts craggy rocks; feasting together with you, without fear shepherding themselves; clouds without water, by winds carried about; trees autumnal, without fruit, twice dead, rooted up;
001:013 wild°{Lit., field} waves of a sea, foaming out their own shames; stars going astray, to whom the gloom of the darkness to the age hath been kept.
001:014 ¶ And prophesy also to these did the seventh from Adam—Enoch—saying, “Lo, the Lord did come in His saintly myriads,
001:015 to do judgment against all, and to convict all their impious ones, concerning all their works of impiety that they did impiously, and concerning all the stiff°{Lit., dry things} things that speak against Him did impious sinners.”
001:016 ¶ These are murmurers, repiners; according to their desires walking, and their mouth doth speak great swellings, giving admiration to persons for the sake of profit;
001:017 ¶ and ye, beloved, remember ye the sayings spoken before by°{Lit., under} the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ:
001:018 that they said to you, that in the last time°{of the Jewish dispensation} there shall be scoffers°{those acting inwardly as boys}, after their own desires of impieties going on,
001:019 these are those setting themselves apart, natural men, the Spirit not having.
001:020 And ye, beloved, on your most holy faith building yourselves up, in the Holy Spirit praying°{Lit., pouring yourselves forth well},
001:021 yourselves in the love of God keep ye, waiting for the kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ—to life age-during;
001:022 and to some be kind, judging thoroughly,
001:023 and some in fear save°{trying to save} ye, out of the fire snatching, hating even the coat from the flesh spotted.
001:024 ¶ And to Him who is able to guard you not stumbling, and to set you in the presence of His glory unblemished, in gladness°{Lit., much leaping.},
001:025 to the only wise God our Saviour, is glory and greatness, power°{See 1 Timothy 1:17} and authority, both now and to all the ages! Amen.
