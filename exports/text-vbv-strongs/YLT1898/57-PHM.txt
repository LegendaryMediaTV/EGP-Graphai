001:001 ¶ °{PHILEMON lived at Colosse, and was a rich convert and fellow-labourer of Paul, who, when a prisoner in Rome, A.D. 61–2, wrote him this letter to induce him to receive back a run-away bondsman—Onesimus—whom Paul had met with and converted to the gospel. This letter was quoted or referred to by Tertullian (A.D. 192), Caius (A.D. 212), Origen (A.D. 230), Jerome (A.D. 392), Eusebius (A.D. 320), and perhaps Ignatius (A.D. 107).␤THIS LETTER may be divided into six parts; v. 1–3 address and salutation; v. 4–7 thanksgiving for Philemon’s state; v. 8–16 entreaty for Onesimus; v. 17–19 personal reasons for this; v. 20–22 expression of confidence and a request; v. 23–25 closing salutations.} Paul, a prisoner°{Lit., bound one.} of Christ Jesus°{the “anointed saviour.”}, and Timotheus the brother°{“the brother,” in the faith.}, to Philemon°{“loving, befriending.”} our beloved and fellow-worker°{“joint worker,” in building up the Colossians.},
001:002 and Apphia°{…Apphia (a female), and Archippus (i.e. chief-horseman, Col. 4. 17), …church (or assembly) at thy house; see Romans 16:15; 1 Corinthians 16:11} the beloved, and Archippus our fellow-soldier, and the assembly in thy house:
001:003 Grace°{Lit., what causes leaping for joy.} to you, and peace°{Lit., what brings into unity.}, from God our Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ°{Or, and (Father of the) Lord Jesus Christ.}!
001:004 ¶ I give thanks°{Lit., leap much for joy} to my God, always making mention of thee in my prayers°{Lit., pouring-forth},
001:005 hearing of thy love and faith that thou hast unto the Lord Jesus and toward all the saints°{Lit., “not of the earth.”},
001:006 that the fellowship of thy faith may become working in the full knowledge of every good thing that is in you toward Christ Jesus;
001:007 for we have much joy and comfort°{Lit., …exhortation…} in thy love, because the bowels of the saints have been refreshed through thee, brother.
001:008 ¶ Wherefore, having in Christ much boldness°{Lit., …full-speech to arrange for thee what comes up.} to command thee that which is fit—
001:009 because of the love I rather entreat°{Lit., …call upon (thee).}, being such an one as Paul the aged, and now also a prisoner of Jesus Christ;
001:010 I entreat thee concerning my child—whom I did beget in my bonds—Onesimus°{profitable.},
001:011 who once was to thee unprofitable, and now is profitable to me and to thee,
001:012 whom I did send again, and thou him (that is, my own bowels) receive,
001:013 whom I did wish to retain to myself, that in thy behalf he might minister°{Lit., …he might be a deacon to me.} to me in the bonds of the good news,
001:014 and apart from thy mind°{Lit., apart from thy knowledge} I willed to do nothing, that as of necessity thy good deed may not be, but of willingness,
001:015 for perhaps because of this he did depart°{Lit., was separated} for an hour, that age-duringly thou mayest have him°{Lit., …have him entirely; see Exodus 21:6},
001:016 no more as a servant°{Lit., no more as a bondsman.}, but above a servant—a brother beloved, especially to me, and how much more to thee, both in the flesh and in the Lord!
001:017 If, then, with me thou hast fellowship, receive him as me,
001:018 and if he did hurt°{Lit., …used thee unjustly…} to thee, or doth owe anything, this to me be reckoning;
001:019 I, Paul did write with my hand, I—I will repay; that I may not say that also thyself, besides, to me thou dost owe.
001:020 Yes, brother, may I have profit of thee in the Lord; refresh my bowels in the Lord;
001:021 ¶ having been confident in thy obedience°{Lit., …submissive hearkening…} I did write to thee, having known that also above what I may say thou wilt do;
001:022 and at the same time also prepare for me a lodging, for I hope that through your prayers I shall be granted to you.
001:023 ¶ Salute°{Lit., draw thee near} thee doth Epaphras°{Epaphroditus, upon Venus, i.q. lovely.}, (my fellow-captive in Christ Jesus,)
001:024 Markus°{“far, distant.”}, Aristarchus°{“best of chiefs.”}, Demas°{a “plebeian,” of the “people.”}, Lukas°{“shining.”}, my fellow-workmen!
001:025 ¶ The grace°{Lit., what causes leaping for joy.} of our Lord Jesus Christ is with your spirit! Amen.
