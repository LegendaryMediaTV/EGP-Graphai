001:001 ¶ °{THIS LETTER is quoted by the same ancient fathers as quote the preceding one, and rests on the same kind of evidence. This Gaius (or Caius) is supposed by some to be the same as Gaius of Macedonia (Acts 19. 29), by others as Caius of Corinth (1 Cor. 1. 14), by others as Gaius of Derbe (Acts 20. 4), and by others as Gaius of Rome (Rom. 16. 23), but nothing is certain on this point. He appears to have been a man of substance and influence.␤This Letter may be divided into five parts; v. 1–4 address and salutation; v. 5–8 approval and encouragement; v. 9, 10 a complaint and promise; v. 11, 12 a warning and testimony; v. 13–15 a hope and salutation.}The Elder°{“the presbyter;” a title of age, or of office; see 1 Peter 5:1, 1 Peter 5:5} to Gaius°{Lat. Caius.} the beloved, whom I love in truth!
001:002 ¶ beloved, concerning all things I desire thee to prosper,°{Lit., I pour myself forth well for thee to have a good journey} and to be in health, even as thy soul doth prosper,
001:003 for I rejoiced°{Lit., was caused to leap much with joy} exceedingly, brethren coming and testifying of the truth in thee, even as thou in truth dost walk;
001:004 greater than these things I have no joy,°{Lit., cause of leaping for joy} that I may hear of my children in truth walking.
001:005 ¶ Beloved, faithfully°{Or, stedfastly} dost thou do whatever thou mayest work to the brethren and to the strangers,
001:006 who did testify of thy love before an assembly, whom thou wilt do well, having sent forward worthily of God,
001:007 because for His name they went forth, nothing receiving from the nations;
001:008 we, then, ought to receive such, that fellow-workers we may become to the truth.
001:009 ¶ I did write to the assembly, but he who is loving the first place among them—Diotrephes°{“nourished by Jove.”}—doth not receive us;
001:010 because of this, if I may come, I will cause him to remember his works that he doth, with evil words prating°{Lit., flooding us with evil words} against us; and not content with these, neither doth he himself receive the brethren, and those intending he doth forbid, and out of the assembly he doth cast.
001:011 ¶ Beloved, be not thou following°{Lit., imitating} that which is evil, but that which is good; he who is doing good, of God he is, and he who is doing evil hath not seen God;
001:012 ¶ to Demetrius°{“belonging to mother earth.”} testimony hath been given by all, and by the truth itself, and we also—we do testify, and ye have known that our testimony is true.
001:013 ¶ Many things I had to write, but I do not wish through ink and pen to write to thee,
001:014 and I hope straightway to see thee, and mouth to mouth we shall speak. Peace to thee! salute thee do the friends; be saluting°{Lit., embracing} the friends by name.
