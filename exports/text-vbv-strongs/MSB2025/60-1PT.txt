001:001 [[A Greeting from Peter°{2 Peter 1:1–2}]] ¶ Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, ¶ To the elect who are exiles of the Dispersion throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia, chosen°{Literally To the elect sojourners of the Diaspora of Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia. 001:003 [[A Living Hope]] ¶ Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! By His great mercy He has given us new birth°{Or has caused us to be born again or has begotten us again} into a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,
001:004 and into an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, reserved in heaven for you,
001:005 who through faith are shielded by God’s power for the salvation that is ready to be revealed in the last time.
001:006 ¶ In this you greatly rejoice, though now for a little while you may have had to suffer grief in various trials
001:007 so that the proven character of your faith—more precious than gold, which perishes even though refined by fire—may result in praise, honor, and glory°{CT and F35 praise, glory, and honor} at the revelation of Jesus Christ.
001:008 ¶ Though you have not known°{CT and TR have not seen} Him, you love Him; and though you do not see Him now, you believe in Him and rejoice with an inexpressible and glorious joy,
001:009 now that you are receiving the goal of your faith, the salvation of your souls.
001:010 ¶ Concerning this salvation, the prophets who foretold the grace to come to you searched and investigated carefully,
001:011 trying to determine the time and setting to which the Spirit of Christ in them was pointing when He predicted the sufferings of Christ and the glories to follow.
001:012 ¶ It was revealed to them that they were not serving themselves, but you,°{TR us} when they foretold the things now announced by those who preached the gospel to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven. Even angels long to look into these things.
001:013 [[A Call to Holiness°{Hebrews 12:14–17}]] ¶ Therefore prepare your minds for action.°{Literally Wherefore gird up the loins of your mind} Be sober-minded. Set your hope fully on the grace to be given you at the revelation of Jesus Christ.
001:014 As obedient children, do not conform to the passions of your former ignorance.
001:015 But just as He who called you is holy, so be holy in all you do,

001:017 ¶ Since you call on a Father who judges each one’s work impartially, conduct yourselves in reverent fear during your stay as foreigners.
001:018 For you know that it was not with perishable things such as silver or gold that you were redeemed from the empty way of life you inherited from your forefathers,
001:019 but with the precious blood of Christ, a lamb without blemish or spot.
001:020 He was known before the foundation of the world, but was revealed in the last times for your sake.
001:021 ¶ Through Him you believe in God, who raised Him from the dead and glorified Him; and so your faith and hope are in God.
001:022 [[The Enduring Word°{Isaiah 40:6–8}]] ¶ Since you have purified your souls by obedience to the truth through the Spirit°{CT does not include through the Spirit.} so that you have a genuine love for your brothers, love one another deeply, from a pure heart.°{SBL, NE, and WH from the heart}
