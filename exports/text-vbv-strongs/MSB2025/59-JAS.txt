001:001 [[A Greeting from James°{Jude 1:1–2}]] ¶ James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, ¶ To the twelve tribes of the Dispersion:°{Literally To the twelve tribes in the Diaspora. Originally referring to the Jewish people living outside the land of Israel, the Diaspora is applied here to the Jewish believers scattered abroad.} ¶ Greetings.
001:002 [[Rejoicing in Trials°{Philippians 1:12–20}]] ¶ Consider it pure joy, my brothers, when you encounter trials of many kinds,
001:003 because you know that the testing of your faith develops perseverance.
001:004 Allow perseverance to finish its work, so that you may be mature and complete, not lacking anything.
001:005 ¶ Now if any of you lacks wisdom, he should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to him.
001:006 But he must ask in faith, without doubting, because he who doubts is like a wave of the sea, blown and tossed by the wind.
001:007 That man should not expect to receive anything from the Lord.
001:008 He is a double-minded man, unstable in all his ways.
001:009 ¶ The brother in humble circumstances should exult in his high position.
001:010 But the one who is rich should exult in his low position, because he will pass away like a flower of the field.
001:011 For the sun rises with scorching heat and withers the plant; its flower falls and its beauty is lost. So too, the rich man will fade away in the midst of his pursuits.
001:012 ¶ Blessed is the man who perseveres under trial, because when he has stood the test, he will receive the crown of life that the Lord°{CT He} has promised to those who love Him.
001:013 [[Good and Perfect Gifts]] ¶ When tempted, no one should say, “God is tempting me.” For God cannot be tempted by evil, nor does He tempt anyone.
001:014 But each one is tempted when by his own evil desires he is lured away and enticed.
001:015 Then after desire has conceived, it gives birth to sin; and sin, when it is full-grown, gives birth to death.
001:016 ¶ Do not be deceived, my beloved brothers.
001:017 Every good and perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of the heavenly lights, with whom there is no change or shifting shadow.°{Or no change or shadow of turning.}
001:018 He chose to give us birth through the word of truth, that we would be a kind of firstfruits of His creation.°{Or of His creatures.}
001:019 [[Hearing and Doing]] ¶ So then, my beloved brothers, everyone°{CT My beloved brothers, understand this: Everyone} should be quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to anger,
001:020 for man’s anger does not bring about the righteousness that God desires.
001:021 Therefore, get rid of all moral filth and every expression of evil, and humbly accept the word planted in you, which can save your souls.
001:022 ¶ Be doers of the word, and not hearers only. Otherwise, you are deceiving yourselves.
001:023 For anyone who hears the word°{F35 law} but does not carry it out is like a man who looks at his face in a mirror,
001:024 and after observing himself goes away and immediately forgets what he looks like.
001:025 But the one who looks intently into the perfect law of freedom, and continues to do so—not being a forgetful hearer, but an effective doer—he will be blessed in what he does.
001:026 ¶ If anyone among you°{CT does not include among you.} considers himself religious and yet does not bridle his tongue, he deceives his heart and his religion is worthless.
001:027 Pure and undefiled religion before our God and Father is this: to care for orphans and widows in their distress, and to keep oneself from being polluted by the world.
002:001 [[A Warning against Favoritism]] ¶ My brothers, as you hold out your faith in our glorious Lord Jesus Christ, do not show favoritism.
002:002 ¶ Suppose a man comes into your meeting°{Greek your synagogue} wearing a gold ring and fine clothes, and a poor man in shabby clothes also comes in.
002:003 If you lavish attention on the man in fine clothes and say, “Here is a seat of honor,” but say to the poor man, “You must stand” or “Sit here under my footstool,”°{CT “Sit at my feet”}
002:004 have you not discriminated among yourselves and become judges with evil thoughts?
002:005 ¶ Listen, my beloved brothers: Has not God chosen the poor of this world to be rich in faith and to inherit the kingdom He promised those who love Him?
002:006 But you have dishonored the poor. Is it not the rich who oppress you and drag you into court?
002:007 Are they not the ones who blaspheme the noble name by which you have been called?°{Or the noble name invoked upon you or the noble name of Him to whom you belong}
002:008 ¶ If you really fulfill the royal law stated in Scripture, “Love your neighbor as yourself,”°{Leviticus 19:18} you are doing well.
002:009 But if you show favoritism, you sin and are convicted by the law as transgressors.
002:010 ¶ Whoever keeps the whole law but stumbles at just one point is guilty of breaking all of it.
002:011 For He who said, “Do not commit adultery,”°{Exodus 20:14; Deuteronomy 5:18} also said, “Do not murder.”°{Exodus 20:13; Deuteronomy 5:17} If you do not commit adultery, but do commit murder, you have become a lawbreaker.
002:012 ¶ Speak and act as those who are going to be judged by the law that gives freedom.
002:013 For judgment without mercy will be shown to anyone who has not been merciful. Mercy triumphs over judgment.
002:014 [[Faith and Works°{Galatians 3:1–9}]] ¶ What good is it, my brothers, if someone claims to have faith, but has no deeds? Can such faith save him?
002:015 Suppose a brother or sister is without clothes and daily food.
002:016 If one of you tells him, “Go in peace; stay warm and well fed,” but does not provide for his physical needs, what good is that?
002:017 So too, faith by itself, if it does not result in action,°{Literally if it does not have works} is dead.
002:018 ¶ But someone will say, “You have faith and I have deeds.” Show me your faith apart from your deeds,°{Literally Show me your faith from your deeds; CT Show me your faith without deeds; TR Show me your faith without your deeds} and I will show you my faith by my deeds.
002:019 You believe that God is one.°{Or that there is one God} Good for you! Even the demons believe that—and shudder.
002:020 ¶ O foolish man, do you want evidence that faith without deeds is dead?°{CT is worthless}
002:021 Was not our father Abraham justified by what he did when he offered his son Isaac on the altar?
002:022 You see that his faith was working with his actions, and his faith was perfected by what he did.
002:023 And the Scripture was fulfilled that says, “Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness,”°{Genesis 15:6} and he was called a friend of God.°{See Isaiah 41:8.}
002:024 As you can see, then,°{CT does not include then.} a man is justified by his deeds and not by faith alone.
002:025 ¶ In the same way, was not even Rahab the prostitute justified by her actions when she welcomed the spies°{Literally messengers} and sent them off on another route?
002:026 For as°{CT As} the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without deeds is dead.
003:001 [[Taming the Tongue°{Psalm 64:1–10}]] ¶ Not many of you should become teachers, my brothers, because you know that we who teach will be judged more strictly.
003:002 We all stumble in many ways. If anyone is never at fault in what he says, he is a perfect man, able to control his whole body.
003:003 ¶ Indeed, when°{CT When or If} we put bits into the mouths of horses to make them obey us, we can guide the whole animal.
003:004 Consider ships as well. Although they are so large and are driven by strong winds, they are steered by a very small rudder wherever the pilot is inclined.
003:005 ¶ In the same way, the tongue is a small part of the body, but it boasts of great things. Consider how small a spark sets a great forest ablaze.
003:006 The tongue also is a fire, a world of wickedness among the parts of the body. It pollutes the whole person, sets the course of his life on fire, and is itself set on fire by hell.°{Greek Gehenna}
003:007 ¶ All kinds of animals, birds, reptiles, and creatures of the sea are being tamed and have been tamed by man,
003:008 but no man can tame the tongue. It is an unruly evil,°{CT a restless evil} full of deadly poison.
003:009 ¶ With the tongue we bless our God°{CT our Lord} and Father, and with it we curse men, who have been made in God’s likeness.
003:010 Out of the same mouth come blessing and cursing. My brothers, this should not be!
003:011 Can both fresh water and salt water°{Literally Can both fresh and bitter} flow from the same spring?
003:012 My brothers, can a fig tree grow olives, or a grapevine bear figs? In the same way, no spring can produce both salt water and fresh water.°{CT Neither can a salt spring produce fresh water}
003:013 [[The Wisdom from Above]] ¶ Who is wise and understanding among you? Let him show it by his good conduct, by deeds done in the humility that comes from wisdom.
003:014 But if you harbor bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not boast in it or deny the truth.
003:015 Such wisdom does not come from above, but is earthly, unspiritual, demonic.
003:016 For where jealousy and selfish ambition exist, there will be disorder and every evil practice.
003:017 ¶ But the wisdom from above is first of all pure, then peace-loving, gentle, accommodating, full of mercy and good fruit, impartial, and sincere.
003:018 Peacemakers who sow in peace reap the fruit of righteousness.°{Literally But the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace by those making peace.}
004:001 [[A Warning against Pride]] ¶ What causes conflicts and quarrels among you? Don’t they come from the passions at war within you?°{Literally passions warring among your members?}
004:002 You crave what you do not have; you kill and covet, but are unable to obtain it. You quarrel and fight. You do not have, because you do not ask.
004:003 And when you do ask, you do not receive, because you ask with wrong motives, that you may squander it on your pleasures.
004:004 ¶ You adulterers and adulteresses!°{See Hosea 3:1; CT You adulteresses!} Do you not know that friendship with the world is hostility toward God? Therefore, whoever chooses to be a friend of the world renders himself°{Or is appointed} an enemy of God.
004:005 Or do you think the Scripture says without reason that the Spirit°{Or the spirit} who dwells°{CT He caused to dwell} in us yearns with envy?
004:006 But He gives us more grace. This is why it says: ¶ “God opposes the proud,␤but gives grace to the humble.”°{Proverbs 3:34 (see also LXX)}
004:007 [[Drawing Near to God]] ¶ Submit yourselves, then, to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.
004:008 Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double-minded.
004:009 Grieve, mourn, and weep. Turn your laughter to mourning, and your joy to gloom.
004:010 Humble yourselves before the Lord, and He will exalt you.
004:011 ¶ Brothers, do not slander one another. Anyone who speaks against his brother and°{CT or} judges him speaks against the law and judges it. And if you judge the law, you are not a practitioner of the law, but a judge of it.
004:012 There is only one Lawgiver,°{CT, F35, and GOC include and Judge.} the One who is able to save and destroy. But who are you to judge the other?°{CT to judge your neighbor?}
004:013 [[Do Not Boast about Tomorrow°{Proverbs 27:1}]] ¶ Come now, you who say, “Today and°{CT and Scrivener TR or} tomorrow let us go°{CT, GOC, and Scrivener TR we will go} to this or that city, spend a year there, carry on business, and make a profit.”
004:014 You do not even know what will happen tomorrow! What is your life? You are a mist that appears for a little while and then vanishes.
004:015 ¶ Instead, you ought to say, “If the Lord is willing, let us live°{GOC, CT, and Scrivener TR we will live} and do this or that.”
004:016 As it is, you boast in your proud intentions. All such boasting is evil.
004:017 Anyone, then, who knows the right thing to do, yet fails to do it, is guilty of sin.
005:001 [[A Warning to the Rich°{Proverbs 23:1–5; 1 Timothy 6:17–19}]] ¶ Come now, you who are rich, weep and wail over the misery to come upon you.
005:002 Your riches have rotted and moths have eaten your clothes.
005:003 Your gold and silver are corroded. Their corrosion will testify against you and consume your flesh like fire. ¶ You have hoarded treasure in the last days.
005:004 Look, the wages you withheld from the workmen who mowed your fields are crying out against you. The cries of the harvesters have reached the ears of the Lord of Hosts.
005:005 ¶ You have lived on earth in luxury and self-indulgence. You have fattened your hearts as°{CT does not include as.} in the day of slaughter.
005:006 You have condemned and murdered the righteous, who did not resist you.
005:007 [[Patience in Suffering°{Job 1:1–5}]] ¶ Be patient, then, brothers, until the Lord’s coming. See how the farmer awaits the precious fruit of the soil—how patient he is for the fall and spring rains.°{Literally for it until it receives the early and the late rain}
005:008 You, too, be patient and strengthen your hearts, because the Lord’s coming is near.
005:009 Do not complain about one another, brothers, so that you will not be judged.°{TR condemned} Look, the Judge is standing at the door!
005:010 ¶ My brothers,°{CT and F35 Brothers} as an example of patience in affliction, take the prophets who spoke in the name of the Lord.
005:011 See how blessed we consider those who have persevered. You have heard of Job’s perseverance and have seen the outcome from the Lord. He is°{CT and TR The Lord is} full of compassion and mercy.
005:012 ¶ Above all, my brothers, do not swear, not by heaven or earth or by any other oath. Simply let your “Yes” be yes, and your “No,” no, so that you will not fall into hypocrisy.°{CT and Scrivener TR under judgment}
005:013 [[The Prayer of Faith]] ¶ Is any one of you suffering? He should pray. Is anyone cheerful? He should sing praises.
005:014 Is any one of you sick? He should call the elders of the church to pray over him and anoint him with oil in the name of the Lord.
005:015 And the prayer offered in faith will restore the one who is sick. The Lord will raise him up. If he has sinned, he will be forgiven.
005:016 ¶ Confess your trespasses°{CT Therefore confess your sins} to each other and pray for each other so that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous man has great power to prevail.
005:017 Elijah was a man just like us. He prayed earnestly that it would not rain, and it did not rain on the land for three and a half years.
005:018 Again he prayed, and the heavens gave rain, and the earth yielded its crops.
005:019 [[Restoring a Sinner]] ¶ Brothers,°{CT My brothers} if one of you should wander from the truth and someone should bring him back,
005:020 consider this: Whoever turns a sinner from the error of his way will save a soul°{CT his soul} from death and cover over a multitude of sins.
