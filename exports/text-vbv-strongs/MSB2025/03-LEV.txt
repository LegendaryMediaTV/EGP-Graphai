001:001 [[Laws for Burnt Offerings°{Leviticus 6:8–13}]] ¶ Then the LORD called to Moses and spoke to him from the Tent of Meeting, saying,
001:002 “Speak to the Israelites and tell them: When any of you brings an offering to the LORD, you may bring as your offering an animal from the herd or the flock.
001:003 ¶ If his offering is a burnt offering from the herd, he is to present an unblemished male. He must bring it to the entrance to the Tent of Meeting for its acceptance before the LORD.
001:004 He is to lay his hand on the head of the burnt offering, so it can be accepted on his behalf to make atonement for him.
001:005 ¶ And he shall slaughter the young bull before the LORD, and Aaron’s sons the priests are to present the blood and splatter it on all sides of the altar at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.
001:006 Next, he is to skin the burnt offering and cut it into pieces.
001:007 ¶ The sons of Aaron the priest shall put a fire on the altar and arrange wood on the fire.
001:008 Then Aaron’s sons the priests are to arrange the pieces, including the head and the fat, atop the burning wood on the altar.
001:009 The entrails and legs must be washed with water, and the priest shall burn all of it on the altar as a burnt offering, a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.
001:010 ¶ If, however, one’s offering is a burnt offering from the flock—from the sheep or goats—he is to present an unblemished male.
001:011 He shall slaughter it on the north side of the altar before the LORD, and Aaron’s sons the priests are to splatter its blood against the altar on all sides.
001:012 He is to cut the animal into pieces, and the priest shall arrange them, including the head and fat, atop the burning wood that is on the altar.
001:013 The entrails and legs must be washed with water, and the priest shall present all of it and burn it on the altar; it is a burnt offering, a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.
001:014 ¶ If, instead, one’s offering to the LORD is a burnt offering of birds, he is to present a turtledove or a young pigeon.
001:015 Then the priest shall bring it to the altar, twist off its head, and burn it on the altar; its blood should be drained out on the side of the altar.
001:016 And he is to remove the crop with its contents°{Or the crop and feathers} and throw it to the east side of the altar, in the place for ashes.
001:017 He shall tear it open by its wings, without dividing the bird completely. And the priest is to burn it on the altar atop the burning wood. It is a burnt offering, a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.
002:001 [[Laws for Grain Offerings°{Leviticus 6:14–23}]] ¶ “When anyone brings a grain offering to the LORD, his offering must consist of fine flour. He is to pour olive oil on it, put frankincense on it,
002:002 and bring it to Aaron’s sons the priests. The priest shall take a handful of the flour and oil, together with all the frankincense, and burn this as a memorial portion on the altar, a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.
002:003 The remainder of the grain offering shall belong to Aaron and his sons; it is a most holy part of the food offerings to the LORD.
002:004 ¶ Now if you bring an offering of grain baked in an oven, it must consist of fine flour, either unleavened cakes mixed with oil or unleavened wafers coated with oil.
002:005 ¶ If your offering is a grain offering prepared on a griddle,°{That is, a shallow pan for baking or frying} it must be unleavened bread made of fine flour mixed with oil.
002:006 Crumble it and pour oil on it; it is a grain offering.
002:007 ¶ If your offering is a grain offering cooked in a pan,°{That is, a deep pan or stew pan} it must consist of fine flour with oil.
002:008 ¶ When you bring to the LORD the grain offering made in any of these ways, it is to be presented to the priest, and he shall take it to the altar.
002:009 The priest is to remove the memorial portion from the grain offering and burn it on the altar as a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.
002:010 But the remainder of the grain offering shall belong to Aaron and his sons; it is a most holy part of the food offerings to the LORD.
002:011 ¶ No grain offering that you present to the LORD may be made with leaven, for you are not to burn any leaven or honey as a food offering to the LORD.
002:012 You may bring them to the LORD as an offering of firstfruits, but they must not go up on the altar as a pleasing aroma.
002:013 And you shall season each of your grain offerings with salt. You must not leave the salt of the covenant of your God out of your grain offering; you are to add salt to each of your offerings.
002:014 ¶ If you bring a grain offering of firstfruits to the LORD, you shall offer crushed heads of new grain roasted on the fire.
002:015 And you are to put oil and frankincense on it; it is a grain offering.
002:016 The priest shall then burn the memorial portion of the crushed grain and the oil, together with all its frankincense, as a food offering to the LORD.
003:001 [[Laws for Peace Offerings°{Leviticus 7:11–21}]] ¶ “If one’s offering is a peace offering and he offers an animal from the herd, whether male or female, he must present it without blemish before the LORD.
003:002 He is to lay his hand on the head of the offering and slaughter it at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting. Then Aaron’s sons the priests shall splatter the blood on all sides of the altar.
003:003 ¶ From the peace offering he is to bring a food offering to the LORD: the fat that covers the entrails, all the fat that is on them,
003:004 both kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which he is to remove with the kidneys.
003:005 Then Aaron’s sons are to burn it on the altar atop the burnt offering that is on the burning wood, as a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.
003:006 ¶ If, however, one’s peace offering to the LORD is from the flock, he must present a male or female without blemish.
003:007 ¶ If he is presenting a lamb for his offering, he must present it before the LORD.
003:008 He is to lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it in front of the Tent of Meeting. Then Aaron’s sons shall splatter its blood on all sides of the altar.
003:009 ¶ And from the peace offering he shall bring a food offering to the LORD consisting of its fat: the entire fat tail cut off close to the backbone, the fat that covers the entrails, all the fat that is on them,
003:010 both kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which he is to remove with the kidneys.
003:011 Then the priest is to burn them on the altar as food, a food offering to the LORD.
003:012 ¶ If one’s offering is a goat, he is to present it before the LORD.
003:013 He must lay his hand on its head and slaughter it in front of the Tent of Meeting. Then Aaron’s sons shall splatter its blood on all sides of the altar.
003:014 ¶ And from his offering he shall present a food offering to the LORD: the fat that covers the entrails, all the fat that is on them,
003:015 both kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which he is to remove with the kidneys.
003:016 Then the priest is to burn the food on the altar as a food offering, a pleasing aroma. All the fat is the LORD’s.
003:017 ¶ This is a permanent statute for the generations to come, wherever you live: You must not eat any fat or any blood.”
004:001 [[Laws for Sin Offerings°{Leviticus 5:1–13; Leviticus 6:24–30}]] ¶ Then the LORD said to Moses,
004:002 “Tell the Israelites to do as follows with one who sins unintentionally against any of the LORD’s commandments and does what is forbidden by them:
004:003 ¶ If the anointed priest sins, bringing guilt on the people, he must bring to the LORD a young bull without blemish as a sin offering°{Or purification offering; here and throughout Leviticus} for the sin he has committed.
004:004 He must bring the bull to the entrance to the Tent of Meeting before the LORD, lay his hand on the bull’s head, and slaughter it before the LORD.
004:005 Then the anointed priest shall take some of the bull’s blood and bring it into the Tent of Meeting.
004:006 The priest is to dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle some of it seven times before the LORD, in front of the veil of the sanctuary.
004:007 The priest must then put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of fragrant incense that is before the LORD in the Tent of Meeting. And he is to pour out the rest of the bull’s blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.
004:008 Then he shall remove all the fat from the bull of the sin offering—the fat that covers the entrails, all the fat that is on them,
004:009 both kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which he is to remove with the kidneys—
004:010 just as the fat is removed from the ox of the peace offering. Then the priest shall burn them on the altar of burnt offering.
004:011 But the hide of the bull and all its flesh, with its head and legs and its entrails and dung—
004:012 all the rest of the bull—he must take outside the camp to a ceremonially clean place where the ashes are poured out, and there he must burn it on a wood fire on the ash heap.
004:013 ¶ Now if the whole congregation of Israel strays unintentionally and the matter escapes the notice of the assembly so that they violate any of the LORD’s commandments and incur guilt by doing what is forbidden,
004:014 when they become aware of the sin they have committed, then the assembly must bring a young bull as a sin offering and present it before the Tent of Meeting.
004:015 The elders of the congregation are to lay their hands on the bull’s head before the LORD, and it shall be slaughtered before the LORD.
004:016 Then the anointed priest is to bring some of the bull’s blood into the Tent of Meeting,
004:017 and he is to dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle it seven times before the LORD in front of the veil.
004:018 He is also to put some of the blood on the horns of the altar that is before the LORD in the Tent of Meeting, and he must pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.
004:019 And he is to remove all the fat from it and burn it on the altar.
004:020 He shall offer this bull just as he did the bull for the sin offering; in this way the priest will make atonement on their behalf, and they will be forgiven.
004:021 Then he is to take the bull outside the camp and burn it, just as he burned the first bull. It is the sin offering for the assembly.
004:022 ¶ When a leader sins unintentionally and does what is prohibited by any of the commandments of the LORD his God, he incurs guilt.
004:023 When he becomes aware of the sin he has committed, he must bring an unblemished male goat as his offering.
004:024 He is to lay his hand on the head of the goat and slaughter it at the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered before the LORD. It is a sin offering.
004:025 Then the priest is to take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger, put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar.
004:026 He must burn all its fat on the altar, like the fat of the peace offerings; thus the priest will make atonement for that man’s sin, and he will be forgiven.
004:027 ¶ And if one of the common people sins unintentionally and does what is prohibited by any of the LORD’s commandments, he incurs guilt.
004:028 When he becomes aware of the sin he has committed, he must bring an unblemished female goat as his offering for that sin.
004:029 He is to lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter it at the place of the burnt offering.
004:030 Then the priest is to take some of its blood with his finger, put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar.
004:031 Then he is to remove all the fat, just as it is removed from the peace offering, and the priest is to burn it on the altar as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. In this way the priest will make atonement for him, and he will be forgiven.
004:032 ¶ If, however, he brings a lamb as a sin offering, he must bring an unblemished female.
004:033 And he is to lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter it as a sin offering at the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered.
004:034 Then the priest is to take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger, put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out the rest of its blood at the base of the altar.
004:035 And he shall remove all the fat, just as the fat of the lamb is removed from the peace offerings, and he shall burn it on the altar along with the food offerings to the LORD. In this way the priest will make atonement for him for the sin he has committed, and he will be forgiven.
005:001 [[Sins Requiring a Sin Offering°{Leviticus 4:1–35; Leviticus 6:24–30}]] ¶ “If someone sins by failing to testify when he hears a public charge about something he has witnessed, whether he has seen it or learned of it, he shall bear the iniquity.
005:002 ¶ Or if a person touches anything unclean—whether the carcass of any unclean wild animal or livestock or crawling creature—even if he is unaware of it, he is unclean and guilty.
005:003 ¶ Or if he touches human uncleanness—anything by which one becomes unclean—even if he is unaware of it, when he realizes it, he is guilty.
005:004 ¶ Or if someone swears thoughtlessly with his lips to do anything good or evil—in whatever matter a man may rashly pronounce an oath—even if he is unaware of it, when he realizes it, he is guilty in the matter.
005:005 ¶ If someone incurs guilt in one of these ways, he must confess the sin he has committed,
005:006 and he must bring his guilt offering to the LORD for the sin he has committed: a female lamb or goat from the flock as a sin offering. And the priest will make atonement for him concerning his sin.
005:007 ¶ If, however, he cannot afford a lamb, he may bring to the LORD as restitution for his sin two turtledoves or two young pigeons—one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering.
005:008 He is to bring them to the priest, who shall first present the one for the sin offering. He is to twist its head at the front of its neck without severing it;
005:009 then he is to sprinkle some of the blood of the sin offering on the side of the altar, while the rest of the blood is drained out at the base of the altar. It is a sin offering.
005:010 And the priest must prepare the second bird as a burnt offering according to the ordinance. In this way the priest will make atonement for him for the sin he has committed, and he will be forgiven.
005:011 ¶ But if he cannot afford two turtledoves or two young pigeons, he may bring a tenth of an ephah of fine flour°{A tenth of an ephah is approximately 2 dry quarts or 2.2 liters (probably about 2.6 pounds or 1.2 kilograms of flour).} as a sin offering. He must not put olive oil or frankincense on it, because it is a sin offering.
005:012 He is to bring it to the priest, who shall take a handful from it as a memorial portion and burn it on the altar atop the food offerings to the LORD; it is a sin offering.
005:013 In this way the priest will make atonement for him for any of these sins he has committed, and he will be forgiven. The remainder will belong to the priest, like the grain offering.”
005:014 [[Laws for Guilt Offerings°{Leviticus 6:1–7; Leviticus 7:1–10}]] ¶ Then the LORD said to Moses,
005:015 “If someone acts unfaithfully and sins unintentionally against any of the LORD’s holy things, he must bring his guilt offering to the LORD: an unblemished ram from the flock, of proper value°{Or flock or its equivalence} in silver shekels according to the sanctuary shekel;°{A shekel is approximately 0.4 ounces or 11.4 grams of silver.} it is a guilt offering.
005:016 Regarding any holy thing he has harmed, he must make restitution by adding a fifth of its value to it and giving it to the priest, who will make atonement on his behalf with the ram as a guilt offering, and he will be forgiven.
005:017 ¶ If someone sins and violates any of the LORD’s commandments even though he was unaware, he is guilty and shall bear his punishment.
005:018 He is to bring to the priest an unblemished ram of proper value from the flock as a guilt offering. Then the priest will make atonement on his behalf for the wrong he has committed in ignorance, and he will be forgiven.
005:019 It is a guilt offering; he was certainly guilty°{Or he has paid full compensation} before the LORD.”
006:001 [[Sins Requiring a Guilt Offering°{Leviticus 5:14–19; Leviticus 7:1–10}]] ¶ And the LORD said to Moses,
006:002 “If someone sins and acts unfaithfully against the LORD by deceiving his neighbor in regard to a deposit or security entrusted to him or stolen, or if he extorts his neighbor
006:003 or finds lost property and lies about it and swears falsely, or if he commits any such sin that a man might commit—
006:004 once he has sinned and becomes guilty, he must return what he has stolen or taken by extortion, or the deposit entrusted to him, or the lost property he found,
006:005 or anything else about which he has sworn falsely. ¶ He must make restitution in full, add a fifth of the value, and pay it to the owner on the day he acknowledges his guilt.
006:006 Then he must bring to the priest his guilt offering to the LORD: an unblemished ram of proper value from the flock.
006:007 In this way the priest will make atonement for him before the LORD, and he will be forgiven for anything he may have done to incur guilt.”

006:010 ¶ And the priest shall put on his linen robe and linen undergarments, and he shall remove from the altar the ashes of the burnt offering that the fire has consumed and place them beside it.
006:011 Then he must take off his garments, put on other clothes, and carry the ashes outside the camp to a ceremonially clean place.
006:012 ¶ The fire on the altar shall be kept burning; it must not be extinguished. Every morning the priest is to add wood to the fire, arrange the burnt offering on it, and burn the fat portions of the peace offerings on it.

006:014 [[The Grain Offering°{Leviticus 2:1–16}]] ¶ Now this is the law of the grain offering: Aaron’s sons shall present it before the LORD in front of the altar.
006:015 The priest is to remove a handful of fine flour and olive oil, together with all the frankincense from the grain offering, and burn the memorial portion on the altar as a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

006:018 Any male among the sons of Aaron may eat it. This is a permanent portion from the food offerings to the LORD for the generations to come. Anything that touches them will become holy.”

006:024 [[The Sin Offering°{Leviticus 4:1–35; Leviticus 5:1–13}]] ¶ And the LORD said to Moses,

006:026 The priest who offers it shall eat it; it must be eaten in a holy place, in the courtyard of the Tent of Meeting.
006:027 Anything that touches its flesh will become holy, and if any of the blood is spattered on a garment, you must wash it in a holy place.
006:028 ¶ The clay pot in which the sin offering is boiled must be broken; if it is boiled in a bronze pot, the pot must be scoured and rinsed with water.
006:029 Any male among the priests may eat it; it is most holy.

007:004 both kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which is to be removed with the kidneys.
007:005 The priest shall burn them on the altar as a food offering to the LORD; it is a guilt offering.

007:007 ¶ The guilt offering is like the sin offering; the same law applies to both. It belongs to the priest who makes atonement with it.

007:011 [[The Peace Offering°{Leviticus 3:1–17}]] ¶ Now this is the law of the peace offering that one may present to the LORD:

027:001 [[Rules about Valuations]] ¶ Then the LORD said to Moses,
027:002 “Speak to the Israelites and say to them, ‘When someone makes a special vow to the LORD involving the value of persons,
027:003 if the valuation concerns a male from twenty to sixty years of age, then your valuation shall be fifty shekels of silver,°{50 shekels is approximately 1.26 pounds or 569.8 grams of silver; also in verse 16.} according to the sanctuary shekel.°{A shekel weighed approximately 0.4 ounces or 11.4 grams; also in verse 25.}
027:004 Or if it is a female, then your valuation shall be thirty shekels.°{30 shekels is approximately 12 ounces or 342 grams of silver.}
027:005 And if the person is from five to twenty years of age, then your valuation for the male shall be twenty shekels,°{20 shekels is approximately 8 ounces or 228 grams of silver.} and for the female ten shekels.°{10 shekels is approximately 4 ounces or 114 grams of silver; also in verse 7.}
027:006 ¶ Now if the person is from one month to five years of age, then your valuation for the male shall be five shekels of silver,°{5 shekels is approximately 2 ounces or 57 grams of silver.} and for the female three shekels of silver.°{3 shekels is approximately 1.2 ounces or 34.2 grams of silver.}
027:007 And if the person is sixty years of age or older, then your valuation shall be fifteen shekels°{15 shekels is approximately 6 ounces or 171 grams of silver.} for the male and ten shekels for the female.
027:008 But if the one making the vow is too poor to pay the valuation, he is to present the person°{Or present himself} before the priest, who shall set the value according to what the one making the vow can afford.
027:009 ¶ If he vows an animal that may be brought as an offering to the LORD, any such animal given to the LORD shall be holy.
027:010 He must not replace it or exchange it, either good for bad or bad for good. But if he does substitute one animal for another, both that animal and its substitute will be holy.
027:011 ¶ But if the vow involves any of the unclean animals that may not be brought as an offering to the LORD, the animal must be presented before the priest.
027:012 The priest shall set its value, whether high or low; as the priest values it, the price will be set.
027:013 If, however, the owner decides to redeem the animal, he must add a fifth to its value.
027:014 ¶ Now if a man consecrates his house as holy to the LORD, then the priest shall value it either as good or bad. The price will stand just as the priest values it.
027:015 But if he who consecrated his house redeems it, he must add a fifth to the assessed value, and it will belong to him.
027:016 ¶ If a man consecrates to the LORD a parcel of his land, then your valuation shall be proportional to the seed required for it—fifty shekels of silver for every homer of barley seed.°{A homer is a dry measure of approximately 6.24 bushels or 220 liters (probably about 291 pounds or 132 kilograms of barley seed).}
027:017 If he consecrates his field during the Year of Jubilee, the price will stand according to your valuation.
027:018 ¶ But if he consecrates his field after the Jubilee, the priest is to calculate the price in proportion to the years left until the next Year of Jubilee, so that your valuation will be reduced.
027:019 And if the one who consecrated the field decides to redeem it, he must add a fifth to the assessed value, and it shall belong to him.
027:020 ¶ If, however, he does not redeem the field, or if he has sold it to another man, it may no longer be redeemed.
027:021 When the field is released in the Jubilee, it will become holy, like a field devoted to the LORD; it becomes the property of the priests.
027:022 ¶ Now if a man consecrates to the LORD a field he has purchased, which is not a part of his own property,
027:023 then the priest shall calculate for him the value up to the Year of Jubilee, and the man shall pay the assessed value on that day as a sacred offering to the LORD.
027:024 In the Year of Jubilee the field shall return to the one from whom it was bought—the original owner of the land.
027:025 Every valuation will be according to the sanctuary shekel, twenty gerahs to the shekel.°{20 gerahs is equivalent to one shekel (approximately 0.4 ounces or 11.4 grams).}
027:026 ¶ But no one may consecrate a firstborn of the livestock, because a firstborn belongs to the LORD. Whether it is an ox or a sheep, it is the LORD’s.
027:027 But if it is among the unclean animals, then he may redeem it according to your valuation and add a fifth of its value. If it is not redeemed, then it shall be sold according to your valuation.
027:028 ¶ Nothing that a man sets apart°{Forms of the Hebrew cherem refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD; similarly in verse 29.} to the LORD from all he owns—whether a man, an animal, or his inherited land—can be sold or redeemed; everything so devoted is most holy to the LORD.
027:029 ¶ No person set apart for destruction may be ransomed; he must surely be put to death.
027:030 [[Instruction on Tithes°{Deuteronomy 14:22–29; Deuteronomy 26:1–15; Nehemiah 13:10–14}]] ¶ Thus any tithe from the land, whether from the seed of the land or the fruit of the trees, belongs to the LORD; it is holy to the LORD.
027:031 If a man wishes to redeem part of his tithe, he must add a fifth to its value.
027:032 ¶ Every tenth animal from the herd or flock that passes under the shepherd’s rod will be holy to the LORD.
027:033 He must not inspect whether it is good or bad, and he shall not make any substitution. But if he does make a substitution, both the animal and its substitute shall become holy; they cannot be redeemed.’”
027:034 ¶ These are the commandments that the LORD gave to Moses for the Israelites on Mount Sinai.
