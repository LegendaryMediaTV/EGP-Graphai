001:001 [[Greetings from Paul and Timothy°{Philippians 1:1–2; Colossians 1:1–2}]] ¶ Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus,°{F35 Jesus Christ} and Timothy our brother, ¶ To Philemon our beloved fellow worker,
001:002 to Apphia our beloved,°{CT our sister} to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church that meets at your°{You and Your are singular throughout this letter, except in verses 3, 22, and 25.} house:
001:003 ¶ Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
001:004 [[Philemon’s Faith and Love]] ¶ I always thank my God, remembering you in my prayers,
001:005 because I hear about your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints.
001:006 I pray that your partnership in the faith may become effective as you fully acknowledge every good thing that is ours in Christ Jesus.°{CT in Christ}
001:007 We take°{CT I take} great joy and encouragement in your love, because you, brother, have refreshed the hearts of the saints.
001:008 [[Paul’s Appeal for Onesimus]] ¶ So although in Christ I am bold enough to order you to do what is proper,
001:009 I prefer to appeal on the basis of love. For I, Paul, am now aged, and a prisoner of Jesus Christ°{CT of Christ Jesus} as well.
001:010 ¶ I appeal to you for my child Onesimus,°{Onesimus means useful (see verse 11) or beneficial (see verse 20).} whose father I became while I was in my°{CT does not include my.} chains.
001:011 Formerly he was useless to you, but now he has become useful both to you and to me.
001:012 I am sending back for you to receive°{CT I am sending back to you} him who is my very heart.
001:013 ¶ I would have liked to keep him with me, so that on your behalf he could minister to me in my chains for the gospel.
001:014 But I did not want to do anything without your consent, so that your goodness will not be out of compulsion, but by your own free will.
001:015 For perhaps this is why he was separated from you for a while, so that you might have him back for good—
001:016 no longer as a slave, but better than a slave, as a beloved brother. He is especially beloved to me, but even more so to you, both in person and in the Lord.
001:017 ¶ So if you consider me a partner, receive him as you would receive me.
001:018 But if he has wronged you in any way or owes you anything, charge it to my account.
001:019 I, Paul, write this with my own hand. I will repay it—not to mention that you owe me your very self.
001:020 ¶ Yes, brother, let me have some benefit°{Greek onaimēn, a play on the name Onesimus} from you in the Lord. Refresh my heart in the Lord.°{CT in Christ}
001:021 ¶ Confident of your obedience, I write to you, knowing that you will do even more than I ask.
001:022 ¶ In the meantime, prepare a guest room for me, because I hope that through your prayers I will be restored to you.
001:023 [[Additional Greetings]] ¶ Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, sends you greetings,
001:024 as do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers.
001:025 ¶ The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ°{NA, SBL, and WH of the Lord Jesus Christ; F35 of our Lord Jesus} be with your spirit. Amen.°{NA, SBL, and WH do not include Amen.}
