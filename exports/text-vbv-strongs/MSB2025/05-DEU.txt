034:010 Since that time, no prophet has risen in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face—
034:011 no prophet who did all the signs and wonders that the LORD sent Moses to do in the land of Egypt to Pharaoh and to all his officials and all his land,
034:012 and no prophet who performed all the mighty acts of power and awesome deeds°{Or terrifying deeds} that Moses did in the sight of all Israel.
