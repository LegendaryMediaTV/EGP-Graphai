001:001 [[The Beginning of Knowledge°{Proverbs 9:1–12}]] ¶ These are the proverbs of Solomon son of David,␤king of Israel,␤
001:002 for gaining wisdom and discipline,␤for comprehending words of insight,␤
001:003 and for receiving instruction in wise living␤and in righteousness, justice, and equity.␤
001:004 To impart prudence to the simple°{The Hebrew word rendered simple in Proverbs refers to one who is naive, without moral direction, or inclined to evil.}␤and knowledge and discretion to the young,␤
001:005 let the wise listen and gain instruction,␤and the discerning acquire wise counsel␤

001:007 ¶ The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge,␤but fools°{The Hebrew words rendered as fool in Proverbs denote one who is morally deficient.} despise wisdom and discipline.
001:008 [[The Enticement of Sin]] ¶ Listen, my son, to your father’s instruction,␤and do not forsake the teaching of your mother.␤

001:010 ¶ My son, if sinners entice you,␤do not yield to them.␤

001:016 For their feet run to evil,␤and they are swift to shed blood.␤

001:032 For the waywardness of the simple will slay them,␤and the complacency of fools will destroy them.␤
