001:001 [[Job’s Character and Wealth°{James 5:7–12}]] ¶ There was a man in the land of Uz whose name was Job. And this man was blameless and upright, fearing God and shunning evil.
001:002 He had seven sons and three daughters,
001:003 and he owned 7,000 sheep, 3,000 camels, 500 yoke of oxen, 500 female donkeys, and a very large number of servants. Job was the greatest man of all the people of the East.
001:004 ¶ Job’s sons would take turns holding feasts in their homes, and they would invite their three sisters to eat and drink with them.
001:005 ¶ And when the days of feasting were over, Job would send for his children to purify them, rising early in the morning to offer burnt offerings for all of them. For Job thought, “Perhaps my children have sinned and cursed God in their hearts.” This was Job’s regular practice.
001:006 [[Satan’s First Attack]] ¶ One day the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and Satan°{That is, the Accuser or the Adversary; here and throughout Job 1} also came with them.
001:007 ¶ “Where have you come from?” said the LORD to Satan. ¶ “From roaming through the earth,” he replied, “and walking back and forth in it.”
001:008 ¶ Then the LORD said to Satan, “Have you considered My servant Job? For there is no one on earth like him, a man who is blameless and upright, who fears God and shuns evil.”
001:009 ¶ Satan answered the LORD, “Does Job fear God for nothing?
001:010 Have You not placed a hedge on every side around him and his household and all that he owns? You have blessed the work of his hands, and his possessions have increased in the land.
001:011 But stretch out Your hand and strike all that he has, and he will surely curse You to Your face.”
001:012 ¶ “Very well,” said the LORD to Satan. “Everything he has is in your hands, but you must not lay a hand on the man himself.” ¶ Then Satan went out from the presence of the LORD.
001:013 [[Job Loses His Children and Possessions]] ¶ One day, while Job’s sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in their oldest brother’s house,
001:014 a messenger came and reported to Job: “While the oxen were plowing and the donkeys were grazing nearby,
001:015 the Sabeans swooped down and took them away. They put the servants to the sword, and I alone have escaped to tell you!”
001:016 ¶ While he was still speaking, another messenger came and reported: “The fire of God fell from heaven. It burned and consumed the sheep and the servants, and I alone have escaped to tell you!”
001:017 ¶ While he was still speaking, another messenger came and reported: “The Chaldeans formed three bands, raided the camels, and took them away. They put the servants to the sword, and I alone have escaped to tell you!”
001:018 ¶ While he was still speaking, another messenger came and reported: “Your sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in their oldest brother’s house,
001:019 when suddenly a mighty wind swept in from the desert and struck the four corners of the house. It collapsed on the young people and they are dead, and I alone have escaped to tell you!”
001:020 ¶ Then Job stood up, tore his robe, and shaved his head. He fell to the ground and worshiped,
001:021 saying: ¶ “Naked I came from my mother’s womb,␤and naked I will return.␤The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away.␤Blessed be the name of the LORD.”
001:022 ¶ In all this, Job did not sin or charge God with wrongdoing.

002:002 ¶ “Where have you come from?” said the LORD to Satan. ¶ “From roaming through the earth,” he replied, “and walking back and forth in it.”

002:011 [[Job’s Three Friends]] ¶ Now when Job’s three friends—Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite—heard about all this adversity that had come upon him, each of them came from his home, and they met together to go and sympathize with Job and comfort him.

042:001 [[Job Submits Himself to the LORD]] ¶ Then Job replied to the LORD:
042:002 ¶ “I know that You can do all things␤and that no plan of Yours can be thwarted.␤
042:003 You asked, ‘Who is this␤who conceals My counsel without knowledge?’°{Job 38:2}␤Surely I spoke of things I did not understand,␤things too wonderful for me to know.␤
042:004 You said, ‘Listen now, and I will speak.␤I will question you, and you shall inform Me.’°{Job 38:3 and Job 40:7}␤

042:007 [[The LORD Rebukes Job’s Friends]] ¶ After the LORD had spoken these words to Job, He said to Eliphaz the Temanite, “My wrath is kindled against you and your two friends. For you have not spoken about Me accurately, as My servant Job has.
042:008 So now, take seven bulls and seven rams, go to My servant Job, and sacrifice a burnt offering for yourselves. Then My servant Job will pray for you, for I will accept his prayer and not deal with you according to your folly. For you have not spoken accurately about Me, as My servant Job has.”
042:009 ¶ So Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite went and did as the LORD had told them; and the LORD accepted Job’s prayer.
042:010 [[The LORD Blesses Job]] ¶ After Job had prayed for his friends, the LORD restored his prosperity and doubled his former possessions.
042:011 All his brothers and sisters and prior acquaintances came and dined with him in his house. They consoled him and comforted him over all the adversity that the LORD had brought upon him. And each one gave him a piece of silver°{Hebrew a kesitah; the value or weight of the kesitah is no longer known.} and a gold ring.
042:012 ¶ So the LORD blessed Job’s latter days more than his first. He owned 14,000 sheep, 6,000 camels, 1,000 yoke of oxen, and 1,000 female donkeys.
042:013 And he also had seven sons and three daughters.
042:014 He named his first daughter Jemimah, his second Keziah, and his third Keren-happuch.
042:015 No women as beautiful as Job’s daughters could be found in all the land, and their father granted them an inheritance among their brothers.
042:016 ¶ After this, Job lived 140 years and saw his children and their children to the fourth generation.
042:017 And so Job died, old and full of years.
