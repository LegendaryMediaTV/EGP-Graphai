001:001 [[Nehemiah’s Prayer°{Deuteronomy 30:1–10}]] ¶ These are the words of Nehemiah son of Hacaliah: ¶ In the month of Chislev,°{Chislev is the ninth month of the Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of November and December.} in the twentieth year, while I was in the citadel of Susa,
001:002 Hanani, one of my brothers, arrived with men from Judah. So I questioned them about the remnant of the Jews who had survived the exile, and also about Jerusalem.
001:003 ¶ And they told me, “The remnant who survived the exile are there in the province, in great trouble and disgrace. The wall of Jerusalem is broken down, and its gates have been burned with fire.”
001:004 ¶ When I heard these words, I sat down and wept. I mourned for days, fasting and praying before the God of heaven.
001:005 ¶ Then I said: ¶ “O LORD, God of heaven, the great and awesome God who keeps His covenant of loving devotion°{Forms of the Hebrew chesed are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as loving devotion; the range of meaning includes love, goodness, kindness, faithfulness, and mercy, as well as loyalty to a covenant.} with those who love Him and keep His commandments,
001:006 let Your eyes be open and Your ears attentive to hear the prayer that I, Your servant, now pray before You day and night for Your servants, the Israelites. ¶ I confess the sins that we Israelites have committed against You. Both I and my father’s house have sinned.
001:007 We have behaved corruptly against You and have not kept the commandments, statutes, and ordinances that You gave Your servant Moses.
001:008 ¶ Remember, I pray, the word that You commanded Your servant Moses when You said, ‘If you are unfaithful, I will scatter you among the nations,
001:009 but if you return to Me and keep and practice My commandments, then even if your exiles have been banished to the farthest horizon,°{Or to the extremity of the heavens} I will gather them from there and bring them to the place I have chosen as a dwelling for My Name.’°{Deuteronomy 30:1–4}
001:010 ¶ They are Your servants and Your people. You redeemed them by Your great power and mighty hand.
001:011 O Lord, may Your ear be attentive to my prayer and to the prayers of Your servants°{Literally to the prayer of Your servant and to the prayer of Your servants} who delight to revere Your name. Give Your servant success this day, I pray, and grant him mercy in the sight of this man.” ¶ (At that time I was the cupbearer to the king.)
002:001 [[Nehemiah Sent to Jerusalem]] ¶ Now in the month of Nisan,°{Nisan is the first month of the Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of March and April.} in the twentieth year of King Artaxerxes, when wine was set before him, I took the wine and gave it to the king. I had never been sad in his presence,
002:002 so the king said to me, “Why is your face sad, though you are not ill? This could only be sadness of the heart.” ¶ I was overwhelmed with fear
002:003 and replied to the king, “May the king live forever! Why should I not be sad when the city where my fathers are buried lies in ruins, and its gates have been destroyed by fire?”
002:004 ¶ “What is your request?” replied the king. ¶ So I prayed to the God of heaven
002:005 and answered the king, “If it pleases the king, and if your servant has found favor in your sight, I ask that you send me to Judah, to the city where my fathers are buried, so that I may rebuild it.”
002:006 ¶ Then the king, with the queen seated beside him, asked me, “How long will your journey take, and when will you return?” So it pleased the king to send me, and I set a time.
002:007 ¶ I also said to him, “If it pleases the king, may letters be given to me for the governors west of the Euphrates,°{Hebrew beyond the River; also in verse 9} so that they will grant me safe passage until I reach Judah.
002:008 And may I have a letter to Asaph, keeper of the king’s forest, so that he will give me timber to make beams for the gates of the citadel to the temple, for the city wall, and for the house I will occupy.” ¶ And because the gracious hand of my God was upon me, the king granted my requests.
002:009 ¶ Then I went to the governors west of the Euphrates and gave them the king’s letters. The king had also sent army officers and cavalry with me.
002:010 ¶ But when Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the Ammonite official heard about this, they were deeply disturbed that someone had come to seek the well-being of the Israelites.
002:011 [[Nehemiah Inspects the Walls]] ¶ After I had arrived in Jerusalem and had been there three days,
002:012 I set out at night with a few men. I did not tell anyone what my God had laid on my heart to do for Jerusalem. The only animal with me was the one on which I was riding.
002:013 ¶ So I went out at night through the Valley Gate toward the Well of the Serpent°{Or Dragon or Jackal} and the Dung Gate, and I inspected the walls of Jerusalem that had been broken down and the gates that had been destroyed by fire.
002:014 ¶ Then I went on to the Fountain Gate and the King’s Pool, but there was no room for the animal under me to get through;
002:015 so I went up the valley by night and inspected the wall. Then I headed back and reentered through the Valley Gate.
002:016 ¶ The officials did not know where I had gone or what I was doing, for I had not yet told the Jews or priests or nobles or officials or any other workers.
002:017 Then I said to them, “You see the trouble we are in. Jerusalem lies in ruins, and its gates have been burned down. Come, let us rebuild the wall of Jerusalem, so that we will no longer be a disgrace.”
002:018 ¶ I also told them about the gracious hand of my God upon me, and what the king had said to me. ¶ “Let us start rebuilding,” they replied, and they set their hands to this good work.
002:019 ¶ But when Sanballat the Horonite, Tobiah the Ammonite official, and Geshem the Arab heard about this, they mocked us and ridiculed us, saying, “What is this you are doing? Are you rebelling against the king?”
002:020 ¶ So I answered them and said, “The God of heaven is the One who will grant us success. We, His servants, will start rebuilding, but you have no portion, right, or claim in Jerusalem.”

005:001 [[Nehemiah Defends the Oppressed]] ¶ About that time there was a great outcry from the people and their wives against their fellow Jews.
005:002 ¶ Some were saying, “We and our sons and daughters are numerous. We must get grain in order to eat and stay alive.”
005:003 ¶ Others were saying, “We are mortgaging our fields, our vineyards, and our homes to get grain during the famine.”
005:004 ¶ Still others were saying, “We have borrowed money to pay the king’s tax on our fields and vineyards.
005:005 We and our children are just like our countrymen and their children, yet we are subjecting our sons and daughters to slavery. Some of our daughters are already enslaved, but we are powerless to redeem them because our fields and vineyards belong to others.”
005:006 ¶ When I heard their outcry and these complaints, I became extremely angry,
005:007 and after serious thought I rebuked the nobles and officials, saying, “You are exacting usury from your own brothers!” ¶ So I called a large assembly against them
005:008 and said, “We have done our best to buy back our Jewish brothers who were sold to foreigners, but now you are selling your own brothers, that they may be sold back to us!” ¶ But they remained silent, for they could find nothing to say.
005:009 ¶ So I continued, “What you are doing is not right. Shouldn’t you walk in the fear of our God to avoid the reproach of our foreign enemies?
005:010 I, as well as my brothers and my servants, have been lending the people money and grain. Please, let us stop this usury.
005:011 Please restore to them immediately their fields, vineyards, olive groves, and houses, along with the percentage of the money, grain, new wine, and oil that you have been assessing them.”
005:012 ¶ “We will restore it,” they replied, “and will require nothing more from them. We will do as you say.” ¶ So I summoned the priests and required of the nobles and officials an oath that they would do what they had promised.
005:013 I also shook out the folds of my robe and said, “May God likewise shake out of his house and possessions every man who does not keep this promise. May such a man be shaken out and have nothing!” ¶ The whole assembly said, “Amen,” and they praised the LORD. And the people did as they had promised.
005:014 [[Nehemiah’s Generosity]] ¶ Furthermore, from the day King Artaxerxes appointed me to be their governor in the land of Judah, from his twentieth year until his thirty-second year (twelve years total), neither I nor my brothers ate the food allotted to the governor.
005:015 ¶ The governors before me had heavily burdened the people, taking from them bread and wine plus forty shekels of silver.°{40 shekels is approximately 1 pound or 453.6 grams of silver.} Their servants also oppressed the people. But I did not do this, because of my fear of God.
005:016 Instead, I devoted myself to the construction of the wall, and all my servants were gathered there for the work; we did not acquire any land.
005:017 ¶ There were 150 Jews and officials at my table, besides the guests from the surrounding nations.
005:018 Each day one ox, six choice sheep, and some fowl were prepared for me, and once every ten days an abundance of all kinds of wine was provided. But I did not demand the food allotted to the governor, because the burden on the people was so heavy.

012:044 [[Provisions for Temple Worship]] ¶ And on that same day men were appointed over the rooms that housed the supplies, contributions, firstfruits, and tithes. The portions specified by the Law for the priests and Levites were gathered into these storerooms from the fields of the villages, because Judah rejoiced over the priests and Levites who were serving.
012:045 ¶ They performed the service of their God and the service of purification, along with the singers and gatekeepers, as David and his son Solomon had prescribed.
012:046 For long ago, in the days of David and Asaph, there were directors for the singers and for the songs of praise and thanksgiving to God.
012:047 ¶ So in the days of Zerubbabel and Nehemiah, all Israel contributed the daily portions for the singers and gatekeepers. They also set aside daily portions for the Levites, and the Levites set aside daily portions for the descendants of Aaron.
013:001 [[Foreigners Excluded]] ¶ At that time the Book of Moses was read aloud in the hearing of the people, and in it they found the passage stating that no Ammonite or Moabite should ever enter the assembly of God,°{See Deuteronomy 23:3–6.}
013:002 because they had not met the Israelites with food and water, but had hired Balaam to call down a curse against them (although our God had turned the curse into a blessing).
013:003 ¶ As soon as the people heard this law, they excluded from Israel all of foreign descent.
013:004 [[The Temple Cleansed]] ¶ Now before this, Eliashib the priest, a relative of Tobiah, had been put in charge of the storerooms of the house of our God
013:005 and had prepared for Tobiah a large room where they had previously stored the grain offerings, the frankincense, the temple articles, and the tithes of grain, new wine, and oil prescribed for the Levites, singers, and gatekeepers, along with the contributions for the priests.
013:006 ¶ While all this was happening, I was not in Jerusalem, because I had returned to Artaxerxes king of Babylon°{Artaxerxes king of Persia is identified here as the king of Babylon because Persia had conquered the Babylonian Empire.} in the thirty-second year of his reign. Some time later I obtained leave from the king
013:007 to return to Jerusalem. Then I discovered the evil that Eliashib had done on behalf of Tobiah by providing him a room in the courts of the house of God.
013:008 ¶ And I was greatly displeased and threw all of Tobiah’s household goods out of the room.
013:009 Then I ordered that the rooms be purified, and I had the articles of the house of God restored to them, along with the grain offerings and frankincense.
013:010 [[Tithes Restored°{Leviticus 27:30–34; Deuteronomy 14:22–29; Deuteronomy 26:1–15}]] ¶ I also learned that because the portions for the Levites had not been given to them, all the Levites and singers responsible for performing the service had gone back to their own fields.
013:011 So I rebuked the officials and asked, “Why has the house of God been neglected?” ¶ Then I gathered the Levites and singers together and stationed them at their posts,
013:012 and all Judah brought a tenth of the grain, new wine, and oil into the storerooms.
013:013 I appointed as treasurers over the storerooms Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe, and Pedaiah of the Levites, with Hanan son of Zaccur, the son of Mattaniah, to assist them, because they were considered trustworthy. They were responsible for distributing the supplies to their fellow Levites.
013:014 ¶ Remember me for this, O my God, and do not blot out my deeds of loving devotion for the house of my God and for its services.
013:015 [[The Sabbath Restored°{Jeremiah 17:19–27}]] ¶ In those days I saw people in Judah treading winepresses on the Sabbath and bringing in grain and loading it on donkeys, along with wine, grapes, and figs. All kinds of goods were being brought into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day. So I warned them against selling food on that day.
013:016 Additionally, men of Tyre who lived there were importing fish and all kinds of merchandise and selling them on the Sabbath to the people of Judah in Jerusalem.
013:017 ¶ Then I rebuked the nobles of Judah and asked, “What is this evil you are doing—profaning the Sabbath day?
013:018 Did not your forefathers do the same things, so that our God brought all this disaster on us and on this city? And now you are rekindling His wrath against Israel by profaning the Sabbath!”
013:019 ¶ When the evening shadows began to fall on the gates of Jerusalem, just before the Sabbath, I ordered that the gates be shut and not opened until after the Sabbath. I posted some of my servants at the gates so that no load could enter on the Sabbath day.
013:020 ¶ Once or twice, the merchants and those who sell all kinds of goods camped outside Jerusalem,
013:021 but I warned them, “Why are you camping in front of the wall? If you do it again, I will lay hands on you.” From that time on, they did not return on the Sabbath.
013:022 Then I instructed the Levites to purify themselves and guard the gates in order to keep the Sabbath day holy. ¶ Remember me for this as well, O my God, and show me mercy according to Your abundant loving devotion.
013:023 [[Intermarriage Forbidden°{Ezra 9:1–4}]] ¶ In those days I also saw Jews who had married women from Ashdod, Ammon, and Moab.
013:024 Half of their children spoke the language of Ashdod or of the other peoples, but could not speak the language of Judah.
013:025 I rebuked them and called down curses on them. I beat some of these men and pulled out their hair. ¶ Then I made them take an oath before God and said, “You must not give your daughters in marriage to their sons or take their daughters as wives for your sons or for yourselves!
013:026 Did not King Solomon of Israel sin in matters like this? There was not a king like him among many nations, and he was loved by his God, who made him king over all Israel—yet foreign women drew him into sin.
013:027 Must we now hear that you too are doing all this terrible evil and acting unfaithfully against our God by marrying foreign women?”
013:028 ¶ Even one of the sons of Jehoiada son of Eliashib the high priest had become a son-in-law to Sanballat the Horonite. Therefore I drove him away from me.
013:029 ¶ Remember them, O my God, because they have defiled the priesthood and the covenant of the priesthood and of the Levites.
013:030 ¶ Thus I purified the priests and Levites from everything foreign, and I assigned specific duties to each of the priests and Levites.
013:031 I also arranged for contributions of wood at the appointed times, and for the firstfruits. ¶ Remember me, O my God, with favor.
