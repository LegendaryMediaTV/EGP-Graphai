001:001 [[Greetings to the Thessalonians°{1 Thessalonians 1:1–10}]] ¶ Paul, Silvanus,°{That is, Silas} and Timothy, ¶ To the church of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:
001:002 ¶ Grace and peace to you from God our Father°{SBL, NE, and WH God the Father} and the Lord Jesus Christ.
001:003 ¶ We are obligated to thank God for you all the time, brothers, as is fitting, because your faith is growing more and more, and your love for one another is increasing.
001:004 That is why we boast among God’s churches about your perseverance and faith in the face of all the persecution and affliction you are enduring.
001:005 [[Christ’s Coming]] ¶ All this is clear evidence of God’s righteous judgment. And so you will be counted worthy of the kingdom of God, for which you are suffering.
001:006 After all, it is only right for God to repay with affliction those who afflict you,
001:007 and to grant relief to you who are oppressed and to us as well. This will take place when the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven with His mighty angels
001:008 in blazing fire, inflicting vengeance on those who do not know God and do not obey the gospel of our Lord Jesus.°{GOC, ALT, HF, F35, and TR our Lord Jesus Christ}
001:009 They will suffer the penalty of eternal destruction, separated from the presence of the Lord and the glory of His might,
001:010 on the day He comes to be glorified in His saints and regarded with wonder by all who have believed, including you who have believed our testimony.
001:011 ¶ To this end, we always pray for you, that our God will count you worthy of His calling, and that He will powerfully fulfill your every good desire and work of faith,
001:012 so that the name of our Lord Jesus°{GOC, ALT, F35, and TR the name of our Lord Jesus Christ} will be glorified in you, and you in Him, according to the grace of our God and of the Lord Jesus Christ.°{Or the grace of our God and Lord, Jesus Christ.}
002:001 [[The Man of Sin]] ¶ Now concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our being gathered together to Him, we ask you, brothers,
002:002 not to be easily disconcerted or alarmed by any spirit or message or letter seeming to be from us, alleging that the Day of Christ°{CT Day of the Lord} has already come.
002:003 Let no one deceive you in any way, for it will not come until the rebellion occurs and the man of sin°{CT the man of lawlessness}—the son of destruction—is revealed.
002:004 He will oppose and exalt himself above every so-called god or object of worship. So he will seat himself as God°{CT does not include as God.} in the temple of God, proclaiming himself to be God.
002:005 ¶ Do you not remember that I told you these things while I was still with you?
002:006 And you know what is now restraining him, so that he may be revealed at the proper time.
002:007 For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work, but the one who now restrains it will continue until he is taken out of the way.
002:008 And then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord will consume°{CT the Lord Jesus will slay} with the breath of His mouth and annihilate by the majesty of His arrival.
002:009 ¶ The coming of the lawless one will be accompanied by the working of Satan, with every kind of power, sign, and false wonder,
002:010 and with every wicked deception directed against those who are perishing, because they refused the love of the truth that would have saved them.
002:011 For this reason God will send them a powerful delusion so that they believe the lie,
002:012 in order that judgment may come upon all who have disbelieved the truth and delighted in wickedness.
002:013 [[Stand Firm]] ¶ But we should always thank God for you, brothers who are loved by the Lord, because God has chosen you from the beginning°{NA and SBL God has chosen you as the firstfruits} to be saved by the sanctification of the Spirit and by faith in the truth.
002:014 To this He called you through our gospel, so that you may share in the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.
002:015 Therefore, brothers, stand firm and cling to the traditions we taught you, whether by speech or by letter.
002:016 ¶ Now may our Lord Jesus Christ Himself and our God and Father,°{CT and God our Father} who by grace has loved us and given us eternal comfort and good hope,
002:017 encourage your hearts and strengthen you in every good word and deed.
003:001 [[Request for Prayer]] ¶ Finally, brothers, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may spread quickly and be held in honor, just as it was with you.
003:002 And pray that we may be delivered from wicked and evil men; for not everyone holds to the faith.
003:003 But the Lord is faithful, and He will strengthen you and guard you from the evil one.°{Or from evil}
003:004 And we have confidence in the Lord that you are doing and will continue to do what we command you.°{CT what we command}
003:005 May the Lord direct your hearts into God’s love and Christ’s perseverance.
003:006 [[A Warning against Idleness]] ¶ Now we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to keep away from any brother who leads an undisciplined life that is not in keeping with the tradition you received°{Or they received} from us.
003:007 For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we were not undisciplined among you,
003:008 nor did we eat anyone’s food without paying for it. Instead, in labor and toil, we worked night and day so that we would not be a burden to any of you.
003:009 Not that we lack this right, but we wanted to offer ourselves as an example for you to imitate.
003:010 For even while we were with you, we gave you this command: “If anyone is unwilling to work, he shall not eat.”
003:011 ¶ Yet we hear that some of you are leading undisciplined lives and accomplishing nothing but being busybodies.
003:012 We command and urge such people by our Lord Jesus Christ to begin working quietly to earn their own living.
003:013 But as for you, brothers, do not grow weary in well-doing.
003:014 ¶ Take note of anyone who does not obey the instructions we have given in this letter. Do not associate with him, so that he may be ashamed.
003:015 Yet do not regard him as an enemy, but warn him as a brother.
003:016 [[Signature and Final Greetings°{1 Corinthians 16:19–24; Colossians 4:15–18}]] ¶ Now may the Lord of peace Himself give you peace at all times and in every way. The Lord be with all of you.
003:017 ¶ This greeting is in my own hand—Paul. This is my mark in every letter; it is the way I write.
003:018 ¶ The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you. Amen.°{CT does not include Amen.}
