001:001 [[A Greeting from the Elder°{2 John 1:1–3}]] ¶ The elder, ¶ To the beloved Gaius, whom I love in the truth:
001:002 ¶ Beloved, I pray that in every way you may prosper and enjoy good health, as your soul also prospers.
001:003 For I was overjoyed when the brothers came and testified about your devotion to the truth, in which you continue to walk.
001:004 I have no greater joy°{WH grace} than to hear that my children are walking in truth.°{CT in the truth}
001:005 [[Gaius Commended for Hospitality]] ¶ Beloved, you are faithful in what you are doing for the brothers and for those who are°{CT even though they are} strangers to you.
001:006 They have testified to the church about your love. You will do well to send them on their way in a manner worthy of God.
001:007 For they went out on behalf of the Name,°{Scrivener TR His name} accepting nothing from the Gentiles.
001:008 Therefore we ought to receive°{CT to support} such men, so that we may be fellow workers for the truth.
001:009 [[Diotrephes and Demetrius]] ¶ I have written to the church about this,°{Literally I have written to the church; CT I have written something to the church} but Diotrephes, who loves to be first, will not accept our instruction.
001:010 So if I come, I will call attention to his malicious slander against us. And unsatisfied with that, he refuses to welcome the brothers and forbids those who want to do so, even putting them out of the church.
001:011 ¶ Beloved, do not imitate what is evil, but what is good. The one who does good is of God; the one who does evil has not seen God.
001:012 ¶ Demetrius has received a good testimony from everyone, and from the truth itself. We also testify for him, and you know°{F35 we know} that our testimony is true.
001:013 [[Conclusion°{2 John 1:12–13}]] ¶ I have many things to write,°{CT to write to you} but I would prefer not to do so with pen and ink.
001:014 Instead, I hope to see you soon and speak with you face to face.°{Literally and we will speak mouth to mouth; some translators begin a new verse (15) after face to face.} ¶ Peace to you. ¶ The friends here send you greetings. ¶ Greet each of our friends there by name.
