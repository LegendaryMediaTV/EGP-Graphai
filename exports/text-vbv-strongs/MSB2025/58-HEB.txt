001:001 [[The Supremacy of the Son°{Colossians 1:15–23}]] ¶ On many past occasions and in many different ways, God spoke to our fathers through the prophets.
001:002 But in these last days He has spoken to us by His Son,°{Or in His Son} whom He appointed heir of all things, and through whom He made the universe.°{Or the world; literally the ages}
001:003 ¶ The Son is the radiance of God’s glory and the exact representation of His nature, upholding all things by His powerful word. After He had provided purification for our sins,°{CT for sins} He sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high.
001:004 So He became as far superior to the angels as the name He has inherited is excellent beyond theirs.
001:005 For to which of the angels did God ever say: ¶ “You are My Son;␤today I have become Your Father”?°{Psalm 2:7; literally today I have begotten You} ¶ Or again: ¶ “I will be His Father,␤and He will be My Son”?°{2 Samuel 7:14; 1 Chronicles 17:13}
001:006 ¶ And again, when God brings His firstborn into the world, He says: ¶ “Let all God’s angels worship Him.”°{Deuteronomy 32:43 (see DSS and LXX)}
001:007 ¶ Now about the angels He says: ¶ “He makes His angels winds,␤His servants flames of fire.”°{Psalm 104:4 (see also LXX)}
001:008 ¶ But about the Son He says: ¶ “Your throne, O God, endures forever and ever,␤and justice is the scepter of Your kingdom.°{Literally the scepter of justice is the scepter of Your kingdom}␤
001:009 You have loved righteousness␤and hated wickedness;␤therefore God, Your God, has anointed You␤above Your companions with the oil of joy.”°{Psalm 45:6–7}
001:010 ¶ And: ¶ “In the beginning, O Lord, You laid the foundations of the earth,␤and the heavens are the work of Your hands.␤
001:011 They will perish, but You remain;␤they will all wear out like a garment.␤
001:012 You will roll them up like a robe;␤they will be changed;°{NA, SBL, and WH like a garment they will be changed}␤but You remain the same,␤and Your years will never end.”°{Psalm 102:25–27}
001:013 ¶ Yet to which of the angels did God ever say: ¶ “Sit at My right hand␤until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet”?°{Psalm 110:1}
001:014 ¶ Are not the angels°{Literally Are they not all} ministering spirits sent to serve those who will inherit salvation?
002:001 [[Salvation Confirmed]] ¶ We must pay closer attention, therefore, to what we have heard, so that we do not drift away.
002:002 For if the message spoken by angels was binding, and every transgression and disobedience received its just punishment,
002:003 how shall we escape if we neglect such a great salvation? ¶ This salvation was first announced by the Lord, was confirmed to us by those who heard Him,
002:004 and was affirmed by God through signs, wonders, various miracles, and gifts of the Holy Spirit distributed according to His will.
002:005 [[Jesus like His Brothers]] ¶ For it is not to angels that He has subjected the world to come, about which we are speaking.
002:006 But somewhere it is testified in these words: ¶ “What is man that You are mindful of him,␤or the son of man that You care for him?␤
002:007 You made him a little lower°{Or a little while lower; also in verse 9} than the angels;␤You crowned him with glory and honor,°{WH and TR include and set him over the works of Your hands.}␤
002:008 and placed everything under his feet.”°{Psalm 8:4–6 (see also LXX)} ¶ When God subjected all things to him, He left nothing outside of his control. Yet at present we do not see everything subject to him.
002:009 But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels, now crowned with glory and honor because He suffered death, so that by the grace of God He might taste death for everyone.
002:010 ¶ In bringing many sons to glory, it was fitting for God, for whom and through whom all things exist, to make the author°{Or pioneer or founder} of their salvation perfect through suffering.
002:011 For both the One who sanctifies and those who are sanctified are of the same family. So Jesus is not ashamed to call them brothers.
002:012 He says: ¶ “I will proclaim Your name to My brothers;␤I will sing Your praises in the assembly.”°{Psalm 22:22 (see also LXX)}
002:013 ¶ And again: ¶ “I will put My trust in Him.”°{Isaiah 8:17} ¶ And once again: ¶ “Here am I, and the children God has given Me.”°{Isaiah 8:18}
002:014 ¶ Now since the children have flesh and blood, He too shared in their humanity, so that by His death He might destroy him who holds the power of death, that is, the devil,
002:015 and free those who all their lives were held in slavery by their fear of death.
002:016 ¶ For surely it is not the angels He helps, but the descendants of Abraham.
002:017 For this reason He had to be made like His brothers in every way, so that He might become a merciful and faithful high priest in service to God, in order to make atonement°{Or to make propitiation} for the sins of the people.
002:018 Because He Himself suffered when He was tempted, He is able to help those who are being tempted.
003:001 [[Jesus Our Apostle and High Priest]] ¶ Therefore, holy brothers, who share in the heavenly calling, set your focus on Jesus Christ,°{CT on Jesus; TR on Christ Jesus} the apostle and high priest whom we confess.
003:002 He was faithful to the One who appointed Him, just as Moses was faithful in all God’s house.°{Literally just as Moses in all His house; SBL just as Moses in His house}
003:003 ¶ For Jesus has been counted worthy of greater glory than Moses, just as the builder of a house has greater honor than the house itself.
003:004 And every house is built by someone, but God is the builder of everything.
003:005 ¶ Now Moses was faithful as a servant in all God’s house,°{Numbers 12:7} testifying to what would be spoken later.
003:006 But Christ is faithful as the Son over God’s house. And we are His house, if we hold firmly to the end°{NE, WH, MT, and TR; other CT do not include the end.} our confidence and the hope of which we boast.
003:007 [[Do Not Harden Your Hearts°{Psalm 95:1–11}]] ¶ Therefore, as the Holy Spirit says: ¶ “Today, if you hear His voice,␤

003:012 [[The Peril of Unbelief]] ¶ See to it, brothers, that none of you has a wicked heart of unbelief that turns away from the living God.
003:013 But exhort one another daily, as long as it is called today, so that none of you may be hardened by sin’s deceitfulness.
003:014 ¶ We have come to share in Christ if we hold firmly to the end the assurance we had at first.
003:015 As it has been said: ¶ “Today, if you hear His voice,␤do not harden your hearts,␤as you did in the rebellion.”°{Psalm 95:7–8}

003:018 And to whom did He swear that they would never enter His rest? Was it not to those who disobeyed?
003:019 So we see that it was because of their unbelief that they were unable to enter.
004:001 [[The Sabbath Rest°{Genesis 2:1–3; Exodus 16:22–30}]] ¶ Therefore, while the promise of entering His rest still stands, let us be careful that none of you be deemed to have fallen short of it.
004:002 For we also received the good news just as they did; but the message they heard was of no value to them, since they did not share the faith of those who comprehended it.°{Literally not having been united in the faith of those who heard}
004:003 ¶ Now we who have believed enter that rest. As for the others, it is just as God has said: ¶ “So I swore on oath in My anger,␤‘They shall never enter My rest.’”°{Psalm 95:11; also in verse 5} ¶ And yet His works have been finished since the foundation of the world.
004:004 For somewhere He has spoken about the seventh day in this manner: “And on the seventh day God rested from all His works.”°{Genesis 2:2}
004:005 And again, as He says in the passage above: “They shall never enter My rest.”
004:006 ¶ Since, then, it remains for some to enter His rest, and since those who formerly heard the good news did not enter because of their disobedience,
004:007 God again designated a certain day as “Today,” when a long time later He spoke through David as was just stated: “Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts.”°{Psalm 95:7–8}
004:008 ¶ For if Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken later about another day.
004:009 There remains, then, a Sabbath rest for the people of God.
004:010 For whoever enters God’s rest also rests from his own work, just as God did from His.
004:011 Let us, therefore, make every effort to enter that rest, so that no one will fall by following the same pattern of disobedience.
004:012 [[The Living Word°{2 Timothy 3:10–17}]] ¶ For the word of God is living and active. Sharper than any double-edged sword, it pierces even to dividing soul and spirit, joints and marrow. It judges the thoughts and intentions of the heart.
004:013 Nothing in all creation is hidden from God’s sight; everything is uncovered and exposed before the eyes of Him to whom we must give account.
004:014 ¶ Therefore, since we have a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold firmly to what we profess.
004:015 For we do not have a high priest who is unable to sympathize with our weaknesses, but we have one who was tempted in every way that we are, yet was without sin.
004:016 Let us then approach the throne of grace with confidence, so that we may receive mercy and find grace to help us in our time of need.
005:001 [[The Perfect High Priest°{Psalm 110:1–7}]] ¶ Every high priest is appointed from among men to represent them in matters relating to God, to offer gifts and sacrifices for sins.
005:002 He is able to deal gently with those who are ignorant and misguided, since he himself is beset by weakness.
005:003 That is why he is obligated to offer sacrifices for his own sins, as well as for the sins of the people.
005:004 ¶ No one takes this honor upon himself; he must be called by God, just as Aaron was.
005:005 So also Christ did not take upon Himself the glory of becoming a high priest, but He was called by the One who said to Him: ¶ “You are My Son;␤today I have become Your Father.”°{Psalm 2:7; literally today I have begotten You}
005:006 ¶ And in another passage God says: ¶ “You are a priest forever␤in the order of Melchizedek.”°{Psalm 110:4}
005:007 ¶ During the days of Jesus’ earthly life, He offered up prayers and petitions with loud cries and tears to the One who could save Him from death, and He was heard because of His reverence.
005:008 Although He was a Son, He learned obedience from what He suffered.
005:009 And having been made perfect, He became the source of eternal salvation to all who obey Him
005:010 and was designated by God as high priest in the order of Melchizedek.
005:011 [[Milk and Solid Food°{1 Corinthians 3:1–9}]] ¶ We have much to say about this, but it is hard to explain, because you are dull of hearing.
005:012 Although by this time you ought to be teachers, you need someone to reteach you the basic principles of God’s word.°{Or of the oracles of God} You need milk, not solid food!
005:013 ¶ For everyone who lives on milk is still an infant, inexperienced in the message of righteousness.
005:014 But solid food is for the mature, who by constant use have trained their senses to distinguish good from evil.
006:001 [[A Call to Maturity]] ¶ Therefore let us leave the elementary teachings about Christ and go on to maturity, not laying again the foundation of repentance from dead works,°{Or from acts that lead to death} and of faith in God,
006:002 instruction about baptisms,°{Or cleansing rites} the laying on of hands, the resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgment.
006:003 And this we will do, if God permits.
006:004 ¶ It is impossible for those who have once been enlightened, who have tasted the heavenly gift, who have shared in the Holy Spirit,
006:005 who have tasted the goodness of the word of God and the powers of the coming age—
006:006 and then have fallen away—to be restored to repentance, because they themselves are crucifying the Son of God all over again and subjecting Him to open shame.
006:007 ¶ For land that drinks in the rain often falling on it and that produces a crop useful to those for whom it is tended receives the blessing of God.
006:008 But land that produces thorns and thistles is worthless, and its curse is imminent. In the end it will be burned.
006:009 ¶ Even though we speak like this, beloved, we are convinced of better things in your case—things that accompany salvation.
006:010 For God is not unjust. He will not forget your work and the labor of love°{CT your work and the love} you have shown for His name as you have ministered to the saints and continue to do so.
006:011 ¶ We want each of you to show this same diligence to the very end, in order to make your hope sure.
006:012 Then you will not be sluggish, but will imitate those who through faith and patience inherit what has been promised.
006:013 [[God’s Unchangeable Promise]] ¶ When God made His promise to Abraham, since He had no one greater to swear by, He swore by Himself,
006:014 saying, “I will surely bless you and multiply your descendants.”°{Genesis 22:17}
006:015 And so Abraham, after waiting patiently, obtained the promise.
006:016 ¶ Men indeed°{CT does not include indeed.} swear by someone greater than themselves, and their oath serves as a confirmation to end all argument.
006:017 So when God wanted to make the unchanging nature of His purpose very clear to the heirs of the promise, He guaranteed it with an oath.
006:018 Thus by two unchangeable things in which it is impossible for God to lie, we who have fled to take hold of the hope set before us may be strongly encouraged.
006:019 ¶ We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure. It enters the inner sanctuary behind the curtain,
006:020 where Jesus our forerunner has entered on our behalf. He has become a high priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.
007:001 [[Melchizedek and Abraham°{Genesis 14:17–24}]] ¶ This Melchizedek was king of Salem and priest of God Most High.°{Genesis 14:18} He met Abraham returning from the slaughter of the kings and blessed him,
007:002 and Abraham apportioned to him a tenth of everything. First, his name means “king of righteousness.” Then also, “king of Salem” means “king of peace.”
007:003 Without father or mother or genealogy, without beginning of days or end of life, like the Son of God, he remains a priest for all time.
007:004 ¶ Consider how great Melchizedek was: Even the patriarch Abraham gave him a tenth of the plunder.
007:005 Now the law commands the sons of Levi who become priests to collect a tenth from the people—that is, from their brothers—though they too are descended from Abraham.
007:006 But Melchizedek, who did not trace his descent from Levi, collected a tenth from Abraham and blessed him who had the promises.
007:007 And indisputably, the lesser is blessed by the greater.
007:008 ¶ In the case of the Levites, mortal men collect the tenth; but in the case of Melchizedek, it is affirmed that he lives on.
007:009 And so to speak, Levi, who collects the tenth, paid the tenth through Abraham.
007:010 For when Melchizedek met Abraham, Levi was still in the loin of his ancestor.
007:011 [[A Superior Priesthood]] ¶ Now if perfection could have been attained through the Levitical priesthood (for on this basis the people received the law), why was there still need for another priest to appear—one in the order of Melchizedek and not in the order of Aaron?
007:012 For when the priesthood is changed, the law must be changed as well.
007:013 ¶ He of whom these things are said belonged to a different tribe, from which no one has ever served at the altar.
007:014 For it is clear that our Lord descended from Judah, a tribe as to which Moses said nothing about priesthood.°{CT about priests}
007:015 ¶ And this point is even more clear if another priest like Melchizedek appears,
007:016 one who has become a priest not by a law of succession, but by the power of an indestructible life.
007:017 For it is testified:°{Literally For He testifies; CT For it is testified (of Him)} ¶ “You are a priest forever␤in the order of Melchizedek.”°{Psalm 110:4}
007:018 ¶ So the former commandment is set aside because it was weak and useless
007:019 (for the law made nothing perfect), and a better hope is introduced, by which we draw near to God.
007:020 ¶ And none of this happened without an oath. For others became priests without an oath,
007:021 but Jesus became a priest with an oath by the One who said to Him: ¶ “The Lord has sworn and will not change His mind:␤‘You are a priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.’”°{Psalm 110:4; CT does not include in the order of Melchizedek.}
007:022 ¶ Because of this oath, Jesus has become the guarantee of a better covenant.
007:023 ¶ Now there have been many other priests, since death prevented them from continuing in office.
007:024 But because Jesus lives forever, He has a permanent priesthood.
007:025 Therefore He is able to save completely°{Or forever} those who draw near to God through Him, since He always lives to intercede for them.
007:026 ¶ Such a high priest truly befits us—One who is holy, innocent, undefiled, set apart from sinners, and exalted above the heavens.
007:027 Unlike the other high priests, He does not need to offer daily sacrifices, first for His own sins and then for the sins of the people; He sacrificed for sin once for all when He offered up Himself.
007:028 For the law appoints as high priests men who are weak; but the oath, which came after the law, appointed the Son, who has been made perfect forever.
008:001 [[Christ’s Eternal Priesthood]] ¶ The point of what we are saying is this: We do have such a high priest, who sat down at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heaven,
008:002 and who ministers in the sanctuary and true tabernacle set up by the Lord, not by man.
008:003 And since every high priest is appointed to offer both gifts and sacrifices, it was necessary for this One also to have something to offer.
008:004 ¶ For°{CT Now} if He were on earth, He would not be a priest, since there are already priests who offer gifts according to the law.
008:005 The place where they serve is a copy and shadow of what is in heaven. This is why Moses was warned when he was about to build the tabernacle: “See to it that you make everything according to the pattern shown you on the mountain.”°{Exodus 25:40; see also Exodus 26:30.}
008:006 [[The New Covenant°{Jeremiah 31:26–40}]] ¶ Now, however, Jesus has received a much more excellent ministry, just as the covenant He mediates is better and is founded on better promises.
008:007 For if that first covenant had been without fault, no place would have been sought for a second.
008:008 But God found fault with the people and said: ¶ “Behold, the days are coming, declares the Lord,␤when I will make a new covenant␤with the house of Israel␤and with the house of Judah.␤
008:009 It will not be like the covenant␤I made with their fathers␤when I took them by the hand␤to lead them out of the land of Egypt,␤because they did not abide by My covenant,␤and I disregarded them, declares the Lord.␤
008:010 For this is the covenant I will make␤with the house of Israel␤after those days,␤declares the Lord.␤I will put My laws in their minds␤and inscribe them on their hearts.␤And I will be their God,␤and they will be My people.␤
008:011 No longer will each one teach his neighbor°{Or his fellow citizen} or his brother,␤saying, ‘Know the Lord,’␤because they will all know Me,␤from the least of them to the greatest.␤
008:012 For I will forgive their iniquities␤and will remember their sins and lawless acts°{CT does not include and lawless acts.} no more.”°{Jeremiah 31:31–34 (see also LXX)}

A broader interpretation could also include priesthood or tabernacle.} He has made the first one obsolete; and what is obsolete and aging will soon disappear.
009:001 [[The Earthly Tabernacle°{Exodus 40:1–33; Acts 7:44–47}]] ¶ Now even°{SBL Now} the first covenant°{Literally the first; also in verse 18; see the footnote for Hebrews 8:13.} had regulations for worship and also an earthly sanctuary.
009:002 A tabernacle was prepared. In its first room were the lampstand, the table, and the consecrated bread.°{Or the Bread of the Presence} This was called the Holy Place.
009:003 Behind the second curtain was a room called the Most Holy Place,°{Or the Holy of Holies}
009:004 containing the golden altar of incense and the gold-covered ark of the covenant. Inside the ark were the gold jar of manna, Aaron’s staff that had budded, and the stone tablets of the covenant.
009:005 Above the ark were the cherubim of glory, overshadowing the mercy seat.°{Or atonement cover} But we cannot discuss these things in detail now.
009:006 ¶ When everything had been prepared in this way, the priests entered regularly into the first room to perform their sacred duties.
009:007 But only the high priest entered the second room, and then only once a year, and never without blood, which he offered for himself and for the sins the people had committed in ignorance.
009:008 ¶ By this arrangement the Holy Spirit was showing that the way into the Most Holy Place°{Or the Holy Place; also in verses 12 and 25} had not yet been disclosed as long as the first tabernacle was still standing.
009:009 It is an illustration for the present time, because the gifts and sacrifices being offered were unable to cleanse the conscience of the worshiper.
009:010 They consist only in food and drink and special washings and°{CT does not include and.} external regulations imposed until the time of reform.
009:011 [[Redemption through His Blood]] ¶ But when Christ came as high priest of the good things to come,°{NA, SBL, and WH that have come} He went through the greater and more perfect tabernacle that is not made by hands and is not a part of this creation.
009:012 He did not enter by the blood of goats and calves, but He entered the Most Holy Place once for all by His own blood, thus securing eternal redemption.
009:013 ¶ For if the blood of bulls and goats°{CT of goats and bulls} and the ashes of a heifer sprinkled on those who are ceremonially unclean sanctify them so that their bodies are clean,
009:014 how much more will the blood of Christ, who through the eternal Spirit°{F35 the Holy Spirit} offered Himself unblemished to God, purify your°{NA, SBL, and WH our} consciences from works of death, so that we may serve the living God!
009:015 ¶ Therefore Christ is the mediator of a new covenant, so that those who are called may receive the promised eternal inheritance, now that He has died to redeem them from the transgressions committed under the first covenant.
009:016 ¶ In the case of a will,°{Greek diathēkē is also translated as covenant throughout this chapter.} it is necessary to establish the death of the one who made it,
009:017 because a will does not take effect until the one who made it has died; it cannot be executed while he is still alive.
009:018 ¶ That is why even the first covenant was not put into effect without blood.
009:019 For when Moses had proclaimed every commandment of the law to all the people, he took the blood of calves and goats,°{SBL does not include and goats.} along with water, scarlet wool, and hyssop, and sprinkled the scroll and all the people,
009:020 saying, “This is the blood of the covenant, which God has commanded you to keep.”°{Exodus 24:8}
009:021 ¶ In the same way, he sprinkled with blood the tabernacle and all the vessels used in worship.
009:022 According to the law, in fact, nearly everything must be purified with blood, and without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness.
009:023 ¶ So it was necessary for the copies of the heavenly things to be purified with these sacrifices, but the heavenly things themselves with better sacrifices than these.
009:024 For Christ did not enter a man-made copy of the true sanctuary, but He entered heaven itself, now to appear on our behalf in the presence of God.
009:025 ¶ Nor did He enter heaven to offer Himself again and again, as the high priest enters the Most Holy Place every year with blood that is not his own.
009:026 Otherwise, Christ would have had to suffer repeatedly since the foundation of the world. But now He has appeared once for all at the end of the ages to do away with sin by the sacrifice of Himself.
009:027 ¶ Just as man is appointed to die once, and after that to face judgment,
009:028 so also Christ was offered once to bear the sins of many; and He will appear a second time, not to bear sin, but to bring salvation to those who eagerly await Him.
010:001 [[Christ’s Perfect Sacrifice°{Psalm 40:1–17}]] ¶ For the law is only a shadow of the good things to come, not the realities themselves. The same sacrifices offered year after year can never°{GOC, NA, F35, SBL, and TR It can never, by the same sacrifices offered year after year,} make perfect those who draw near to worship.
