001:004 because we have heard about your faith in Christ Jesus and your love for all the saints—

001:006 that has come to you. ¶ All over the world this gospel is bearing fruit and growing,°{ALT and TR do not include and growing.} just as it has been doing among you since the day you heard it and truly understood the grace of God.
001:007 You also learned it°{CT You learned it} from Epaphras, our beloved fellow servant, who is a faithful minister of Christ on your°{SBL, TH, and WH our} behalf,
001:008 and who also informed us of your love in the Spirit.
001:009 ¶ For this reason, since the day we heard about you, we have not stopped praying for you and asking God to fill you with the knowledge of His will in all spiritual wisdom and understanding,
001:010 so that you may walk in a manner worthy of the Lord and may please Him in every way: bearing fruit in every good work, growing in the knowledge of God,
001:011 being strengthened with all power according to His glorious might so that you may have full endurance and patience, and joyfully
001:012 giving thanks to the Father,°{GOC our God and Father} who has qualified us°{CT you} to share in the inheritance of the saints in the light.
001:013 ¶ He has rescued us from the dominion of darkness and brought us into the kingdom of His beloved Son,
001:014 in whom we have redemption,°{TR, ALT, and F35 include through His blood.} the forgiveness of sins.
001:015 [[The Supremacy of the Son°{Hebrews 1:1–14}]] ¶ The Son is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn over all creation.
001:016 For in Him all things were created, things in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or dominions or rulers or authorities. All things were created through Him and for Him.
001:017 ¶ He is before all things, and in Him all things hold together.
001:018 And He is the head of the body, the church; He is the beginning and firstborn from among the dead, so that in all things He may have preeminence.
001:019 For God was pleased to have all His fullness dwell in Him,
001:020 and through Him to reconcile to Himself all things, whether things on earth or things in heaven, by making peace through the blood of His cross.
001:021 ¶ Once you were alienated from God and were hostile in your minds, engaging in evil deeds.
001:022 But now He has reconciled you°{SBL you have been reconciled} by Christ’s physical body through death to present you holy, unblemished, and blameless in His presence—
001:023 if°{CT if indeed} you continue in your faith, established and firm, not moved from the hope of the gospel you heard, which has been proclaimed to every creature°{Or in all creation} under heaven, and of which I, Paul, have become a servant.
001:024 [[Paul’s Suffering for the Church°{2 Corinthians 11:16–33}]] ¶ Now I rejoice in my sufferings for you, and I fill up in my flesh what is lacking in regard to Christ’s afflictions for the sake of His body, which is the church.
001:025 I became its servant by the commission God gave me to fully proclaim to you the word of God,
001:026 the mystery that was hidden for ages and generations but is now revealed to His saints.
001:027 To them God has chosen to make known among the Gentiles the glorious riches of this mystery, which is Christ in you, the hope of glory.
001:028 ¶ We proclaim Him, admonishing and teaching everyone with all wisdom, so that we may present everyone perfect°{Or fully mature} in Christ Jesus.°{CT and F35 in Christ}
001:029 To this end I also labor, striving with all His energy working powerfully within me.
002:001 [[Absent in Body, Present in Spirit°{Revelation 3:14–22}]] ¶ For I want you to know how much I am struggling for you and for those at Laodicea, and for all who have not met me face to face,
002:002 that they may be encouraged in heart, knit together in love, and filled with the full riches of complete understanding, so that they may know the mystery of God, both of the Father and of Christ,°{NA, SBL, and WH the mystery of God, namely Christ,}
002:003 in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge.
002:004 ¶ I say this so that no one will deceive you by smooth rhetoric.
002:005 For although I am absent from you in body, I am present with you in spirit, and I delight to see your orderly condition and firm faith in Christ.
002:006 [[Alive with Christ°{Ephesians 2:1–10}]] ¶ Therefore, just as you have received Christ Jesus as Lord, continue to walk in Him,
002:007 rooted and built up in Him, established in the faith as you were taught, and overflowing in it°{NA, SBL, and TH do not include in it.} with thankfulness.
002:008 ¶ See to it that no one takes you captive through philosophy and empty deception, which are based on human tradition and the spiritual forces of the world rather than on Christ.
002:009 For in Christ all the fullness of the Deity dwells in bodily form.
002:010 And you have been made complete in Christ, who is the head over every ruler and authority.
002:011 ¶ In Him you were also circumcised, in the putting off of your sinful nature, with the circumcision performed by Christ°{Literally in the cutting away of the body of sins of the flesh, by the circumcision of Christ} and not by human hands.
002:012 And having been buried with Him in baptism, you were raised with Him through your faith in the power of God, who raised Him from the dead.
002:013 ¶ When you were dead in your trespasses and in the uncircumcision of your sinful nature, God made you alive with Christ. He forgave us all our trespasses,
002:014 having canceled the debt ascribed to us in the decrees that stood against us. He took it away, nailing it to the cross!
002:015 And having disarmed the powers and authorities, He made a public spectacle of them, triumphing over them by the cross.
002:016 ¶ Therefore let no one judge you by what you eat or drink, or with regard to a feast, a New Moon, or a Sabbath.
002:017 These are a shadow of the things to come, but the body that casts it belongs to Christ.°{Literally but the body is of the Christ}
002:018 Do not let anyone who delights in false humility and the worship of angels disqualify you with speculation about what he has not seen.°{NA, SBL, WH, and TH what he has seen} Such a person is puffed up without basis by his unspiritual mind.
002:019 He has lost connection to the head, from whom the whole body, supported and knit together by its joints and ligaments, grows as God causes it to grow.
002:020 ¶ If°{GOC, F35, and TR If then} you have died with Christ to the spiritual forces of the world, why, as though you still belonged to the world, do you submit to its regulations:
002:021 “Do not handle, do not taste, do not touch!”?
002:022 These will all perish with use, because they are based on human commands and teachings.
002:023 Such restrictions indeed have an appearance of wisdom, with their self-prescribed worship, their false humility, and their harsh treatment of the body; but they are of no value against the indulgence of the flesh.
003:001 [[Put On the New Self°{Ephesians 4:17–32}]] ¶ Therefore, since you have been raised with Christ, strive for the things above, where Christ is seated at the right hand of God.
003:002 Set your minds on things above, not on earthly things.
003:003 For you died, and your life is now hidden with Christ in God.
003:004 When Christ, who is our°{NA, SBL, and TH your} life, appears, then you also will appear with Him in glory.
003:005 ¶ Put to death, therefore, the components of your earthly nature: sexual immorality, impurity, lust, evil desires, and greed, which is idolatry.
003:006 Because of these, the wrath of God is coming on the sons of disobedience.°{NE, WH, and Tischendorf do not include on the sons of disobedience.}
003:007 When you lived among them, you also used to walk in these ways.
003:008 But now you must put aside all such things as these: anger, rage, malice, slander, and filthy language from your lips.
003:009 ¶ Do not lie to one another, since you have taken off the old self with its practices,
003:010 and have put on the new self, which is being renewed in knowledge in the image of its Creator.
003:011 Here there is no Greek or Jew, circumcised or uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian, slave, or free, but Christ is all and is in all.
003:012 ¶ Therefore, as the elect of God, holy and beloved, clothe yourselves with hearts of compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience.
003:013 Bear with one another and forgive any complaint you may have against someone else. Forgive as Christ°{CT the Lord} forgave you.
003:014 And over all these virtues put on love, which is the bond of perfect unity.
003:015 Let the peace of God°{CT of Christ} rule in your hearts, for to this you were called as members of one body. And be thankful.
003:016 ¶ Let the word of Christ richly dwell within you as you teach and admonish one another with all wisdom, and as you sing psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs with gratitude in your hearts to the Lord.°{CT to God}
003:017 And whatever you do, in word or deed, do it all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the Father°{Literally to the God and Father} through Him.

003:020 ¶ Children, obey your parents in everything, for this is pleasing to the Lord.
