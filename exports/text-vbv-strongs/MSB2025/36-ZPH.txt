001:015 That day will be␤a day of wrath,␤a day of trouble and distress,␤a day of destruction and desolation,␤a day of darkness and gloom,␤a day of clouds and blackness,␤
001:016 a day of horn blast and battle cry␤against the fortified cities,␤and against the high corner towers.␤
001:017 I will bring such distress on mankind␤that they will walk like the blind,␤because they have sinned against the LORD.␤Their blood will be poured out like dust␤and their flesh like dung.␤
