001:001 [[Paul Greets the Saints in Rome]] ¶ Paul, a servant of Jesus Christ,°{CT of Christ Jesus} called to be an apostle, and set apart for the gospel of God—
001:002 the gospel He promised beforehand through His prophets in the Holy Scriptures,
001:003 regarding His Son, who was a descendant of David according to the flesh,
001:004 and who through the Spirit of holiness was declared with power to be the Son of God by His resurrection from the dead: Jesus Christ our Lord.
001:005 ¶ Through Him and on behalf of His name, we received grace and apostleship to call all those among the Gentiles to the obedience that comes from faith.
001:006 And you also are among those who are called to belong to Jesus Christ.
001:007 ¶ To all in Rome who are loved by God and called to be saints: ¶ Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
001:008 [[Unashamed of the Gospel]] ¶ First, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you, because your faith is being proclaimed all over the world.
001:009 God, whom I serve with my spirit°{Or in my spirit} in preaching the gospel of His Son, is my witness how constantly I remember you
001:010 in my prayers at all times, asking that now at last by God’s will I may succeed in coming to you.
001:011 For I long to see you so that I may impart to you some spiritual gift to strengthen you,
001:012 that is, that you and I may be mutually encouraged by each other’s faith.
001:013 ¶ I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, how often I planned to come to you (but have been prevented from visiting°{Literally but have been prevented} until now), in order that I might have a harvest among you, just as I have had among the other Gentiles.
001:014 I am obligated both to Greeks and non-Greeks,°{Literally to Greeks and barbarians} both to the wise and the foolish.
001:015 That is why I am so eager to preach the gospel also to you who are in Rome.
001:016 ¶ I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ,°{CT does not include of Christ.} because it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes, first to the Jew, then to the Greek.
001:017 For the gospel reveals the righteousness of God that comes by faith from start to finish,°{Literally For in it is revealed the righteousness of God from faith to faith} just as it is written: “The righteous will live by faith.”°{Habakkuk 2:4}
001:018 [[God’s Wrath against Sin]] ¶ The wrath of God is being revealed from heaven against all the godlessness and wickedness of men who suppress the truth by their wickedness.
001:019 For what may be known about God is plain to them, because God has made it plain to them.
001:020 For since the creation of the world God’s invisible qualities, His eternal power and divine nature, have been clearly seen, being understood from His workmanship, so that men are without excuse.
001:021 ¶ For although they knew God, they neither glorified Him as God nor gave thanks to Him, but they became futile in their thinking and darkened in their foolish hearts.
001:022 Although they claimed to be wise, they became fools,
001:023 and exchanged°{F35 exchanged for themselves} the glory of the immortal God for images of mortal man and birds and animals and reptiles.
001:024 ¶ Therefore God gave them over in the desires of their hearts to impurity for the dishonoring of their bodies with one another.
001:025 They exchanged the truth of God for a lie, and worshiped and served the creature rather than the Creator, who is forever worthy of praise!°{Or forever blessed} Amen.
001:026 ¶ For this reason God gave them over to dishonorable passions. Even their women exchanged natural relations for unnatural ones.
001:027 Likewise, the men abandoned natural relations with women and burned with lust for one another. Men committed indecent acts with other men, and received in themselves the due penalty for their error.
001:028 ¶ Furthermore, since they did not see fit to acknowledge God, He gave them up to a depraved mind, to do what ought not to be done.
001:029 They have become filled with every kind of wickedness, sexual immorality,°{CT does not include sexual immorality.} evil, greed, and depravity. They are full of envy, murder, strife, deceit, and malice. They are gossips,
001:030 slanderers, God-haters, insolent, arrogant, and boastful. They invent new forms of evil; they disobey their parents.
001:031 They are senseless, faithless, heartless, unforgiving,°{CT does not include unforgiving.} merciless.
001:032 ¶ Although they know God’s righteous decree that those who do such things are worthy of death, they not only continue to do these things, but also approve of those who practice them.
002:001 [[God’s Righteous Judgment°{Psalm 75:1–10}]] ¶ You, therefore, have no excuse, you who pass judgment on another. For on whatever grounds you judge the other, you are condemning yourself, because you who pass judgment do the same things.
002:002 And we know that God’s judgment against those who do such things is based on truth.
002:003 So when you, O man, pass judgment on others, yet do the same things, do you think you will escape God’s judgment?
002:004 Or do you disregard the riches of His kindness, tolerance, and patience, not realizing that God’s kindness leads you to repentance?
002:005 ¶ But because of your hard and unrepentant heart, you are storing up wrath against yourself for the day of wrath, revelation, and God’s righteous judgment.°{CT and TR for the day of wrath, when God’s righteous judgment will be revealed}
002:006 God “will repay each one according to his deeds.”°{Psalm 62:12}
002:007 To those who by perseverance in doing good seek glory, honor, and immortality, He will give eternal life.
002:008 But for those who are self-seeking and who reject the truth and follow wickedness, there will be anger and wrath.°{CT wrath and anger}
002:009 ¶ There will be trouble and distress for every human being who does evil, first for the Jew, then for the Greek;
002:010 but glory, honor, and peace for everyone who does good, first for the Jew, then for the Greek.
002:011 For God does not show favoritism.
002:012 ¶ All who sin apart from the law will also perish apart from the law, and all who sin under the law will be judged by the law.
002:013 For it is not the hearers of the law who are righteous before God, but it is the doers of the law who will be declared righteous.
002:014 ¶ Indeed, when Gentiles, who do not have the law, do by nature what the law requires, they are a law to themselves, even though they do not have the law.
002:015 So they show that the work of the law is written on their hearts, their consciences also bearing witness, and their thoughts either accusing or defending them
002:016 on the day when God will judge men’s secrets through Jesus Christ,°{NA, SBL, and WH through Christ Jesus} as proclaimed by my gospel.
002:017 [[The Jews and the Law]] ¶ Behold, you call yourself a Jew. You°{CT Now you, if you call yourself a Jew; if you} rely on the law and boast in God.
002:018 And you know°{CT if you know} His will and approve of what is superior because you are instructed by the law.
002:019 And you are convinced°{CT if you are convinced} that you are a guide for the blind, a light for those in darkness,
002:020 an instructor of the foolish, a teacher of infants, because you have in the law the embodiment of knowledge and truth.
002:021 You, then, who teach others, do you not teach yourself? You who preach against stealing, do you steal?
002:022 You who forbid adultery, do you commit adultery? You who abhor idols, do you rob temples?
002:023 You who boast in the law, do you dishonor God by breaking the law?
002:024 As it is written: “God’s name is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you.”°{Isaiah 52:5 (see also LXX)}
002:025 ¶ Circumcision has value if you observe the law, but if you break the law, your circumcision has become uncircumcision.
002:026 If a man who is not circumcised keeps the requirements of the law, will not his uncircumcision be regarded as circumcision?
002:027 The one who is physically uncircumcised yet keeps the law will condemn you who, even though you have the written code and circumcision, are a lawbreaker.
002:028 ¶ A man is not a Jew because he is one outwardly, nor is circumcision only outward and physical.
002:029 No, a man is a Jew because he is one inwardly, and circumcision is a matter of the heart, by the Spirit, not by the written code. Such a man’s praise does not come from men, but from God.
003:001 [[God Remains Faithful]] ¶ What, then, is the advantage of being a Jew? Or what is the value of circumcision?
003:002 Much in every way. First of all, they have been entrusted with the very words°{Or the oracles} of God.
003:003 ¶ What if some did not have faith? Will their lack of faith nullify God’s faithfulness?
003:004 Certainly not! Let God be true and every man a liar. As it is written: ¶ “So that You may be proved right when You speak␤and victorious when You judge.”°{Or when You are judged; Psalm 51:4 (see also LXX)}
003:005 ¶ But if our unrighteousness highlights the righteousness of God, what shall we say? That God is unjust to inflict His wrath on us? I am speaking in human terms.
003:006 Certainly not! In that case, how could God judge the world?
003:007 For°{NA, SBL, and WH But} if my falsehood accentuates God’s truthfulness, to the increase of His glory, why am I still condemned as a sinner?
003:008 Why not say, as some slanderously claim that we say, “Let us do evil that good may result”? Their condemnation is deserved!
003:009 [[There Is No One Righteous°{Psalm 14:1–7; Psalm 53:1–6; Isaiah 59:1–17}]] ¶ What then? Are we any better? Not at all. For we have already made the charge that Jews and Greeks alike are all under sin.
003:010 As it is written: ¶ “There is no one righteous,␤not even one.␤
003:011 There is no one who understands,␤no one who seeks God.␤
003:012 All have turned away,␤they have together become worthless;␤there is no one who does good,␤not even one.”°{Psalm 14:1–3 and Psalm 53:1–3 (see also LXX)}␤
003:013 “Their throats are open graves;␤their tongues practice deceit.”°{Psalm 5:9}␤“The venom of vipers is on their lips.”°{Psalm 140:3}␤
003:014 “Their mouths are full␤of cursing and bitterness.”°{Psalm 10:7 (see also LXX)}␤
003:015 “Their feet are swift to shed blood;␤
003:016 ruin and misery lie in their wake,␤
003:017 and the way of peace they have not known.”°{Isaiah 59:7–8 (see also LXX)}␤
003:018 “There is no fear of God␤before their eyes.”°{Psalm 36:1}
003:019 ¶ Now we know that whatever the law says, it says to those who are under the law, so that every mouth may be silenced and the whole world held accountable to God.
003:020 Therefore no one will be justified in His sight by works of the law. For the law merely brings awareness of sin.
003:021 [[Righteousness through Faith in Christ°{Philippians 3:1–11}]] ¶ But now, apart from the law, the righteousness of God has been revealed, as attested by the Law and the Prophets.
003:022 And this righteousness from God comes through faith in Jesus Christ to all and upon all°{CT does not include and upon all.} who believe. There is no distinction,
003:023 for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God,
003:024 and are justified freely by His grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.
003:025 ¶ God presented Him as an atoning sacrifice°{Or as a propitiation} in His blood through faith, in order to demonstrate His righteousness, because in His forbearance He had passed over the sins committed beforehand.
003:026 He did this to demonstrate His righteousness at the present time, so as to be just and to justify the one who has faith in Jesus.
003:027 ¶ Where, then, is boasting? It is excluded. On what principle? On that of works? No, but on that of faith.
003:028 Therefore°{CT For} we maintain that a man is justified by faith apart from works of the law.
003:029 ¶ Is God the God of Jews only? Is He not the God of Gentiles too? Yes, of Gentiles too,
003:030 since there is only one God, who will justify the circumcised by faith and the uncircumcised through that same faith.
003:031 ¶ Do we, then, nullify the law by this faith? Certainly not! Instead, we uphold the law.
004:001 [[Abraham Justified by Faith°{Genesis 15:1–7; Psalm 32:1–11; Hebrews 11:8–19}]] ¶ What then shall we say that Abraham, our forefather according to the flesh, has discovered?
004:002 If Abraham was indeed justified by works, he had something to boast about, but not before God.
004:003 For what does the Scripture say? “Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness.”°{Genesis 15:6}
004:004 ¶ Now the wages of the worker are not credited as a gift, but as an obligation.
004:005 However, to the one who does not work, but believes in Him who justifies the ungodly, his faith is credited as righteousness.
004:006 And David speaks likewise of the blessedness of the man to whom God credits righteousness apart from works:
004:007 ¶ “Blessed are they whose lawless acts are forgiven,␤whose sins are covered.␤
004:008 Blessed is the man␤whose sin the Lord will never count against him.”°{Psalm 32:1–2 (see also LXX)}
004:009 ¶ Is this blessing only on the circumcised, or also on the uncircumcised? We have been saying that Abraham’s faith was credited to him as righteousness.
004:010 In what context was it credited? Was it after his circumcision, or before? It was not after, but before.°{Literally How then was it reckoned—being in circumcision, or in uncircumcision? Not in circumcision, but in uncircumcision.}
004:011 ¶ And he received the sign of circumcision as a seal of the righteousness that he had by faith while he was still uncircumcised. So then, he is the father of all who believe but are not circumcised, in order that righteousness might be credited to them as well.°{SBL, TH, and WH do not include as well.}
004:012 And he is also the father of the circumcised who not only are circumcised, but who also walk in the footsteps of the faith that our father Abraham had before he was circumcised.
004:013 [[Abraham Receives the Promise°{Genesis 15:8–21}]] ¶ For the promise to Abraham and his offspring that he would be heir of the world was not given through the law, but through the righteousness that comes by faith.
004:014 For if those who live by the law are heirs, faith is useless and the promise is worthless,
004:015 because the law brings wrath. For°{CT And} where there is no law, there is no transgression.
004:016 ¶ Therefore, the promise comes by faith, so that it may rest on grace and may be guaranteed to all Abraham’s offspring—not only to those who are of the law, but also to those who are of the faith of Abraham. He is the father of us all.
004:017 As it is written: “I have made you a father of many nations.”°{Genesis 17:5} He is our father in the presence of God, in whom he believed, the God who gives life to the dead and calls into being what does not yet exist.
004:018 ¶ Against all hope, Abraham in hope believed and so became the father of many nations, just as he had been told, “So shall your offspring be.”°{Genesis 15:5}
004:019 Without weakening in his faith, he acknowledged neither°{CT does not include neither.} the decrepitness of his body (since he was about a hundred years old) nor°{CT and} the lifelessness of Sarah’s womb.
004:020 Yet he did not waver through disbelief in the promise of God, but was strengthened in his faith and gave glory to God,
004:021 being fully persuaded that God was able to do what He had promised.
004:022 This is why “it was credited to him as righteousness.”°{Genesis 15:6}
004:023 ¶ Now the words “it was credited to him” were written not only for Abraham,
004:024 but also for us, to whom righteousness will be credited—for us who believe in Him who raised Jesus our Lord from the dead.
004:025 He was delivered over to death for our trespasses and was raised to life for our justification.
005:001 [[The Triumph of Faith]] ¶ Therefore, since we have been justified through faith, we have°{Or let us have} peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ,
005:002 through whom we have gained access by faith into this grace in which we stand. And we rejoice°{Or exult; also in verses 3 and 11} in the hope of the glory of God.
005:003 ¶ Not only that, but we also rejoice in our sufferings, because we know that suffering produces perseverance;
005:004 perseverance, character; and character, hope.
005:005 And hope does not disappoint us, because God has poured out His love into our hearts through the Holy Spirit, whom He has given us.
005:006 [[Christ’s Sacrifice for the Ungodly°{John 3:1–21}]] ¶ For at the right time,°{CT just the right time} while we were still powerless, Christ died for the ungodly.
005:007 Very rarely will anyone die for a righteous man, though for a good man someone might possibly dare to die.
005:008 But God proves His love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us.
005:009 ¶ Therefore, since we have now been justified by His blood, how much more shall we be saved from wrath through Him!
005:010 For if, when we were enemies of God, we were reconciled to Him through the death of His Son, how much more, having been reconciled, shall we be saved through His life!
005:011 Not only that, but we also rejoice in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now received reconciliation.
005:012 [[Death in Adam, Life in Christ°{Genesis 3:1–7}]] ¶ Therefore, just as sin entered the world through one man, and death through sin, so also death was passed on to all men, because all sinned.
005:013 For sin was in the world before the law was given; but sin is not taken into account when there is no law.
005:014 Nevertheless, death reigned from Adam until Moses, even over those who did not sin in the way that Adam transgressed. He is a pattern of the One to come.
005:015 ¶ But the gift is not like the trespass. For if the many died by the trespass of the one man, how much more did God’s grace and the gift that came by the grace of the one man, Jesus Christ, abound to the many!
005:016 Again, the gift is not like the result of the one man’s sin: The judgment that followed one sin brought condemnation, but the gift that followed many trespasses brought justification.
005:017 For if, by the trespass of the one man, death reigned through that one man, how much more will those who receive the abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness reign in life through the one man, Jesus Christ!
005:018 ¶ So then, just as one trespass brought condemnation for all men, so also one act of righteousness brought justification and life for all men.
005:019 For just as through the disobedience of the one man the many were made sinners, so also through the obedience of the one man the many will be made righteous.
005:020 ¶ The law came in so that the trespass would increase; but where sin increased, grace increased all the more,
005:021 so that, just as sin reigned in death, so also grace might reign through righteousness to bring eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.
006:001 [[Dead to Sin, Alive to God°{2 Corinthians 4:7–18}]] ¶ What then shall we say? Shall we continue in sin so that grace may increase?
006:002 Certainly not! How can we who died to sin live in it any longer?
006:003 Or aren’t you aware that all of us who were baptized into Christ Jesus were baptized into His death?
006:004 We were therefore buried with Him through baptism into death, in order that, just as Christ was raised from the dead through the glory of the Father, we too may walk in newness of life.
006:005 ¶ For if we have been united with Him like this in His death, we will certainly also be united with Him in His resurrection.
006:006 We know that our old self was crucified with Him so that the body of sin might be rendered powerless, that we should no longer be slaves to sin.
006:007 For anyone who has died has been freed from sin.
006:008 ¶ Now if we died with Christ, we believe that we will also live with Him.
006:009 For we know that since Christ was raised from the dead, He cannot die again; death no longer has dominion over Him.
006:010 The death He died, He died to sin once for all; but the life He lives, He lives to God.
006:011 So you too must count yourselves dead to sin, but alive to God in Christ Jesus our Lord.°{CT does not include our Lord.}
006:012 ¶ Therefore do not let sin reign in your mortal body so that you obey it in its desires.°{CT so that you obey its desires}
006:013 Do not present the parts of your body to sin as instruments of wickedness, but present yourselves to God as those who have been brought from death to life; and present the parts of your body to Him as instruments of righteousness.
006:014 For sin shall not be your master, because you are not under law, but under grace.
006:015 [[The Wages of Sin]] ¶ What then? Shall we sin because we are not under law, but under grace? Certainly not!
006:016 Do you not know that when you offer yourselves as obedient slaves, you are slaves to the one you obey, whether you are slaves to sin leading to death, or to obedience leading to righteousness?
006:017 But thanks be to God that, though you once were slaves to sin, you wholeheartedly obeyed the form of teaching to which you were committed.
006:018 You have been set free from sin and have become slaves to righteousness.
006:019 ¶ I am speaking in human terms because of the weakness of your flesh. Just as you used to offer the parts of your body in slavery to impurity and to escalating wickedness, so now offer them in slavery to righteousness leading to holiness.
006:020 ¶ For when you were slaves to sin, you were free of obligation to righteousness.
006:021 What fruit did you reap at that time from the things of which you are now ashamed? The outcome of those things is death.
006:022 But now that you have been set free from sin and have become slaves to God, the fruit you reap leads to holiness, and the outcome is eternal life.
006:023 For the wages of sin is death,°{See Proverbs 10:16.} but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.
007:001 [[Release from the Law°{Galatians 3:15–25}]] ¶ Do you not know, brothers (for I am speaking to those who know the law), that the law has authority over a man only as long as he lives?
007:002 For instance, a married woman is bound by law to her husband as long as he lives. But if her husband dies, she is released from the law of marriage.
007:003 So then, if she is joined to another man while her husband is still alive, she is called an adulteress; but if her husband dies, she is free from that law and is not an adulteress if she marries another man.
007:004 ¶ Therefore, my brothers, you also died to the law through the body of Christ, that you might belong to another, to Him who was raised from the dead, in order that we might bear fruit to God.
007:005 For when we lived according to the flesh, the sinful passions aroused by the law were at work in our bodies, bearing fruit for death.
007:006 But now, having died to what bound us, we have been released from the law, so that we serve in the new way of the Spirit, and not in the old way of the written code.
007:007 [[God’s Law Is Holy]] ¶ What then shall we say? Is the law sin? Certainly not! Indeed, I would not have been mindful of sin if not for the law. For I would not have been aware of coveting if the law had not said, “Do not covet.”°{Exodus 20:17; Deuteronomy 5:21}
007:008 But sin, seizing its opportunity through the commandment, produced in me every kind of covetous desire. For apart from the law, sin is dead.
007:009 ¶ Once I was alive apart from the law; but when the commandment came, sin sprang to life and I died.
007:010 So I discovered that the very commandment that was meant to bring life actually brought death.
007:011 For sin, seizing its opportunity through the commandment, deceived me and through the commandment put me to death.
007:012 ¶ So then, the law is holy, and the commandment is holy, righteous, and good.
007:013 [[Struggling with Sin]] ¶ Has that which°{CT Did that which} is good, then, become death to me? Certainly not! But in order that sin might be exposed as sin, it produced death in me through what was good, so that through the commandment sin might become utterly sinful.
007:014 ¶ We know that the law is spiritual; but I am unspiritual, sold as a slave to sin.
007:015 I do not understand what I do. For what I want to do, I do not do. But what I hate, I do.
007:016 And if I do what I do not want to do, I admit that the law is good.
007:017 In that case, it is no longer I who do it, but it is sin living in me that does it.
007:018 ¶ I know that nothing good lives in me, that is, in my flesh; for I have the desire to do what is good, but I cannot discover how to carry it out.°{CT I cannot carry it out}
007:019 For I do not do the good I want to do. Instead, I keep on doing the evil I do not want to do.
007:020 And if I do what I do not want, it is no longer I who do it, but it is sin living in me that does it.
007:021 ¶ So this is the principle I have discovered: When I want to do good, evil is right there with me.
007:022 For in my inner being I delight in God’s law.
007:023 But I see another law at work in my body, warring against the law of my mind and holding me captive to the law of sin that dwells within me.°{Literally captive to the law of sin being in my members.}
007:024 ¶ What a wretched man I am! Who will rescue me from this body of death?
007:025 I thank God°{CT Thanks be to God} through Jesus Christ our Lord! ¶ So then, with my mind I serve the law of God, but with my flesh I serve the law of sin.
008:001 [[Walking by the Spirit°{Ezekiel 36:16–38; Galatians 5:16–26}]] ¶ Therefore there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus, who do not walk according to the flesh but according to the Spirit.°{CT does not include who do not walk according to the flesh but according to the Spirit.}
008:002 For in Christ Jesus the law of the Spirit of life set me°{CT you} free from the law of sin and death.
008:003 For what the law was powerless to do in that it was weakened by the flesh, God did by sending His own Son in the likeness of sinful man, as an offering for sin.°{Literally in the likeness of sinful flesh and for sin} He thus condemned sin in the flesh,
008:004 so that the righteous standard of the law might be fulfilled in us, who do not walk according to the flesh but according to the Spirit.
008:005 ¶ Those who live according to the flesh set their minds on the things of the flesh; but those who live according to the Spirit set their minds on the things of the Spirit.
008:006 The mind of the flesh is death, but the mind of the Spirit is life and peace,

008:008 Those controlled by the flesh°{Literally Those being in the flesh; similarly in verse 9} cannot please God.
008:009 ¶ You, however, are controlled not by the flesh, but by the Spirit, if the Spirit of God lives in you. And if anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ, he does not belong to Christ.
008:010 But if Christ is in you, your body is dead because of sin, yet your spirit is alive°{Or yet the Spirit is life} because of righteousness.
008:011 And if the Spirit of Him who raised Jesus from the dead is living in you, He who raised Christ°{SBL and WH Christ Jesus} from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through His Spirit, who lives in you.
008:012 [[Heirs with Christ]] ¶ Therefore, brothers, we have an obligation, but it is not to the flesh, to live according to it.
008:013 For if you live according to the flesh, you will die; but if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live.

008:016 The Spirit Himself testifies with our spirit that we are God’s children.
008:017 And if we are children, then we are heirs: heirs of God and co-heirs with Christ—if indeed we suffer with Him, so that we may also be glorified with Him.
008:018 [[Future Glory°{2 Corinthians 5:1–10}]] ¶ I consider that our present sufferings are not comparable to the glory that will be revealed in us.
008:019 The creation waits in eager expectation for the revelation of the sons of God.
008:020 For the creation was subjected to futility, not by its own will, but because of the One who subjected it, in hope
008:021 that the creation itself will be set free from its bondage to decay and brought into the glorious freedom of the children of God.
008:022 ¶ We know that the whole creation has been groaning together in the pains of childbirth until the present time.
008:023 Not only that, but we ourselves, who have the firstfruits of the Spirit, groan inwardly as we wait eagerly for our adoption as sons, the redemption of our bodies.
008:024 For in this hope we were saved; but hope that is seen is no hope at all. Why would one hope for°{CT Who hopes for} what he can already see?
008:025 But if we hope for what we do not yet see, we wait for it patiently.
008:026 ¶ In the same way, the Spirit helps us in our weaknesses.°{CT weakness} For we do not know how we ought to pray, but the Spirit Himself intercedes for us°{CT literal does not include for us.} with groans too deep for words.
008:027 And He who searches our hearts knows the mind of the Spirit, because the Spirit intercedes for the saints according to the will of God.
008:028 [[God Works in All Things°{Ephesians 1:3–14}]] ¶ And we know that all things work together°{CT that God works all things together} for the good of those who love Him, who are called according to His purpose.
008:029 For those God foreknew, He also predestined to be conformed to the image of His Son, so that He would be the firstborn among many brothers.
008:030 And those He predestined, He also called; those He called, He also justified; those He justified, He also glorified.
008:031 ¶ What then shall we say in response to these things? If God is for us, who can be against us?
008:032 He who did not spare His own Son but gave Him up for us all, how will He not also, along with Him, freely give us all things?
