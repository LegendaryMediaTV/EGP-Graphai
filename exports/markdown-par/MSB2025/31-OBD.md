## Chapter 1

### The Destruction of Edom<sup>a</sup>

<sup>1</sup> This is the vision of Obadiah:

This is what the Lord GOD says about Edom—

We have heard a message from the LORD;<br>an envoy has been sent among the nations<br>to say, “Rise up,<br>and let us go to battle against her!”—

<sup>2</sup> “Behold, I will make you small among the nations;<br>you will be deeply despised.<br>
<sup>3</sup> The pride of your heart has deceived you,<br>O dwellers in the clefts of the rocks<sup>b</sup><br>whose habitation is the heights,<br>who say in your heart,<br>‘Who can bring me down to the ground?’<br>
<sup>4</sup> Though you soar like the eagle<br>and make your nest among the stars,<br>even from there I will bring you down,” declares the LORD.<br>
<sup>5</sup> “If thieves came to you,<br>if robbers by night—<br>oh, how you will be ruined—<br>would they not steal only what they wanted?<br>If grape gatherers came to you,<br>would they not leave some gleanings?<br>
<sup>6</sup> But how Esau will be pillaged,<br>his hidden treasures sought out!<br>
<sup>7</sup> All the men allied with you<br>will drive you to the border;<br>the men at peace with you<br>will deceive and overpower you.<br>Those who eat your bread<sup>c</sup><br>will set a trap for you<br>without your awareness of it.<br>
<sup>8</sup> In that day, declares the LORD,<br>will I not destroy the wise men of Edom<br>and the men of understanding<br>in the mountains of Esau?<br>
<sup>9</sup> Then your mighty men, O Teman,<br>will be terrified,<br>so that everyone in the mountains of Esau<br>will be cut down in the slaughter.

<sup>10</sup> Because of the violence against your brother Jacob,<br>you will be covered with shame<br>and cut off forever.<br>
<sup>11</sup> On the day you stood aloof<br>while strangers carried off his wealth<br>and foreigners entered his gate<br>and cast lots for Jerusalem,<br>you were just like one of them.<br>
<sup>12</sup> But you should not gloat in that day,<br>your brother’s day of misfortune,<br>nor rejoice over the people of Judah<br>in the day of their destruction,<br>nor boast proudly<sup>d</sup><br>in the day of their distress.<br>
<sup>13</sup> You should not enter the gate of My people<br>in the day of their disaster,<br>nor gloat over their affliction<br>in the day of their disaster,<br>nor loot their wealth<br>in the day of their disaster.<br>
<sup>14</sup> Nor should you stand at the crossroads<br>to cut off their fugitives,<br>nor deliver up their survivors<br>in the day of their distress.

### The Deliverance of Israel

<sup>15</sup> For the Day of the LORD is near<br>for all the nations.<br>As you have done, it will be done to you;<br>your recompense will return upon your own head.<br>
<sup>16</sup> For as you drank on My holy mountain,<br>so all the nations will drink continually.<br>They will drink and gulp it down;<br>they will be as if they had never existed.<br>
<sup>17</sup> But on Mount Zion there will be deliverance,<br>and it will be holy,<br>and the house of Jacob<br>will reclaim their possession.<br>
<sup>18</sup> Then the house of Jacob will be a blazing fire,<br>and the house of Joseph a burning flame;<br>but the house of Esau will be stubble—<br>Jacob will set it ablaze and consume it.<br>Therefore no survivor will remain<br>from the house of Esau.” For the LORD has spoken.<br>
<sup>19</sup> Those from the Negev will possess the mountains of Esau;<br>those from the foothills<sup>e</sup> will possess the land of the Philistines.<br>They will occupy the fields of Ephraim and Samaria,<br>and Benjamin will possess Gilead.<br>
<sup>20</sup> And the exiles of this host of the Israelites<br>will possess the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath;<br>and the exiles from Jerusalem who are in Sepharad<br>will possess the cities of the Negev.<br>
<sup>21</sup> The deliverers will ascend<sup>f</sup> Mount Zion<br>to rule over the mountains of Esau.

And the kingdom will belong to the LORD.

> - <sup>a</sup> Heading. Jeremiah 49:7–22
> - <sup>b</sup> 3. Or _in the retreats of Sela_
> - <sup>c</sup> 7. Hebrew does not include _Those who eat_.
> - <sup>d</sup> 12. Hebrew _nor enlarge your mouth_
> - <sup>e</sup> 19. Hebrew _Shephelah_ or _lowlands_; that is, the western foothills of Judea
> - <sup>f</sup> 21. Or _The deliverers will go up from_; LXX _Those being delivered will go up_
