## Chapter 1

### Judgment to Come<sup>a</sup>

<sup>1</sup> This is the word of the LORD that came to Micah the Moreshite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah—what he saw regarding Samaria and Jerusalem:

<sup>2</sup> Hear, O peoples, all of you;<br>listen, O earth, and everyone in it!<br>May the Lord GOD bear witness against you,<br>the Lord from His holy temple.<br>
<sup>3</sup> For behold, the LORD comes forth<br>from His dwelling place;<br>He will come down and tread<br>on the high places of the earth.<br>
<sup>4</sup> The mountains will melt beneath Him,<br>and the valleys will split apart,<br>like wax before the fire,<br>like water rushing down a slope.

<sup>5</sup> All this is for the transgression of Jacob<br>and the sins of the house of Israel.<br>What is the transgression of Jacob?<br>Is it not Samaria?<br>And what is the high place of Judah?<br>Is it not Jerusalem?

<sup>6</sup> Therefore I will make Samaria<br>a heap of rubble in the open field,<br>a planting area for a vineyard.<br>I will pour her stones into the valley<br>and expose her foundations.<br>
<sup>7</sup> All her carved images will be smashed to pieces;<br>all her wages will be burned in the fire,<br>and I will destroy all her idols.<br>Since she collected the wages of a prostitute,<br>they will be used again on a prostitute.

### Weeping and Mourning

<sup>8</sup> Because of this I will lament and wail;<br>I will walk barefoot and naked.<br>I will howl like a jackal<sup>b</sup><br>and mourn like an ostrich.<sup>c</sup><br>
<sup>9</sup> For her wound is incurable;<br>it has reached even Judah;<br>it has approached the gate of my people,<br>as far as Jerusalem itself.

<sup>10</sup> Do not tell it in Gath;<sup>d</sup> do not weep at all.<br>Roll in the dust in Beth-leaphrah.<sup>e</sup><br>
<sup>11</sup> Depart in shameful nakedness,<br>O dwellers of Shaphir.<sup>f</sup><br>The dwellers of Zaanan<sup>g</sup><br>will not come out.<br>Beth-ezel<sup>h</sup> is in mourning;<br>its support is taken from you.<br>
<sup>12</sup> For the dwellers of Maroth<sup>i</sup> pined for good,<br>but calamity came down from the LORD,<br>even to the gate of Jerusalem.

<sup>13</sup> Harness your chariot horses,<br>O dweller of Lachish.<sup>j</sup><br>You were the beginning of sin to the Daughter of Zion,<br>for the transgressions of Israel were found in you.<br>
<sup>14</sup> Therefore, send farewell gifts<sup>k</sup> to Moresheth-gath;<sup>l</sup><br>the houses of Achzib<sup>m</sup> will prove deceptive<br>to the kings of Israel.<br>
<sup>15</sup> I will again bring a conqueror against you,<br>O dweller of Mareshah.<sup>n</sup><br>The glory of Israel will come to Adullam.<br>
<sup>16</sup> Shave yourselves bald and cut off your hair<br>in mourning for your precious children;<br>make yourselves as bald as an eagle,<br>for they will go from you into exile.

> - <sup>a</sup> Heading. Isaiah 7:17–25
> - <sup>b</sup> 8. Or _a serpent_ or _a dragon_
> - <sup>c</sup> 8. Literally _like daughters of an ostrich_ or _like daughters of an owl_
> - <sup>d</sup> 10. _Gath_ sounds like the Hebrew for _tell_.
> - <sup>e</sup> 10. _Beth-leaphrah_ means _house of dust_.
> - <sup>f</sup> 11. _Shaphir_ means _pleasant_.
> - <sup>g</sup> 11. _Zaanan_ sounds like the Hebrew for _come out_.
> - <sup>h</sup> 11. _Beth-ezel_ means _adjoining house_.
> - <sup>i</sup> 12. _Maroth_ sounds like the Hebrew for _bitter_.
> - <sup>j</sup> 13. _Lachish_ sounds like the Hebrew term for _team of horses_.
> - <sup>k</sup> 14. Or _give dowry_
> - <sup>l</sup> 14. _Moresheth_ sounds like the Hebrew for _gift_ or _dowry_.
> - <sup>m</sup> 14. _Achzib_ means _deception_.
> - <sup>n</sup> 15. _Mareshah_ sounds like the Hebrew for _conqueror_.

## Chapter 2

### Woe to Oppressors

<sup>1</sup> Woe to those who devise iniquity<br>and plot evil on their beds!<br>At morning’s light they accomplish it<br>because the power is in their hands.<br>
<sup>2</sup> They covet fields and seize them;<br>they take away houses.<br>They deprive a man of his home,<br>a fellow man of his inheritance.

<sup>3</sup> Therefore this is what the LORD says:

“I am planning against this nation a disaster<br>from which you cannot free your necks.<br>Then you will not walk so proudly,<br>for it will be a time of calamity.<br>
<sup>4</sup> In that day they will take up a proverb against you<br>and taunt you with this bitter lamentation:<br>‘We are utterly ruined!<br>He has changed the portion of my people.<br>How He has removed it from me!<br>He has allotted our fields to traitors.’”

<sup>5</sup> Therefore, you will have no one in the assembly of the LORD<br>to divide the land by lot.

### Reproof of False Prophets<sup>a</sup>

<sup>6</sup> “Do not preach,” they preach.<br>“Do not preach these things;<br>disgrace will not overtake us.”<br>
<sup>7</sup> Should it be said, O house of Jacob,<br>“Is the Spirit of the LORD impatient?<br>Are these the things He does?”

Do not My words bring good<br>to him who walks uprightly?<br>
<sup>8</sup> But of late My people have risen up<br>like an enemy:<br>You strip off the splendid robe<br>from unsuspecting passersby<br>like men returning from battle.<br>
<sup>9</sup> You drive the women of My people<br>from their pleasant homes.<br>You take away My blessing<br>from their children forever.

<sup>10</sup> Arise and depart,<br>for this is not your place of rest,<br>because its defilement brings destruction—<br>a grievous destruction!<br>
<sup>11</sup> If a man of wind were to come<br>and say falsely,<br>“I will preach to you of wine and strong drink,”<sup>b</sup><br>he would be just the preacher for this people!

### The Remnant of Israel<sup>c</sup>

<sup>12</sup> I will surely gather all of you, O Jacob;<br>I will collect the remnant of Israel.<br>I will bring them together like sheep in a pen,<br>like a flock in the midst of its pasture—<br>a noisy throng.<br>
<sup>13</sup> One who breaks open the way<br>will go up before them;<br>they will break through the gate,<br>and go out by it.<br>Their King will pass through before them,<br>the LORD as their leader.

> - <sup>a</sup> Heading. Ezekiel 13:1–16
> - <sup>b</sup> 11. Or _“I will prophesy to you for wine and strong drink”_
> - <sup>c</sup> Heading. Micah 5:7–15

## Chapter 3

### Rulers and Prophets Condemned

<sup>1</sup> Then I said:

“Hear now, O leaders of Jacob,<br>you rulers of the house of Israel.<br>Should you not know justice?<br>
<sup>2</sup> You hate good and love evil.<br>You tear the skin from my people<br>and strip the flesh from their bones.<br>
<sup>3</sup> You eat the flesh of my people<br>after stripping off their skin<br>and breaking their bones.<br>You chop them up like flesh for the cooking pot,<br>like meat in a cauldron.”

<sup>4</sup> Then they will cry out to the LORD,<br>but He will not answer them.<br>At that time He will hide His face from them<br>because of the evil they have done.

<sup>5</sup> This is what the LORD says:

“As for the prophets<br>who lead My people astray,<br>who proclaim peace<br>while they chew with their teeth,<br>but declare war against one<br>who puts nothing in their mouths:<br>
<sup>6</sup> Therefore night will come over you without visions,<br>and darkness without divination.<br>The sun will set on these prophets,<br>and the daylight will turn black over them.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Then the seers will be ashamed<br>and the diviners will be disgraced.<br>They will all cover their mouths<br>because there is no answer from God.”

<sup>8</sup> As for me, however, I am filled with power<br>by the Spirit of the LORD,<br>with justice and courage,<br>to declare to Jacob his transgression<br>and to Israel his sin.

<sup>9</sup> Now hear this, O leaders of the house of Jacob<br>and rulers of the house of Israel,<br>who despise justice<br>and pervert all that is right,<br>
<sup>10</sup> who build Zion with bloodshed<br>and Jerusalem with iniquity.<br>
<sup>11</sup> Her leaders judge for a bribe,<br>her priests teach for a price,<br>and her prophets practice divination for money.<br>Yet they lean upon the LORD, saying,<br>“Is not the LORD among us?<br>No disaster can come upon us.”<br>
<sup>12</sup> Therefore, because of you,<br>Zion will be plowed like a field,<br>Jerusalem will become a heap of rubble,<br>and the temple mount a wooded ridge.

## Chapter 4

### The Mountain of the House of the LORD<sup>a</sup>

<sup>1</sup> In the last days the mountain of the house of the LORD<br>will be established as the chief of the mountains;<br>it will be raised above the hills,<br>and the peoples will stream to it.

<sup>2</sup> And many nations will come and say:

“Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD,<br>to the house of the God of Jacob.<br>He will teach us His ways,<br>so that we may walk in His paths.”

For the law will go forth from Zion<br>and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Then He will judge between many peoples<br>and arbitrate for strong nations far and wide.<br>Then they will beat their swords into plowshares<br>and their spears into pruning hooks.<br>Nation will no longer take up the sword against nation,<br>nor will they train anymore for war.<br>
<sup>4</sup> And each man will sit under his own vine<br>and under his own fig tree,<br>with no one to frighten him.<br>For the mouth of the LORD of Hosts has spoken.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Though all the nations<br>may walk in the name of their gods,<br>yet we will walk in the name of the LORD our God<br>forever and ever.

### The Restoration of Zion<sup>b</sup>

<sup>6</sup> “On that day,” declares the LORD,<br>“I will gather the lame;<br>I will assemble the outcast,<br>even those whom I have afflicted.<br>
<sup>7</sup> And I will make the lame into a remnant,<br>the outcast into a strong nation.<br>Then the LORD will rule over them in Mount Zion<br>from that day and forever.<br>
<sup>8</sup> And you, O watchtower of the flock,<br>O stronghold<sup>c</sup> of the Daughter of Zion—<br>the former dominion will be restored to you;<br>sovereignty will come to the Daughter of Jerusalem.”

<sup>9</sup> Why do you now cry aloud?<br>Is there no king among you?<br>Has your counselor perished<br>so that anguish grips you like a woman in labor?<br>
<sup>10</sup> Writhe in agony, O Daughter of Zion,<br>like a woman in labor.<br>For now you will leave the city<br>and camp in the open fields.<br>You will go to Babylon;<br>there you will be rescued;<br>there the LORD will redeem you<br>from the hand of your enemies!

<sup>11</sup> But now many nations<br>have assembled against you,<br>saying, “Let her be defiled,<br>and let us feast our eyes on Zion.”<br>
<sup>12</sup> But they do not know the thoughts of the LORD<br>or understand His plan,<br>for He has gathered them<br>like sheaves to the threshing floor.<br>
<sup>13</sup> Rise and thresh, O Daughter of Zion,<br>for I will give you horns of iron<br>and hooves of bronze<br>to break to pieces many peoples.<br>Then you will devote<sup>d</sup> their gain to the LORD,<br>their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.

> - <sup>a</sup> Heading. Isaiah 2:1–4
> - <sup>b</sup> Heading. Zechariah 8:1–23
> - <sup>c</sup> 8. Or _hill_; Hebrew _And you, Migdal-eder, the Ophel_
> - <sup>d</sup> 13. Or _devote to destruction_

## Chapter 5

### A Ruler from Bethlehem<sup>a</sup>

<sup>1</sup> Now, O daughter of troops,<br>mobilize your troops;<br>for a siege is laid against us!<br>With a rod they will strike the cheek<br>of the judge of Israel.<br>
<sup>2</sup> But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah,<br>who are small among the clans<sup>b</sup> of Judah,<br>out of you will come forth for Me<br>One to be ruler over Israel<sup>c</sup>—<br>One whose origins are of old,<br>from the days of eternity.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Therefore Israel will be abandoned<br>until she who is in labor has given birth;<br>then the rest of His brothers will return<br>to the children of Israel.

<sup>4</sup> He will stand and shepherd His flock<br>in the strength of the LORD,<br>in the majestic name of the LORD His God.<br>And they will dwell securely,<br>for then His greatness will extend<br>to the ends of the earth.<br>
<sup>5</sup> And He will be our peace<sup>d</sup><br>when Assyria invades our land<br>and tramples our citadels.<br>We will raise against it seven shepherds,<br>even eight leaders of men.<br>
<sup>6</sup> And they will rule the land of Assyria with the sword,<br>and the land of Nimrod with the blade drawn.<br>So He will deliver us<br>when Assyria invades our land<br>and marches into our borders.

### The Remnant of Jacob<sup>e</sup>

<sup>7</sup> Then the remnant of Jacob will be<br>in the midst of many peoples<br>like dew from the LORD,<br>like showers on the grass,<br>which do not wait for man<br>or linger for mankind.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Then the remnant of Jacob will be among the nations,<br>in the midst of many peoples,<br>like a lion among the beasts of the forest,<br>like a young lion among flocks of sheep,<br>which tramples and tears as it passes through,<br>with no one to rescue them.<br>
<sup>9</sup> Your hand will be lifted over your foes,<br>and all your enemies will be cut off.

<sup>10</sup> “In that day,”<br>declares the LORD,<br>“I will remove your horses from among you<br>and wreck your chariots.<br>
<sup>11</sup> I will remove the cities of your land<br>and tear down all your strongholds.<br>
<sup>12</sup> I will cut the sorceries from your hand,<br>and you will have no fortune-tellers.<br>
<sup>13</sup> I will also cut off the carved images<br>and sacred pillars from among you,<br>so that you will no longer bow down<br>to the work of your own hands.<br>
<sup>14</sup> I will root out the Asherah poles from your midst<br>and demolish your cities.<br>
<sup>15</sup> I will take vengeance in anger and wrath<br>upon the nations that have not obeyed Me.”

> - <sup>a</sup> Heading. Matthew 2:1–12
> - <sup>b</sup> 2. Or _thousands_
> - <sup>c</sup> 2. Cited in Matthew 2:6
> - <sup>d</sup> 5. Or _their peace_
> - <sup>e</sup> Heading. Micah 2:12–13

## Chapter 6

### The Case against Israel

<sup>1</sup> Hear now what the LORD says:

“Arise, plead your case before the mountains,<br>and let the hills hear your voice.<br>
<sup>2</sup> Hear, O mountains, the LORD’s indictment,<br>you enduring foundations of the earth.<br>For the LORD has a case against His people,<br>and He will argue it against Israel:

<sup>3</sup> ‘My people, what have I done to you?<br>Testify against Me how I have wearied you!<br>
<sup>4</sup> For I brought you up from the land of Egypt<br>and redeemed you from the house of slavery.<br>I sent Moses before you,<br>as well as Aaron and Miriam.<br>
<sup>5</sup> My people, remember what Balak king of Moab counseled<br>and what Balaam son of Beor answered.<br>Remember your journey from Shittim<sup>a</sup> to Gilgal,<br>so that you may acknowledge the righteousness of the LORD.’”

<sup>6</sup> With what shall I come before the LORD<br>when I bow before the God on high?<br>Should I come to Him with burnt offerings,<br>with year-old calves?<br>
<sup>7</sup> Would the LORD be pleased with thousands of rams,<br>with ten thousand rivers of oil?<br>Shall I present my firstborn for my transgression,<br>the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?

<sup>8</sup> He has shown you, O man, what is good.<br>And what does the LORD require of you<br>but to act justly, to love mercy,<br>and to walk humbly with your God?

### The Punishment of Israel

<sup>9</sup> The voice of the LORD calls out to the city<br>(and it is sound wisdom to fear Your name):<br>“Heed the rod<br>and the One who ordained it.<br>
<sup>10</sup> Can I forget any longer,<br>O house of the wicked,<br>the treasures of wickedness<br>and the short ephah, which is accursed?<br>
<sup>11</sup> Can I excuse dishonest scales<br>or bags of false weights?<br>
<sup>12</sup> For the wealthy of the city<br>are full of violence,<br>and its residents speak lies;<br>their tongues are deceitful in their mouths.

<sup>13</sup> Therefore I am striking you severely,<br>to ruin you because of your sins.<br>
<sup>14</sup> You will eat but not be satisfied,<br>and your hunger will remain with you.<br>What you acquire, you will not preserve;<br>and what you save, I will give to the sword.<br>
<sup>15</sup> You will sow but not reap;<br>you will press olives but not anoint yourselves with oil;<br>you will tread grapes but not drink the wine.<br>
<sup>16</sup> You have kept the statutes of Omri<br>and all the practices of Ahab’s house;<br>you have followed their counsel.<br>Therefore I will make you a desolation,<br>and your inhabitants an object of contempt;<br>you will bear the scorn of the nations.”<sup>b</sup>

> - <sup>a</sup> 5. Or _Acacia Grove_
> - <sup>b</sup> 16. LXX; Hebrew _scorn of My people_

## Chapter 7

### Israel’s Great Misery<sup>a</sup>

<sup>1</sup> Woe is me!

For I am like one gathering summer fruit<br>at the gleaning of the vineyard;<br>there is no cluster to eat,<br>no early fig that I crave.<br>
<sup>2</sup> The godly man has perished from the earth;<br>there is no one upright among men.<br>They all lie in wait for blood;<br>they hunt one another with a net.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Both hands are skilled at evil;<br>the prince and the judge demand a bribe.<br>When the powerful utters his evil desire,<br>they all conspire together.<br>
<sup>4</sup> The best of them is like a brier;<br>the most upright is sharper than a hedge of thorns.<br>The day for your watchmen has come,<br>the day of your visitation.<sup>b</sup><br>Now is the time of their confusion.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Do not rely on a friend;<br>do not trust in a companion.<br>Seal the doors of your mouth<br>from her who lies in your arms.<sup>c</sup><br>
<sup>6</sup> For a son dishonors his father,<br>a daughter rises against her mother,<br>and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law.<br>A man’s enemies are the members<br>of his own household.<sup>d</sup>

### Israel’s Confession and Comfort

<sup>7</sup> But as for me, I will look to the LORD;<br>I will wait for the God of my salvation.<br>My God will hear me.

<sup>8</sup> Do not gloat over me, my enemy!<br>Though I have fallen, I will arise;<br>though I sit in darkness,<br>the LORD will be my light.<br>
<sup>9</sup> Because I have sinned against Him,<br>I must endure the rage of the LORD,<br>until He argues my case<br>and executes justice for me.<br>He will bring me into the light;<br>I will see His righteousness.<br>
<sup>10</sup> Then my enemy will see<br>and will be covered with shame—<br>she who said to me,<br>“Where is the LORD your God?”<br>My eyes will see her;<br>at that time she will be trampled<br>like mud in the streets.

<sup>11</sup> The day for rebuilding your walls will come—<br>the day for extending your boundary.<br>
<sup>12</sup> On that day they will come to you<br>from Assyria and the cities of Egypt,<br>even from Egypt to the Euphrates,<sup>e</sup><br>from sea to sea and mountain to mountain.<br>
<sup>13</sup> Then the earth will become desolate<br>because of its inhabitants,<br>as the fruit of their deeds.

### God’s Compassion on Israel

<sup>14</sup> Shepherd with Your staff Your people,<br>the flock of Your inheritance.<br>They live alone in a woodland,<br>surrounded by pastures.<sup>f</sup><br>Let them graze in Bashan and Gilead,<br>as in the days of old.

<sup>15</sup> As in the days when you came out of Egypt,<br>I will show My wonders.<br>
<sup>16</sup> Nations will see and be ashamed,<br>deprived of all their might.<br>They will put their hands over their mouths,<br>and their ears will become deaf.<br>
<sup>17</sup> They will lick the dust like a snake,<br>like reptiles slithering on the ground.<br>They will come trembling from their strongholds<br>in the presence of the LORD our God;<br>they will tremble in fear of You.

<sup>18</sup> Who is a God like You,<br>who pardons iniquity<br>and passes over the transgression<br>of the remnant of His inheritance—<br>who does not retain His anger forever,<br>because He delights in loving devotion?<sup>g</sup><br>
<sup>19</sup> He will again have compassion on us;<br>He will vanquish our iniquities.<br>You will cast out all our sins<br>into the depths of the sea.<br>
<sup>20</sup> You will show faithfulness to Jacob<br>and loving devotion to Abraham,<br>as You swore to our fathers<br>from the days of old.

> - <sup>a</sup> Heading. Matthew 10:34–39; Luke 12:49–53
> - <sup>b</sup> 4. Or _your punishment_
> - <sup>c</sup> 5. Hebrew _in your bosom_
> - <sup>d</sup> 6. Cited in Matthew 10:35–36; see also Luke 12:53.
> - <sup>e</sup> 12. Hebrew _the River_
> - <sup>f</sup> 14. Or _in a woodland, in the midst of Carmel_
> - <sup>g</sup> 18. Forms of the Hebrew _chesed_ are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as _loving devotion_; the range of meaning includes _love_, _goodness_, _kindness_, _faithfulness_, and _mercy_, as well as _loyalty to a covenant_.
