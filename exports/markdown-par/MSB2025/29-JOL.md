## Chapter 1

### The Invasion of Locusts

<sup>1</sup> This is the word of the LORD that came to Joel son of Pethuel:

<sup>2</sup> Hear this, O elders;<br>and give ear, all who dwell in the land.<br>Has anything like this ever happened in your days<br>or in the days of your fathers?<br>
<sup>3</sup> Tell it to your children;<br>let your children tell it to their children,<br>and their children to the next generation.<br>
<sup>4</sup> What the devouring locust has left,<br>the swarming locust has eaten;<br>what the swarming locust has left,<br>the young locust has eaten;<br>and what the young locust has left,<br>the destroying locust has eaten.<sup>a</sup>

<sup>5</sup> Wake up, you drunkards, and weep;<br>wail, all you drinkers of wine,<br>because of the sweet wine,<br>for it has been cut off from your mouth.<br>
<sup>6</sup> For a nation has invaded My land,<br>powerful and without number;<br>its teeth are the teeth of a lion,<br>and its fangs are the fangs of a lioness.<br>
<sup>7</sup> It has laid waste My grapevine<br>and splintered My fig tree.<br>It has stripped off the bark and thrown it away;<br>the branches have turned white.

### A Call to Mourning

<sup>8</sup> Wail like a virgin dressed in sackcloth,<br>grieving for the husband of her youth.<br>
<sup>9</sup> Grain and drink offerings have been cut off<br>from the house of the LORD;<br>the priests are in mourning,<br>those who minister before the LORD.<br>
<sup>10</sup> The field is ruined;<br>the land mourns.<br>For the grain is destroyed,<br>the new wine is dried up, and the oil fails.<br>
<sup>11</sup> Be dismayed, O farmers,<br>wail, O vinedressers,<br>over the wheat and barley,<br>because the harvest of the field has perished.<br>
<sup>12</sup> The grapevine is dried up,<br>and the fig tree is withered;<br>the pomegranate, palm, and apple—<br>all the trees of the orchard—are withered.<br>Surely the joy of mankind has dried up.

### A Call to Repentance<sup>b</sup>

<sup>13</sup> Put on sackcloth and lament, O priests;<br>wail, O ministers of the altar.<br>Come, spend the night in sackcloth,<br>O ministers of my God,<br>because the grain and drink offerings<br>are withheld from the house of your God.<br>
<sup>14</sup> Consecrate a fast;<br>proclaim a solemn assembly!<br>Gather the elders<br>and all the residents of the land<br>to the house of the LORD your God,<br>and cry out to the LORD.

<sup>15</sup> Alas for the day!<br>For the Day of the LORD is near,<br>and it will come<br>as destruction from the Almighty.<sup>c</sup><br>
<sup>16</sup> Has not the food been cut off<br>before our very eyes—<br>joy and gladness<br>from the house of our God?<br>
<sup>17</sup> The seeds lie shriveled beneath the clods;<br>the storehouses are in ruins;<br>the granaries are broken down,<br>for the grain has withered away.<br>
<sup>18</sup> How the cattle groan!<br>The herds wander in confusion<br>because they have no pasture.<br>Even the flocks of sheep are suffering.

<sup>19</sup> To You, O LORD, I call,<br>for fire has consumed the open pastures<br>and flames have scorched all the trees of the field.<br>
<sup>20</sup> Even the beasts of the field pant for You,<br>for the streams of water have dried up,<br>and fire has consumed the open pastures.

> - <sup>a</sup> 4. The precise identification of the four kinds of locusts mentioned here is uncertain.
> - <sup>b</sup> Heading. Amos 5:4–15; Zephaniah 2:1–3; Luke 13:1–5
> - <sup>c</sup> 15. Hebrew _Shaddai_

## Chapter 2

### The Army of Locusts<sup>a</sup>

<sup>1</sup> Blow the ram’s horn in Zion;<br>sound the alarm on My holy mountain!<br>Let all who dwell in the land tremble,<br>for the Day of the LORD is coming;<br>indeed, it is near—<br>
<sup>2</sup> a day of darkness and gloom,<br>a day of clouds and blackness.<br>Like the dawn overspreading the mountains<br>a great and strong army appears,<br>such as never was of old,<br>nor will ever be in ages to come.

<sup>3</sup> Before them a fire devours,<br>and behind them a flame scorches.<br>The land before them is like the Garden of Eden,<br>but behind them, it is like a desert wasteland—<br>surely nothing will escape them.<br>
<sup>4</sup> Their appearance is like that of horses,<br>and they gallop like swift steeds.<sup>b</sup><br>
<sup>5</sup> With a sound like that of chariots<br>they bound over the mountaintops,<br>like the crackling of fire consuming stubble,<br>like a mighty army deployed for battle.

<sup>6</sup> Nations writhe in horror before them;<br>every face turns pale.<br>
<sup>7</sup> They charge like mighty men;<br>they scale the walls like men of war.<br>Each one marches in formation,<br>not swerving from the course.<br>
<sup>8</sup> They do not jostle one another;<br>each proceeds in his path.<br>They burst through the defenses,<br>never breaking ranks.<br>
<sup>9</sup> They storm the city;<br>they run along the wall;<br>they climb into houses,<br>entering through windows like thieves.

<sup>10</sup> Before them the earth quakes;<br>the heavens tremble.<br>The sun and moon grow dark,<br>and the stars lose their brightness.<br>
<sup>11</sup> The LORD raises His voice<br>in the presence of His army.<br>Indeed, His camp is very large,<br>for mighty are those who obey His command.<br>For the Day of the LORD is great and very dreadful.<br>Who can endure it?

### Return with All Your Heart

<sup>12</sup> “Yet even now,”<br>declares the LORD,<br>“return to Me with all your heart,<br>with fasting, weeping, and mourning.”

<sup>13</sup> So rend your hearts and not your garments,<br>and return to the LORD your God.<br>For He is gracious and compassionate,<br>slow to anger, abounding in loving devotion.<sup>c</sup><br>And He relents from sending disaster.<br>
<sup>14</sup> Who knows? He may turn and relent<br>and leave a blessing behind Him—<br>grain and drink offerings<br>for the LORD your God.

<sup>15</sup> Blow the ram’s horn in Zion,<br>consecrate a fast,<br>proclaim a sacred assembly.<br>
<sup>16</sup> Gather the people, sanctify the congregation,<br>assemble the aged, gather the children,<br>even those nursing at the breast.<br>Let the bridegroom leave his room,<br>and the bride her chamber.<br>
<sup>17</sup> Let the priests who minister before the LORD<br>weep between the portico and the altar,<br>saying, “Spare Your people, O LORD,<br>and do not make Your heritage a reproach,<br>an object of scorn among the nations.<br>Why should they say among the peoples,<br>‘Where is their God?’”

### Restoration Promised

<sup>18</sup> Then the LORD became jealous for His land,<br>and He spared His people.

<sup>19</sup> And the LORD answered His people:

“Behold, I will send you<br>grain, new wine, and oil,<br>and by them you will be satisfied.<br>I will never again make you<br>a reproach among the nations.

<sup>20</sup> The northern army I will drive away from you,<br>banishing it to a barren and desolate land,<br>its front ranks into the Eastern Sea,<sup>d</sup><br>and its rear guard into the Western Sea.<sup>e</sup><br>And its stench will rise;<br>its foul odor will ascend.

For He has done great things.

<sup>21</sup> Do not be afraid, O land;<br>rejoice and be glad,<br>for the LORD has done great things.<br>
<sup>22</sup> Do not be afraid, O beasts of the field,<br>for the open pastures have turned green,<br>the trees bear their fruit,<br>and the fig tree and vine yield their best.<sup>f</sup><br>
<sup>23</sup> Be glad, O children of Zion,<br>and rejoice in the LORD your God,<br>for He has given you the autumn rains<br>for your vindication.<br>He sends you showers,<br>both autumn and spring rains, as before.<br>
<sup>24</sup> The threshing floors will be full of grain,<br>and the vats will overflow with new wine and oil.

<sup>25</sup> I will repay you for the years eaten by locusts—<br>the swarming locust, the young locust,<br>the destroying locust, and the devouring locust<sup>g</sup>—<br>My great army that I sent against you.<br>
<sup>26</sup> You will have plenty to eat,<br>until you are satisfied.<br>You will praise the name of the LORD your God,<br>who has worked wonders for you.<br>My people will never again<br>be put to shame.<br>
<sup>27</sup> Then you will know that I am present in Israel<br>and that I am the LORD your God,<br>and there is no other.<br>My people will never again<br>be put to shame.

### I Will Pour Out My Spirit<sup>h</sup>

<sup>28</sup> And afterward, I will pour out My Spirit on all people.<br>Your sons and daughters will prophesy,<br>your old men will dream dreams,<br>your young men will see visions.<br>
<sup>29</sup> Even on My menservants and maidservants,<br>I will pour out My Spirit in those days.<br>
<sup>30</sup> I will show wonders in the heavens and on the earth,<br>blood and fire and columns<sup>i</sup> of smoke.<br>
<sup>31</sup> The sun will be turned to darkness<br>and the moon to blood<br>before the coming of the great and awesome<sup>j</sup> Day of the LORD.<br>
<sup>32</sup> And everyone who calls on the name of the LORD<br>will be saved;<sup>k</sup><br>for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem<br>there will be deliverance, as the LORD has promised,<br>among the remnant called by the LORD.

> - <sup>a</sup> Heading. Amos 7:1–9
> - <sup>b</sup> 4. Or _like charioteers_ or _like cavalry_
> - <sup>c</sup> 13. Forms of the Hebrew _chesed_ are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as _loving devotion_; the range of meaning includes _love_, _goodness_, _kindness_, _faithfulness_, and _mercy_, as well as _loyalty to a covenant_.
> - <sup>d</sup> 20. That is, the Dead Sea
> - <sup>e</sup> 20. That is, the Mediterranean Sea, also called the Great Sea
> - <sup>f</sup> 22. Literally _their strength_
> - <sup>g</sup> 25. The precise identification of the four kinds of insects mentioned here is uncertain.
> - <sup>h</sup> Heading. Acts 2:14–36
> - <sup>i</sup> 30. LXX _clouds_ or _billows_
> - <sup>j</sup> 31. Or _dreadful_; LXX _glorious_
> - <sup>k</sup> 32. Cited in Acts 2:17–21 and Romans 10:13

## Chapter 3

### The LORD Judges the Nations

<sup>1</sup> “Yes, in those days and at that time,<br>when I restore Judah and Jerusalem from captivity,<sup>a</sup><br>
<sup>2</sup> I will gather all the nations<br>and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat.<sup>b</sup><br>There I will enter into judgment against them<br>concerning My people, My inheritance, Israel,<br>whom they have scattered among the nations<br>as they divided up My land.<br>
<sup>3</sup> They cast lots for My people;<br>they bartered a boy for a prostitute<br>and sold a girl for wine to drink.

<sup>4</sup> Now what do you have against Me, O Tyre, Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Are you rendering against Me a recompense? If you retaliate against Me, I will swiftly and speedily return your recompense upon your heads.
<sup>5</sup> For you took My silver and gold and carried off My finest treasures to your temples.<sup>c</sup>
<sup>6</sup> You sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks,<sup>d</sup> to send them far from their homeland.

<sup>7</sup> Behold, I will rouse them from the places to which you sold them; I will return your recompense upon your heads.
<sup>8</sup> I will sell your sons and daughters into the hands of the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans—to a distant nation.”

Indeed, the LORD has spoken.

<sup>9</sup> Proclaim this among the nations:

“Prepare for war;<br>rouse the mighty men;<br>let all the men of war<br>advance and attack!<br>
<sup>10</sup> Beat your plowshares into swords<br>and your pruning hooks into spears.<br>Let the weak say, ‘I am strong!’<br>
<sup>11</sup> Come quickly, all you surrounding nations,<br>and gather yourselves.<br>Bring down Your mighty ones,<br>O LORD.

<sup>12</sup> Let the nations be roused<br>and advance to the Valley of Jehoshaphat,<br>for there I will sit down<br>to judge all the nations on every side.<br>
<sup>13</sup> Swing the sickle,<br>for the harvest is ripe.<sup>e</sup><br>Come, trample the grapes,<br>for the winepress is full;<br>the wine vats overflow<br>because their wickedness is great.

<sup>14</sup> Multitudes, multitudes<br>in the valley of decision!<br>For the Day of the LORD is near<br>in the valley of decision.<br>
<sup>15</sup> The sun and moon will grow dark,<br>and the stars will no longer shine.<br>
<sup>16</sup> The LORD will roar from Zion<br>and raise His voice from Jerusalem;<br>heaven and earth will tremble.<br>But the LORD will be a refuge for His people,<br>a stronghold for the people of Israel.

### Blessings for God’s People

<sup>17</sup> Then you will know that I am the LORD your God,<br>who dwells in Zion, My holy mountain.<br>Jerusalem will be holy,<br>never again to be overrun by foreigners.

<sup>18</sup> And in that day the mountains will drip with sweet wine,<br>and the hills will flow with milk.<br>All the streams of Judah will run with water,<br>and a spring will flow from the house of the LORD<br>to water the Valley of Acacias.<sup>f</sup><br>
<sup>19</sup> Egypt will become desolate,<br>and Edom a desert wasteland,<br>because of the violence done to the people of Judah,<br>in whose land they shed innocent blood.<br>
<sup>20</sup> But Judah will be inhabited forever,<br>and Jerusalem from generation to generation.<br>
<sup>21</sup> For I will avenge their blood,<br>which I have not yet avenged.”<sup>g</sup> For the LORD dwells in Zion.

> - <sup>a</sup> 1. Or _restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem_
> - <sup>b</sup> 2. _Jehoshaphat_ means _the LORD judges_; also in verse 12.
> - <sup>c</sup> 5. Or _palaces_
> - <sup>d</sup> 6. Or _to the peoples of Javan_
> - <sup>e</sup> 13. LXX _the harvest has come_; see also Mark 4:29.
> - <sup>f</sup> 18. Or _Valley of Shittim_
> - <sup>g</sup> 21. Or _For I will pardon their bloodguilt, which I have not yet pardoned._
