## Chapter 1

### The Burden against Nineveh<sup>a</sup>

<sup>1</sup> This is the burden against Nineveh, the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite:

<sup>2</sup> The LORD is a jealous and avenging God;<br>the LORD is avenging and full of wrath.<br>The LORD takes vengeance on His foes<br>and reserves wrath for His enemies.<br>
<sup>3</sup> The LORD is slow to anger<br>and great in power;<br>the LORD will by no means<br>leave the guilty unpunished.<br>His path is in the whirlwind and storm,<br>and clouds are the dust beneath His feet.<br>
<sup>4</sup> He rebukes the sea and dries it up;<br>He makes all the rivers run dry.<br>Bashan and Carmel wither,<br>and the flower of Lebanon wilts.<br>
<sup>5</sup> The mountains quake before Him,<br>and the hills melt away;<br>the earth trembles at His presence—<br>the world and all its dwellers.<br>
<sup>6</sup> Who can withstand His indignation?<br>Who can endure His burning anger?<br>His wrath is poured out like fire;<br>even rocks are shattered before Him.

<sup>7</sup> The LORD is good,<br>a stronghold in the day of distress;<br>He cares for those who trust in Him.<br>
<sup>8</sup> But with an overwhelming flood<br>He will make an end of Nineveh<sup>b</sup><br>and pursue His enemies into darkness.

<sup>9</sup> Whatever you plot against the LORD,<br>He will bring to an end.<br>Affliction will not rise up<br>a second time.<br>
<sup>10</sup> For they will be entangled as with thorns<br>and consumed like the drink of a drunkard—<br>like stubble that is fully dry.<br>
<sup>11</sup> From you, O Nineveh, comes forth<br>a plotter of evil against the LORD,<br>a counselor of wickedness.

<sup>12</sup> This is what the LORD says:

“Though they are allied and numerous,<br>yet they will be cut down and pass away.<br>Though I have afflicted you, O Judah,<br>I will afflict you no longer.<br>
<sup>13</sup> For I will now break their yoke from your neck<br>and tear away your shackles.”

<sup>14</sup> The LORD has issued a command concerning you, O Nineveh:

“There will be no descendants<br>to carry on your name.<br>I will cut off the carved image and cast idol<br>from the house of your gods;<br>I will prepare your grave,<br>for you are contemptible.”

<sup>15</sup> Look to the mountains—<br>the feet of one who brings good news,<br>who proclaims peace!<br>Celebrate your feasts, O Judah;<br>fulfill your vows.<br>For the wicked will never again march through you;<br>they will be utterly cut off.

> - <sup>a</sup> Heading. Jonah 1:1–3
> - <sup>b</sup> 8. Literally _of her place_

## Chapter 2

### The Overthrow of Nineveh

<sup>1</sup> One who scatters advances<br>against you, O Nineveh.<br>Guard the fortress!<br>Watch the road!<br>Brace yourselves!<sup>a</sup><br>Summon all your strength!

<sup>2</sup> For the LORD will restore the splendor of Jacob<br>like the splendor of Israel,<br>though destroyers have laid them waste<br>and ruined the branches of their vine.

<sup>3</sup> The shields of his mighty men are red;<br>the valiant warriors are dressed in scarlet.<br>The fittings of the chariots flash like fire<br>on the day they are prepared,<br>and the spears of cypress<br>have been brandished.<sup>b</sup><br>
<sup>4</sup> The chariots dash through the streets;<br>they rush around the plazas,<br>appearing like torches,<br>darting about like lightning.<br>
<sup>5</sup> He summons his nobles;<br>they stumble as they advance.<br>They race to its wall;<br>the protective shield is set in place.<br>
<sup>6</sup> The river gates are thrown open<br>and the palace collapses.

<sup>7</sup> It is decreed that the city be exiled<br>and carried away;<br>her maidservants moan like doves,<br>and beat upon their breasts.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Nineveh has been like a pool of water<br>throughout her days,<br>but now it is draining away.<br>“Stop! Stop!” they cry,<br>but no one turns back.<br>
<sup>9</sup> “Plunder the silver!<br>Plunder the gold!”<br>There is no end to the treasure,<br>an abundance of every precious thing.<br>
<sup>10</sup> She is emptied!<br>Yes, she is desolate and laid waste!<br>Hearts melt, knees knock,<br>bodies tremble, and every face grows pale!

<sup>11</sup> Where is the lions’ lair<br>or the feeding ground of the young lions,<br>where the lion and lioness prowled with their cubs,<br>with nothing to frighten them away?<br>
<sup>12</sup> The lion mauled enough for its cubs<br>and strangled prey for the lioness.<br>It filled its dens with the kill,<br>and its lairs with mauled prey.

<sup>13</sup> “Behold, I am against you,”<br>declares the LORD of Hosts.<br>“I will reduce your chariots to cinders,<br>and the sword will devour your young lions.<br>I will cut off your prey from the earth,<br>and the voices of your messengers<br>will no longer be heard.”

> - <sup>a</sup> 1. Hebrew _Strengthen your loins!_
> - <sup>b</sup> 3. Hebrew; alternately, the spears may be of _pine_ or _juniper_ or _fir_; LXX and Syriac _they are prepared, and the horsemen rush to and fro._

## Chapter 3

### Judgment on Nineveh

<sup>1</sup> Woe to the city of blood,<br>full of lies,<br>full of plunder,<br>never without prey.<br>
<sup>2</sup> The crack of the whip,<br>the rumble of the wheel,<br>galloping horse<br>and bounding chariot!<br>
<sup>3</sup> Charging horseman,<br>flashing sword,<br>shining spear;<br>heaps of slain,<br>mounds of corpses,<br>dead bodies without end—<br>they stumble over their dead—<br>
<sup>4</sup> because of the many harlotries of the harlot,<br>the seductive mistress of sorcery,<br>who betrays nations by her prostitution<br>and clans by her witchcraft.

<sup>5</sup> “Behold, I am against you,”<br>declares the LORD of Hosts.<br>“I will lift your skirts over your face.<br>I will show your nakedness to the nations<br>and your shame to the kingdoms.<br>
<sup>6</sup> I will pelt you with filth<br>and treat you with contempt;<br>I will make a spectacle of you.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Then all who see you<br>will recoil from you and say,<br>‘Nineveh is devastated;<br>who will grieve for her?’<br>Where can I find<br>comforters for you?”

<sup>8</sup> Are you better than Thebes,<sup>a</sup><br>stationed by the Nile with water around her,<br>whose rampart was the sea,<br>whose wall was the water?<br>
<sup>9</sup> Cush<sup>b</sup> and Egypt were her boundless strength;<br>Put and Libya were her allies.<br>
<sup>10</sup> Yet she became an exile;<br>she went into captivity.<br>Her infants were dashed to pieces<br>at the head of every street.<br>They cast lots for her dignitaries,<br>and all her nobles were bound in chains.<br>
<sup>11</sup> You too will become drunk;<br>you will go into hiding<br>and seek refuge from the enemy.

<sup>12</sup> All your fortresses are fig trees<br>with the first ripe figs;<br>when shaken, they fall<br>into the mouth of the eater!

<sup>13</sup> Look at your troops—<br>they are like your women!<br>The gates of your land<br>are wide open to your enemies;<br>fire consumes their bars.

<sup>14</sup> Draw your water for the siege;<br>strengthen your fortresses.<br>Work the clay and tread the mortar;<br>repair the brick kiln!<br>
<sup>15</sup> There the fire will devour you;<br>the sword will cut you down<br>and consume you like a young locust.<br>Make yourself many like the young locust;<br>make yourself many like the swarming locust!<br>
<sup>16</sup> You have multiplied your merchants<br>more than the stars of the sky.<br>The young locust strips the land<br>and flies away.<br>
<sup>17</sup> Your guards<sup>c</sup> are like the swarming locust,<br>and your scribes<sup>d</sup> like clouds of locusts<br>that settle on the walls on a cold day.<br>When the sun rises, they fly away,<br>and no one knows where.

<sup>18</sup> O king of Assyria, your shepherds slumber;<br>your officers sleep.<br>Your people are scattered on the mountains<br>with no one to gather them.<br>
<sup>19</sup> There is no healing for your injury;<br>your wound is severe.<br>All who hear the news of you<br>applaud your downfall,<br>for who has not experienced<br>your constant cruelty?

> - <sup>a</sup> 8. Hebrew _No-amon_
> - <sup>b</sup> 9. That is, the upper Nile region
> - <sup>c</sup> 17. Or _princes_
> - <sup>d</sup> 17. Or _marshals_
