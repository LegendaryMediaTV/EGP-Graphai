## Chapter 1

### The Beginning of Knowledge<sup>a</sup>

<sup>1</sup> These are the proverbs of Solomon son of David,<br>king of Israel,<br>
<sup>2</sup> for gaining wisdom and discipline,<br>for comprehending words of insight,<br>
<sup>3</sup> and for receiving instruction in wise living<br>and in righteousness, justice, and equity.<br>
<sup>4</sup> To impart prudence to the simple<sup>b</sup><br>and knowledge and discretion to the young,<br>
<sup>5</sup> let the wise listen and gain instruction,<br>and the discerning acquire wise counsel<br>
<sup>6</sup> by understanding the proverbs and parables,<br>the sayings and riddles of the wise.

<sup>7</sup> The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge,<br>but fools<sup>c</sup> despise wisdom and discipline.

### The Enticement of Sin

<sup>8</sup> Listen, my son, to your father’s instruction,<br>and do not forsake the teaching of your mother.<br>
<sup>9</sup> For they are a garland of grace on your head<br>and a pendant around your neck.

<sup>10</sup> My son, if sinners entice you,<br>do not yield to them.<br>
<sup>11</sup> If they say, “Come along, let us lie in wait for blood,<br>let us ambush the innocent without cause,<br>
<sup>12</sup> let us swallow them alive like Sheol,<br>and whole like those descending into the Pit.<br>
<sup>13</sup> We will find all manner of precious goods;<br>we will fill our houses with plunder.<br>
<sup>14</sup> Throw in your lot with us;<br>let us all share one purse”—<br>
<sup>15</sup> my son, do not walk the road with them<br>or set foot upon their path.<br>
<sup>16</sup> For their feet run to evil,<br>and they are swift to shed blood.<br>
<sup>17</sup> How futile it is to spread the net<br>where any bird can see it!<br>
<sup>18</sup> But they lie in wait for their own blood;<br>they ambush their own lives.<br>
<sup>19</sup> Such is the fate of all who are greedy,<br>whose unjust gain takes the lives of its possessors.

### Wisdom Calls Aloud

<sup>20</sup> Wisdom calls out in the street,<br>she lifts her voice in the square;<br>
<sup>21</sup> in the main concourse<sup>d</sup> she cries aloud,<br>at the city gates she makes her speech:

<sup>22</sup> “How long, O simple ones, will you love your simple ways?<br>How long will scoffers delight in their scorn<br>and fools hate knowledge?<br>
<sup>23</sup> If you had repented at my rebuke,<br>then surely I would have poured out my spirit on you;<br>I would have made my words known to you.<br>
<sup>24</sup> Because you refused my call,<br>and no one took my outstretched hand,<br>
<sup>25</sup> because you neglected all my counsel,<br>and wanted none of my correction,<br>
<sup>26</sup> in turn I will mock your calamity;<br>I will sneer when terror strikes you,<br>
<sup>27</sup> when your dread comes like a storm,<br>and your destruction like a whirlwind,<br>when distress and anguish overwhelm you.

<sup>28</sup> Then they will call on me, but I will not answer;<br>they will earnestly seek me, but will not find me.<br>
<sup>29</sup> For they hated knowledge<br>and chose not to fear the LORD.<br>
<sup>30</sup> They accepted none of my counsel;<br>they despised all my reproof.<br>
<sup>31</sup> So they will eat the fruit of their own way,<br>and be filled with their own devices.<br>
<sup>32</sup> For the waywardness of the simple will slay them,<br>and the complacency of fools will destroy them.<br>
<sup>33</sup> But whoever listens to me will dwell in safety,<br>secure from the fear of evil.”

> - <sup>a</sup> Heading. Proverbs 9:1–12
> - <sup>b</sup> 4. The Hebrew word rendered _simple_ in Proverbs refers to one who is naive, without moral direction, or inclined to evil.
> - <sup>c</sup> 7. The Hebrew words rendered as _fool_ in Proverbs denote one who is morally deficient.
> - <sup>d</sup> 21. Or _from the top of the wall_

## Chapter 2

### The Benefits of Wisdom

<sup>1</sup> My son, if you accept my words<br>and hide my commandments within you,<br>
<sup>2</sup> if you incline your ear to wisdom<br>and direct your heart to understanding,<br>
<sup>3</sup> if you truly call out to insight<br>and lift your voice to understanding,<br>
<sup>4</sup> if you seek it like silver<br>and search it out like hidden treasure,<br>
<sup>5</sup> then you will discern the fear of the LORD<br>and discover the knowledge of God.

<sup>6</sup> For the LORD gives wisdom;<br>from His mouth come knowledge and understanding.<br>
<sup>7</sup> He stores up sound wisdom for the upright;<br>He is a shield to those who walk with integrity,<br>
<sup>8</sup> to guard the paths of justice<br>and protect the way of His saints.

<sup>9</sup> Then you will discern righteousness<br>and justice and equity—every good path.<br>
<sup>10</sup> For wisdom will enter your heart,<br>and knowledge will delight your soul.<br>
<sup>11</sup> Discretion will watch over you,<br>and understanding will guard you,<br>
<sup>12</sup> to deliver you from the way of evil,<br>from the man who speaks perversity,<br>
<sup>13</sup> from those who leave the straight paths<br>to walk in the ways of darkness,<br>
<sup>14</sup> from those who enjoy doing evil<br>and rejoice in the twistedness of evil,<br>
<sup>15</sup> whose paths are crooked<br>and whose ways are devious.

<sup>16</sup> It will rescue you from the forbidden woman,<sup>a</sup><br>from the stranger<sup>b</sup> with seductive words<br>
<sup>17</sup> who abandons the partner of her youth<br>and forgets the covenant of her God.<br>
<sup>18</sup> For her house sinks down to death,<br>and her tracks to the departed spirits.<sup>c</sup><br>
<sup>19</sup> None who go to her return<br>or negotiate the paths of life.

<sup>20</sup> So you will follow in the ways of the good,<br>and keep to the paths of the righteous.<br>
<sup>21</sup> For the upright will inhabit the land,<br>and the blameless will remain in it;<br>
<sup>22</sup> but the wicked will be cut off from the land,<br>and the unfaithful will be uprooted.

> - <sup>a</sup> 16. Or _wayward wife_
> - <sup>b</sup> 16. Or _adulteress_
> - <sup>c</sup> 18. Hebrew _to the Rephaim_

## Chapter 3

### Trust in the LORD with All Your Heart

<sup>1</sup> My son, do not forget my teaching,<br>but let your heart keep my commandments;<br>
<sup>2</sup> for they will add length to your days,<br>years and peace to your life.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Never let loving devotion<sup>a</sup> or faithfulness leave you;<br>bind them around your neck,<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.<br>
<sup>4</sup> Then you will find favor and high regard<br>in the sight of God and man.

<sup>5</sup> Trust in the LORD with all your heart,<br>and lean not on your own understanding;<br>
<sup>6</sup> in all your ways acknowledge Him,<br>and He will make your paths straight.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Be not wise in your own eyes;<br>fear the LORD and turn away from evil.<br>
<sup>8</sup> This will bring healing to your body<sup>b</sup><br>and refreshment<sup>c</sup> to your bones.

<sup>9</sup> Honor the LORD with your wealth<br>and with the firstfruits of all your crops;<br>
<sup>10</sup> then your barns will be filled with plenty,<br>and your vats will overflow with new wine.

<sup>11</sup> My son, do not reject the discipline of the LORD,<br>and do not loathe His rebuke;<sup>d</sup><br>
<sup>12</sup> for the LORD disciplines the one He loves,<br>as does a father the son in whom he delights.<sup>e</sup>

### The Blessings of Wisdom

<sup>13</sup> Blessed is the man who finds wisdom,<br>the man who acquires understanding,<br>
<sup>14</sup> for she is more profitable than silver,<br>and her gain is better than fine gold.<br>
<sup>15</sup> She is more precious than rubies;<br>nothing you desire compares with her.<br>
<sup>16</sup> Long life is in her right hand;<br>in her left hand are riches and honor.<br>
<sup>17</sup> All her ways are pleasant,<br>and all her paths are peaceful.<br>
<sup>18</sup> She is a tree of life to those who embrace her,<br>and those who lay hold of her are blessed.

<sup>19</sup> The LORD founded the earth by wisdom<br>and established the heavens by understanding.<br>
<sup>20</sup> By His knowledge the watery depths were broken open,<br>and the clouds dripped with dew.

<sup>21</sup> My son, do not lose sight of this:<br>Preserve sound judgment and discernment.<br>
<sup>22</sup> They will be life to your soul<br>and adornment to your neck.<br>
<sup>23</sup> Then you will go on your way in safety,<br>and your foot will not stumble.<br>
<sup>24</sup> When you lie down, you will not be afraid;<br>when you rest, your sleep will be sweet.<br>
<sup>25</sup> Do not fear sudden danger<br>or the ruin that overtakes the wicked,<br>
<sup>26</sup> for the LORD will be your confidence<br>and will keep your foot from the snare.

<sup>27</sup> Do not withhold good from the deserving<sup>f</sup><br>when it is within your power to act.<br>
<sup>28</sup> Do not tell your neighbor,<br>“Come back tomorrow and I will provide”—<br>when you already have the means.<br>
<sup>29</sup> Do not devise evil against your neighbor,<br>for he trustfully dwells beside you.<br>
<sup>30</sup> Do not accuse a man without cause,<br>when he has done you no harm.

<sup>31</sup> Do not envy a violent man<br>or choose any of his ways;<br>
<sup>32</sup> for the LORD detests the perverse,<br>but He is a friend to the upright.<br>
<sup>33</sup> The curse of the LORD is on the house of the wicked,<br>but He blesses the home of the righteous.<br>
<sup>34</sup> He mocks the mockers,<br>but gives grace to the humble.<sup>g</sup><br>
<sup>35</sup> The wise will inherit honor,<br>but fools are held up to shame.

> - <sup>a</sup> 3. Forms of the Hebrew _chesed_ are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as _loving devotion_; the range of meaning includes _love_, _goodness_, _kindness_, _faithfulness_, and _mercy_, as well as _loyalty to a covenant_.
> - <sup>b</sup> 8. Hebrew _navel_
> - <sup>c</sup> 8. Or _medicine_
> - <sup>d</sup> 11. LXX _and do not lose heart when He rebukes_; cited in Hebrews 12:5
> - <sup>e</sup> 12. LXX _and He flogs every son He receives_; cited in Hebrews 12:6
> - <sup>f</sup> 27. Or _from its owners_
> - <sup>g</sup> 34. LXX _The Lord opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble_; cited in James 4:6 and 1 Peter 5:5

## Chapter 4

### A Father’s Instruction

<sup>1</sup> Listen, my sons, to a father’s instruction;<br>pay attention and gain understanding.<br>
<sup>2</sup> For I give you sound teaching;<br>do not abandon my directive.<br>
<sup>3</sup> When I was a son to my father,<br>tender and the only child of my mother,<br>
<sup>4</sup> he taught me and said,<br>“Let your heart lay hold of my words;<br>keep my commands and you will live.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Get wisdom, get understanding;<br>do not forget my words or turn from them.<br>
<sup>6</sup> Do not forsake wisdom, and she will preserve you;<br>love her, and she will guard you.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Wisdom is supreme; so acquire wisdom.<br>And whatever you may acquire,<sup>a</sup> gain understanding.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Prize her, and she will exalt you;<br>if you embrace her, she will honor you.<br>
<sup>9</sup> She will set a garland of grace on your head;<br>she will present you with a crown of beauty.”

<sup>10</sup> Listen, my son, and receive my words,<br>and the years of your life will be many.<br>
<sup>11</sup> I will guide you in the way of wisdom;<br>I will lead you on straight paths.<br>
<sup>12</sup> When you walk, your steps will not be impeded;<br>when you run, you will not stumble.<br>
<sup>13</sup> Hold on to instruction; do not let go.<br>Guard it, for it is your life.<br>
<sup>14</sup> Do not set foot on the path of the wicked<br>or walk in the way of evildoers.<br>
<sup>15</sup> Avoid it; do not travel on it.<br>Turn from it and pass on by.<br>
<sup>16</sup> For they cannot sleep<br>unless they do evil;<br>they are deprived of slumber<br>until they make someone fall.<br>
<sup>17</sup> For they eat the bread of wickedness<br>and drink the wine of violence.<br>
<sup>18</sup> The path of the righteous is like the first gleam of dawn,<br>shining brighter and brighter until midday.<br>
<sup>19</sup> But the way of the wicked is like the darkest gloom;<br>they do not know what makes them stumble.

<sup>20</sup> My son, pay attention to my words;<br>incline your ear to my sayings.<br>
<sup>21</sup> Do not lose sight of them;<br>keep them within your heart.<br>
<sup>22</sup> For they are life to those who find them,<br>and health to the whole body.<br>
<sup>23</sup> Guard your heart with all diligence,<br>for from it flow springs of life.<br>
<sup>24</sup> Put away deception from your mouth;<br>keep your lips from perverse speech.<br>
<sup>25</sup> Let your eyes look forward;<br>fix your gaze straight ahead.<br>
<sup>26</sup> Make a level path for your feet,<sup>b</sup><br>and all your ways will be sure.<br>
<sup>27</sup> Do not swerve to the right or to the left;<br>turn your feet away from evil.

> - <sup>a</sup> 7. Or _And whatever else you acquire_
> - <sup>b</sup> 26. Or _Ponder the path for your feet_; LXX _Make straight paths for your feet_; cited in Hebrews 12:13

## Chapter 5

### Avoiding Immorality<sup>a</sup>

<sup>1</sup> My son, pay attention to my wisdom;<br>incline your ear to my insight,<br>
<sup>2</sup> that you may maintain discretion<br>and your lips may preserve knowledge.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Though the lips of the forbidden woman<sup>b</sup> drip honey<br>and her speech<sup>c</sup> is smoother than oil,<br>
<sup>4</sup> in the end she is bitter as wormwood,<br>sharp as a double-edged sword.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Her feet go down to death;<br>her steps lead straight to Sheol.<sup>d</sup><br>
<sup>6</sup> She does not consider the path of life;<br>she does not know that her ways are unstable.

<sup>7</sup> So now, my sons, listen to me,<br>and do not turn aside from the words of my mouth.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Keep your path far from her;<br>do not go near the door of her house,<br>
<sup>9</sup> lest you concede your vigor to others,<br>and your years to one who is cruel;<br>
<sup>10</sup> lest strangers feast on your wealth,<br>and your labors enrich the house of a foreigner.<br>
<sup>11</sup> At the end of your life you will groan<br>when your flesh and your body are spent,<br>
<sup>12</sup> and you will say, “How I hated discipline,<br>and my heart despised reproof!<br>
<sup>13</sup> I did not listen to the voice of my teachers<br>or incline my ear to my mentors.<br>
<sup>14</sup> I am on the brink of utter ruin<br>in the midst of the whole assembly.”

<sup>15</sup> Drink water from your own cistern,<br>and running water from your own well.<br>
<sup>16</sup> Why should your springs flow in the streets,<br>your streams of water in the public squares?<br>
<sup>17</sup> Let them be yours alone,<br>never to be shared with strangers.<br>
<sup>18</sup> May your fountain be blessed,<br>and may you rejoice in the wife of your youth:<br>
<sup>19</sup> A loving doe, a graceful fawn—<br>may her breasts satisfy you always;<br>may you be captivated<sup>e</sup> by her love forever.<br>
<sup>20</sup> Why be captivated, my son, by an adulteress,<br>or embrace the bosom of a stranger?<sup>f</sup><br>
<sup>21</sup> For a man’s ways are before the eyes of the LORD,<br>and the LORD examines<sup>g</sup> all his paths.<br>
<sup>22</sup> The iniquities of a wicked man entrap him;<br>the cords of his sin entangle him.<br>
<sup>23</sup> He dies for lack of discipline,<br>led astray by his own great folly.

> - <sup>a</sup> Heading. Leviticus 20:10–21; 1 Corinthians 5:1–8
> - <sup>b</sup> 3. Or _the adulteress_
> - <sup>c</sup> 3. Or _her palate_
> - <sup>d</sup> 5. Or _lay hold of Sheol_
> - <sup>e</sup> 19. Or _be led astray_; also in verse 20
> - <sup>f</sup> 20. Or _a foreign woman_ or _another man’s wife_
> - <sup>g</sup> 21. Or _ponders_ or _makes level_

## Chapter 6

### Warnings against Foolishness

<sup>1</sup> My son, if you have put up security for your neighbor,<br>if you have struck hands in pledge with a stranger,<br>
<sup>2</sup> if you have been trapped by the words of your lips,<br>ensnared by the words of your mouth,<br>
<sup>3</sup> then do this, my son, to free yourself,<br>for you have fallen into your neighbor’s hands:<br>Go, humble yourself,<sup>a</sup><br>and press your plea with your neighbor.<br>
<sup>4</sup> Allow no sleep to your eyes<br>or slumber to your eyelids.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Free yourself, like a gazelle from the hand of the hunter,<sup>b</sup><br>like a bird from the snare of the fowler.

<sup>6</sup> Walk in the manner of the ant, O slacker;<br>observe its ways and become wise.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Without a commander,<br>without an overseer or ruler,<br>
<sup>8</sup> it prepares its provisions in summer;<br>it gathers its food at harvest.<br>
<sup>9</sup> How long will you lie there, O slacker?<br>When will you get up from your sleep?<br>
<sup>10</sup> A little sleep, a little slumber,<br>a little folding of the hands to rest,<br>
<sup>11</sup> and poverty will come upon you like a robber,<br>and need like a bandit.

<sup>12</sup> A worthless person, a wicked man,<br>walks with a perverse mouth,<br>
<sup>13</sup> winking his eyes, speaking with his feet,<br>and pointing with his fingers.<br>
<sup>14</sup> With deceit in his heart he devises evil;<br>he continually sows discord.<br>
<sup>15</sup> Therefore calamity will come upon him suddenly;<br>in an instant he will be shattered beyond recovery.

<sup>16</sup> There are six things that the LORD hates, seven that are detestable to Him:<br>
<sup>17</sup> haughty eyes,<br>a lying tongue,<br>hands that shed innocent blood,<br>
<sup>18</sup> a heart that devises wicked schemes,<br>feet that run swiftly to evil,<br>
<sup>19</sup> a false witness who gives false testimony,<br>and one who stirs up discord among brothers.

### Warnings against Adultery

<sup>20</sup> My son, keep your father’s commandment,<br>and do not forsake your mother’s teaching.<br>
<sup>21</sup> Bind them always upon your heart;<br>tie them around your neck.<br>
<sup>22</sup> When you walk, they will guide you;<br>when you lie down, they will watch over you;<br>when you awake, they will speak to you.<br>
<sup>23</sup> For this commandment is a lamp, this teaching is a light,<br>and the reproofs of discipline are the way to life,<br>
<sup>24</sup> to keep you from the evil woman,<br>from the smooth tongue of the adulteress.<sup>c</sup>

<sup>25</sup> Do not lust in your heart for her beauty<br>or let her captivate you with her eyes.<sup>d</sup><br>
<sup>26</sup> For the levy of the prostitute is poverty,<sup>e</sup><br>and the adulteress preys upon your very life.<br>
<sup>27</sup> Can a man embrace fire<br>and his clothes not be burned?<br>
<sup>28</sup> Can a man walk on hot coals<br>without scorching his feet?<br>
<sup>29</sup> So is he who sleeps with another man’s wife;<br>no one who touches her will go unpunished.

<sup>30</sup> Men do not despise the thief<br>if he steals to satisfy his hunger.<br>
<sup>31</sup> Yet if caught, he must pay sevenfold;<br>he must give up all the wealth of his house.<br>
<sup>32</sup> He who commits adultery lacks judgment;<br>whoever does so destroys himself.<br>
<sup>33</sup> Wounds and dishonor will befall him,<br>and his reproach will never be wiped away.<br>
<sup>34</sup> For jealousy enrages a husband,<br>and he will show no mercy in the day of vengeance.<br>
<sup>35</sup> He will not be appeased by any ransom,<br>or persuaded by lavish gifts.

> - <sup>a</sup> 3. Or _hasten_
> - <sup>b</sup> 5. Hebrew does not include _of the hunter_.
> - <sup>c</sup> 24. Or _the stranger_
> - <sup>d</sup> 25. Or _eyelids_ or _eyelashes_
> - <sup>e</sup> 26. Literally _a crust of bread_

## Chapter 7

### Warnings about the Adulteress

<sup>1</sup> My son, keep my words<br>and treasure my commandments within you.<br>
<sup>2</sup> Keep my commandments and live;<br>guard my teachings as the apple<sup>a</sup> of your eye.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Tie them to your fingers;<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.<br>
<sup>4</sup> Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,”<br>and call understanding your kinsman,<br>
<sup>5</sup> that they may keep you from the adulteress,<br>from the stranger with seductive words.

<sup>6</sup> For at the window of my house<br>I looked through the lattice.<br>
<sup>7</sup> I saw among the simple,<br>I noticed among the youths,<br>a young man lacking judgment,<br>
<sup>8</sup> crossing the street near her corner,<br>strolling down the road to her house,<br>
<sup>9</sup> at twilight, as the day was fading<br>into the dark of the night.

<sup>10</sup> Then a woman came out to meet him,<br>with the attire of a harlot and cunning of heart.<br>
<sup>11</sup> She is loud and defiant;<br>her feet do not remain at home.<br>
<sup>12</sup> Now in the street, now in the squares,<br>she lurks at every corner.<br>
<sup>13</sup> She seizes him and kisses him;<br>she brazenly says to him:

<sup>14</sup> “I have made my peace offerings;<br>today I have paid my vows.<br>
<sup>15</sup> So I came out to meet you;<br>I sought you, and I have found you.<br>
<sup>16</sup> I have decked my bed with coverings,<br>with colored linen from Egypt.<br>
<sup>17</sup> I have perfumed my bed with myrrh,<br>with aloes, and with cinnamon.<br>
<sup>18</sup> Come, let us take our fill of love till morning.<br>Let us delight in loving caresses!<br>
<sup>19</sup> For my husband is not at home;<br>he has gone on a long journey.<br>
<sup>20</sup> He took with him a bag of money<br>and will not return till the moon is full.”

<sup>21</sup> With her great persuasion she entices him;<br>with her flattering lips she lures him.<br>
<sup>22</sup> He follows her on impulse,<br>like an ox going to the slaughter,<br>like a deer bounding into a trap,<sup>b</sup><br>
<sup>23</sup> until an arrow pierces his liver,<br>like a bird darting into a snare—<br>not knowing it will cost him his life.

<sup>24</sup> Now, my sons, listen to me,<br>and attend to the words of my mouth.<br>
<sup>25</sup> Do not let your heart turn aside to her ways;<br>do not stray into her paths.<br>
<sup>26</sup> For she has brought many down to death;<br>her slain are many in number.<br>
<sup>27</sup> Her house is the road to Sheol,<br>descending to the chambers of death.

> - <sup>a</sup> 2. Literally _the pupil_
> - <sup>b</sup> 22. Probable reading (see LXX, Vulgate, Syriac); Hebrew _like fetters to discipline a fool_

## Chapter 8

### The Excellence of Wisdom

<sup>1</sup> Does not wisdom call out,<br>and understanding raise her voice?<br>
<sup>2</sup> On the heights overlooking the road,<br>at the crossroads she takes her stand.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Beside the gates to the city,<br>at the entrances she cries out:

<sup>4</sup> “To you, O men, I call out,<br>and my cry is to the sons of men.<br>
<sup>5</sup> O simple ones, learn to be shrewd;<br>O fools, gain understanding.<sup>a</sup><br>
<sup>6</sup> Listen, for I speak of noble things,<br>and the opening of my lips will reveal right.

<sup>7</sup> For my mouth will speak the truth,<br>and wickedness is detestable to my lips.<br>
<sup>8</sup> All the words of my mouth are righteous;<br>none are crooked or perverse.<br>
<sup>9</sup> They are all plain to the discerning,<br>and upright to those who find knowledge.<br>
<sup>10</sup> Receive my instruction instead of silver,<br>and knowledge rather than pure gold.<br>
<sup>11</sup> For wisdom is more precious than rubies,<br>and nothing you desire compares with her.

<sup>12</sup> I, wisdom, dwell together with prudence,<br>and I find knowledge and discretion.<br>
<sup>13</sup> To fear the LORD is to hate evil;<br>I hate arrogant pride, evil conduct, and perverse speech.<br>
<sup>14</sup> Counsel and sound judgment are mine;<br>I have insight and strength.<br>
<sup>15</sup> By me kings reign,<br>and rulers enact just laws;<br>
<sup>16</sup> By me princes rule,<br>and all nobles who govern justly.<sup>b</sup>

<sup>17</sup> I love those who love me,<br>and those who seek me early shall find me.<br>
<sup>18</sup> With me are riches and honor,<br>enduring wealth and righteousness.<br>
<sup>19</sup> My fruit is better than gold, pure gold,<br>and my harvest surpasses choice silver.<br>
