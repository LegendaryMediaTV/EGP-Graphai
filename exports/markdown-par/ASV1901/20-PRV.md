## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel:<br>
<sup>2</sup> To know wisdom and instruction;<br>To discern the words of understanding;<br>
<sup>3</sup> To receive instruction in wise dealing,<br>In righteousness and justice and equity;<br>
<sup>4</sup> To give prudence to the simple,<br>To the young man knowledge and discretion:<br>
<sup>5</sup> That the wise man may hear, and increase in learning;<br>And that the man of understanding may attain unto sound counsels:<br>
<sup>6</sup> To understand a proverb, and <sup>a</sup>a figure,<br>The words of the wise, and their <sup>b</sup>dark sayings.<br>
<sup>7</sup> The fear of Jehovah is the <sup>c</sup>beginning of knowledge;<br>_But_ the foolish despise wisdom and instruction.<br>
<sup>8</sup> My son, hear the instruction of thy father,<br>And forsake not the <sup>d</sup>law of thy mother:<br>
<sup>9</sup> For they shall be a chaplet of grace unto thy head,<br>And chains about thy neck.<br>
<sup>10</sup> My son, if sinners entice thee,<br>Consent thou not.<br>
<sup>11</sup> If they say, Come with us,<br>Let us lay wait for blood;<br>Let us lurk privily for the innocent without cause;<br>
<sup>12</sup> Let us swallow them up alive as Sheol,<sup>e</sup><br>And whole, as those that go down into the pit;<br>
<sup>13</sup> We shall find all precious substance;<br>We shall fill our houses with spoil;<br>
<sup>14</sup> <sup>f</sup>Thou shalt cast thy lot among us;<br>We will all have one purse:<br>
<sup>15</sup> My son, walk not thou in the way with them;<br>Refrain thy foot from their path:<br>
<sup>16</sup> For their feet run to evil,<br>And they make haste to shed blood.<br>
<sup>17</sup> For in vain is the net spread<br>In the sight of any bird:<br>
<sup>18</sup> And these lay wait for their own blood;<br>They lurk privily for their own lives.<br>
<sup>19</sup> So are the ways of every one that is greedy of gain;<br>It taketh away the life of the owners thereof.<br>
<sup>20</sup> Wisdom crieth aloud in the street;<br>She uttereth her voice in the broad places;<br>
<sup>21</sup> She crieth <sup>g</sup>in the chief place of concourse;<br>At the entrance of the gates,<br>In the city, she uttereth her words:<br>
<sup>22</sup> How long, ye simple ones, will ye love simplicity?<br>And scoffers delight them in scoffing,<br>And fools hate knowledge?<br>
<sup>23</sup> Turn you at my reproof:<br>Behold, I will pour out my spirit upon you;<br>I will make known my words unto you.<br>
<sup>24</sup> Because I have called, and ye have refused;<br>I have stretched out my hand, and no man hath regarded;<br>
<sup>25</sup> But ye have set at nought all my counsel,<br>And would none of my reproof:<br>
<sup>26</sup> I also will laugh in _the day of_ your calamity;<br>I will mock when your fear cometh;<br>
<sup>27</sup> When your fear cometh as <sup>h</sup>a storm,<br>And your calamity cometh on as a whirlwind;<br>When distress and anguish come upon you.<br>
<sup>28</sup> Then will they call upon me, but I will not answer;<br>They will seek me <sup>i</sup>diligently, but they shall not find me:<br>
<sup>29</sup> For that they hated knowledge,<br>And did not choose the fear of Jehovah,<br>
<sup>30</sup> They would none of my counsel,<br>They despised all my reproof.<br>
<sup>31</sup> Therefore shall they eat of the fruit of their own way,<br>And be filled with their own devices.<br>
<sup>32</sup> For the backsliding of the simple shall slay them,<br>And the careless ease of fools shall destroy them.<br>
<sup>33</sup> But whoso hearkeneth unto me shall dwell securely,<br>And shall be quiet without fear of evil.<br>

## Chapter 2

<sup>2</sup> So as to incline thine ear unto wisdom,<br>And apply thy heart to understanding;<br>
<sup>3</sup> Yea, if thou cry after discernment,<br>And lift up thy voice for understanding;<br>
<sup>4</sup> If thou seek her as silver,<br>And search for her as for hid treasures:<br>
<sup>5</sup> Then shalt thou understand the fear of Jehovah,<br>And find the knowledge of God.<br>
<sup>6</sup> For Jehovah giveth wisdom;<br>Out of his mouth _cometh_ knowledge and understanding:<br>
<sup>7</sup> He layeth up sound wisdom for the upright;<sup>a</sup><br>_He is_ a shield to them that walk in integrity;<br>
<sup>8</sup> <sup>b</sup>That he may guard the paths of justice,<sup>c</sup><br>And preserve the way of his saints.<br>

<sup>10</sup> For wisdom shall enter into thy heart,<br>And knowledge shall be pleasant unto thy soul;<br>
<sup>11</sup> Discretion shall watch over thee;<br>Understanding shall keep thee:<br>
<sup>12</sup> To deliver thee from the way of <sup>d</sup>evil,<br>From the men that speak perverse things;<br>
<sup>13</sup> Who forsake the paths of uprightness,<br>To walk in the ways of darkness;<br>
<sup>14</sup> Who rejoice to do evil,<br>And delight in the perverseness of <sup>e</sup>evil;<br>
<sup>15</sup> Who are crooked in their ways,<br>And wayward in their paths:<br>
<sup>16</sup> To deliver thee from the strange woman,<br>Even from the foreigner that <sup>f</sup>flattereth with her words;<br>
<sup>17</sup> That forsaketh the <sup>g</sup>friend of her youth,<br>And forgetteth the covenant of her God:<br>
<sup>18</sup> For <sup>h</sup>her house inclineth unto death,<br>And her paths unto <sup>i</sup>the dead;<br>
<sup>19</sup> None that go unto her return again,<br>Neither do they attain unto the paths of life:<br>

<sup>21</sup> For the upright shall dwell in the <sup>j</sup>land,<br>And the perfect shall remain in it.<br>
<sup>22</sup> But the wicked shall be cut off from the land,<sup>k</sup><br>And the treacherous shall be rooted out of it.<br>

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> My son, forget not my <sup>a</sup>law;<br>But let thy heart keep my commandments:<br>
<sup>2</sup> For length of days, and years of life,<br>And peace, will they add to thee.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Let not kindness and truth forsake thee:<br>Bind them about thy neck;<br>Write them upon the tablet of thy heart:<br>
<sup>4</sup> So shalt thou find favor and <sup>b</sup>good understanding<br>In the sight of God and man.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Trust in Jehovah with all thy heart,<br>And lean not upon thine own understanding:<br>
<sup>6</sup> In all thy ways acknowledge him,<br>And he will <sup>c</sup>direct thy paths.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Be not wise in thine own eyes;<br>Fear Jehovah, and depart from evil:<br>
<sup>8</sup> It will be health to thy navel,<br>And <sup>d</sup>marrow to thy bones.<br>
<sup>9</sup> Honor Jehovah with thy substance,<br>And with the first-fruits of all thine increase:<br>
<sup>10</sup> So shall thy barns be filled with plenty,<br>And thy vats shall overflow with new wine.<br>
<sup>11</sup> My son, despise not the <sup>e</sup>chastening of Jehovah;<br>Neither be weary of his reproof:<br>
<sup>12</sup> For whom Jehovah loveth he reproveth,<br>Even as a father the son in whom he delighteth.<br>
<sup>13</sup> Happy is the man that findeth wisdom,<br>And the man that <sup>f</sup>getteth understanding.<br>
<sup>14</sup> For the gaining of it is better than the gaining of silver,<br>And the profit thereof than fine gold.<br>
<sup>15</sup> She is more precious than rubies:<br>And none of the things thou canst desire are to be compared unto her.<br>
<sup>16</sup> Length of days is in her right hand;<br>In her left hand are riches and honor.<br>
<sup>17</sup> Her ways are ways of pleasantness,<br>And all her paths are peace.<br>
<sup>18</sup> She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her:<br>And happy is every one that retaineth her.<br>
<sup>19</sup> Jehovah by wisdom founded the earth;<br>By understanding he established the heavens.<br>
<sup>20</sup> By his knowledge the depths were broken up,<br>And the skies drop down the dew.<br>
<sup>21</sup> My son, let them not depart from thine eyes;<br>Keep sound wisdom and discretion:<br>
<sup>22</sup> So shall they be life unto thy soul,<br>And grace to thy neck.<br>
<sup>23</sup> Then shalt thou walk in thy way securely,<br>And <sup>g</sup>thy foot shall not stumble.<br>
<sup>24</sup> When thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid:<br>Yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy sleep shall be sweet.<br>
<sup>25</sup> Be not afraid of sudden fear,<br>Neither of the <sup>h</sup>desolation of the wicked, when it cometh:<br>
<sup>26</sup> For Jehovah will be thy confidence,<br>And will keep thy foot from being taken.<br>
<sup>27</sup> Withhold not good from them to whom it is due,<br>When it is in the power of thy hand to do it.<br>
<sup>28</sup> Say not unto thy neighbor, Go, and come again,<br>And to-morrow I will give;<br>When thou hast it by thee.<br>
<sup>29</sup> Devise not evil against thy neighbor,<br>Seeing he dwelleth securely by thee.<br>
<sup>30</sup> Strive not with a man without cause,<br>If he have done thee no harm.<br>
<sup>31</sup> Envy thou not the man of violence,<br>And choose none of his ways.<br>
<sup>32</sup> For the perverse is an abomination to Jehovah;<br>But his <sup>i</sup>friendship is with the upright.<br>
<sup>33</sup> The curse of Jehovah is in the house of the wicked;<br>But he blesseth the habitation of the righteous.<br>
<sup>34</sup> <sup>j</sup>Surely he scoffeth at the scoffers;<sup>k</sup><br>But he giveth grace unto the lowly.<br>
<sup>35</sup> The wise shall inherit glory;<br>But <sup>l</sup>shame shall be the promotion of fools.<br>

## Chapter 4

<sup>1</sup> Hear, _my_ sons, the instruction of a father,<br>And attend to know understanding:<br>
<sup>2</sup> For I give you good doctrine;<br>Forsake ye not my <sup>a</sup>law.<br>
<sup>3</sup> For I was a son unto my father,<br>Tender and <sup>b</sup>only beloved in the sight of my mother.<br>
<sup>4</sup> And he taught me, and said unto me:<br>Let thy heart retain my words;<br>Keep my commandments, and live;<br>
<sup>5</sup> Get wisdom, get understanding;<br>Forget not, neither decline from the words of my mouth;<br>
<sup>6</sup> Forsake her not, and she will preserve thee;<br>Love her, and she will keep thee.<br>
<sup>7</sup> <sup>c</sup>Wisdom _is_ the principal thing; _therefore_ get wisdom;<br>Yea, with all <sup>d</sup>thy getting get understanding.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Exalt her, and she will promote thee;<br>She will bring thee to honor, when thou dost embrace her.<br>
<sup>9</sup> She will give to thy head a chaplet of grace;<br>A crown of <sup>e</sup>beauty will she deliver to thee.<br>
<sup>10</sup> Hear, O my son, and receive my sayings;<br>And the years of thy life shall be many.<br>
<sup>11</sup> I have taught thee in the way of wisdom;<br>I have led thee in paths of uprightness.<br>
<sup>12</sup> When thou goest, thy steps shall not be straitened;<br>And if thou runnest, thou shalt not stumble.<br>
<sup>13</sup> Take fast hold of instruction; let her not go:<br>Keep her; for she is thy life.<br>
<sup>14</sup> Enter not into the path of the wicked,<br>And walk not in the way of evil men.<br>
<sup>15</sup> Avoid it, pass not by it;<br>Turn from it, and pass on.<br>
<sup>16</sup> For they sleep not, except they do evil;<br>And their sleep is taken away, unless they cause some to fall.<br>

<sup>19</sup> The way of the wicked is as darkness:<br>They know not at what they stumble.<br>
<sup>20</sup> My son, attend to my words;<br>Incline thine ear unto my sayings.<br>
<sup>21</sup> Let them not depart from thine eyes;<br>Keep them in the midst of thy heart.<br>
<sup>22</sup> For they are life unto those that find them,<br>And health to all their flesh.<br>
<sup>23</sup> Keep thy heart <sup>f</sup>with all diligence;<br>For out of it are the issues of life.<br>
<sup>24</sup> Put away from thee a wayward mouth,<br>And perverse lips put far from thee.<br>
<sup>25</sup> Let thine eyes look right on,<br>And let thine eyelids look straight before thee.<br>
<sup>26</sup> <sup>g</sup>Make level the path of thy feet,<br>And let all thy ways be <sup>h</sup>established.<br>
<sup>27</sup> Turn not to the right hand nor to the left:<br>Remove thy foot from evil.<br>

## Chapter 5

<sup>1</sup> My son, attend unto my wisdom;<br>Incline thine ear to my understanding:<br>
<sup>2</sup> That thou mayest preserve discretion,<br>And that thy lips may keep knowledge.<br>
<sup>3</sup> For the lips of a strange woman drop honey,<br>And her mouth is smoother than oil:<br>
<sup>4</sup> But in the end she is bitter as wormwood,<br>Sharp as a two-edged sword.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Her feet go down to death;<br>Her steps take hold on Sheol;<br>
<sup>6</sup> <sup>a</sup>So that she findeth not the level path of life:<br>Her ways are unstable, _and_<sup>b</sup> she knoweth _it_ not.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Now therefore, _my_ sons, hearken unto me,<br>And depart not from the words of my mouth.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Remove thy way far from her,<br>And come not nigh the door of her house;<br>
<sup>9</sup> Lest thou give thine honor unto others,<br>And thy years unto the cruel;<br>
<sup>10</sup> Lest strangers be filled with thy <sup>c</sup>strength,<br>And thy labors _be_ in the house of an alien,<br>
<sup>11</sup> And thou <sup>d</sup>mourn at thy latter end,<br>When thy flesh and thy body are consumed,<br>
<sup>12</sup> And say, How have I hated instruction,<br>And my heart despised reproof;<br>
<sup>13</sup> Neither have I obeyed the voice of my teachers,<br>Nor inclined mine ear to them that instructed me!<br>
<sup>14</sup> I was well-nigh in all evil<br>In the midst of the assembly and congregation.<br>
<sup>15</sup> Drink waters out of thine own cistern,<br>And running waters out of thine own well.<br>
<sup>16</sup> <sup>e</sup>Should thy springs be dispersed abroad,<br>And streams of water in the streets?<br>
<sup>17</sup> Let them be for thyself alone,<br>And not for strangers with thee.<br>
<sup>18</sup> Let thy fountain be blessed;<br>And rejoice in the wife of thy youth.<br>
<sup>19</sup> _As_ a loving hind and a pleasant doe,<br>Let her breasts satisfy thee at all times;<br>And <sup>f</sup>be thou ravished always with her love.<br>
<sup>20</sup> For why shouldest thou, my son, <sup>g</sup>be ravished with a strange woman,<br>And embrace the bosom of a foreigner?<br>
<sup>21</sup> For the ways of man are before the eyes of Jehovah;<br>And he <sup>h</sup>maketh level all his paths.<br>
<sup>22</sup> His own iniquities shall take the wicked,<br>And he shall be holden with the cords of his sin.<br>
<sup>23</sup> He shall die for lack of instruction;<br>And in the greatness of his folly he shall go astray.<br>

## Chapter 30

<sup>1</sup> The words of Agur the son of <sup>a</sup>Jakeh; the <sup>b</sup>oracle.<br>The man saith <sup>c</sup>unto Ithiel, unto Ithiel and Ucal:<br>
<sup>2</sup> Surely I am more brutish than any man,<br>And have not the understanding of a man;<br>
<sup>3</sup> And I have not learned wisdom,<sup>d</sup><br>Neither have I the knowledge of the Holy One.<br>
<sup>4</sup> Who hath ascended up into heaven, and descended?<br>Who hath gathered the wind in his fists?<br>Who hath bound the waters in his garment?<br>Who hath established all the ends of the earth?<br>What is his name, and what is his son’s name, if thou knowest?<br>
<sup>5</sup> Every word of God is <sup>e</sup>tried:<br>He is a shield unto them that take refuge in him.<br>
<sup>6</sup> Add thou not unto his words,<br>Lest he reprove thee, and thou be found a liar.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Two things have I asked of thee;<br>Deny me _them_ not before I die:<br>
<sup>8</sup> Remove far from me falsehood and lies;<br>Give me neither poverty nor riches;<br>Feed me with <sup>f</sup>the food that is needful for me:<br>
<sup>9</sup> Lest I be full, and deny _thee_, and say, Who is Jehovah?<br>Or lest I be poor, and steal,<br>And <sup>g</sup>use profanely the name of my God.<br>
<sup>10</sup> Slander not a servant unto his master,<br>Lest he curse thee, and thou be held guilty.<br>
<sup>11</sup> There is a generation that curse their father,<br>And bless not their mother.<br>
<sup>12</sup> There is a generation that are pure in their own eyes,<br>And _yet_ are not washed from their filthiness.<br>
<sup>13</sup> There is a generation, oh how lofty are their eyes!<br>And their eyelids are lifted up.<br>
<sup>14</sup> There is a generation whose teeth are _as_ swords, and their jaw teeth _as_ knives,<br>To devour the poor from off the earth, and the needy from among men.<br>
<sup>15</sup> The <sup>h</sup>horseleach hath two daughters, <sup>i</sup>_crying_, Give, give.<br>There are three things that are never satisfied,<br>_Yea_, four that say not, Enough:<br>
<sup>16</sup> Sheol; and the barren womb;<br>The earth that is not satisfied with water;<br>And the fire that saith not, Enough.<br>
<sup>17</sup> The eye that mocketh at his father,<br>And despiseth to obey his mother,<br>The ravens of <sup>j</sup>the valley shall pick it out,<br>And the <sup>k</sup>young eagles shall eat it.<br>
<sup>18</sup> There are three things which are too wonderful for me,<br>Yea, four which I know not:<br>
<sup>19</sup> The way of an eagle in the air;<br>The way of a serpent upon a rock;<br>The way of a ship in the midst of the sea;<br>And the way of a man with a maiden.<br>
<sup>20</sup> So is the way of an adulterous woman;<br>She eateth, and wipeth her mouth,<br>And saith, I have done no wickedness.<br>
<sup>21</sup> <sup>l</sup>For three things the earth doth tremble,<br>And for four, _which_ it cannot bear:<br>
<sup>22</sup> For a servant when he is king;<br>And a fool when he is filled with food;<br>
<sup>23</sup> For an odious woman when she is married;<br>And a handmaid that is heir to her mistress.<br>
<sup>24</sup> There are four things which are little upon the earth,<br>But they are exceeding wise:<br>
<sup>25</sup> The ants are a people not strong,<br>Yet they provide their food in the summer;<br>
<sup>26</sup> The conies are but a feeble folk,<br>Yet make they their houses in the rocks;<br>
<sup>27</sup> The locusts have no king,<br>Yet go they forth all of them by bands;<br>
<sup>28</sup> The lizard <sup>m</sup>taketh hold with her hands,<br>Yet is she in kings’ palaces.<br>
<sup>29</sup> There are three things which are stately in their march,<br>Yea, four which are stately in going:<br>
<sup>30</sup> The lion, which is mightiest among beasts,<br>And turneth not away for any;<br>
<sup>31</sup> The <sup>n</sup>greyhound; the he-goat also;<br>And the king <sup>o</sup>against whom there is no rising up.<br>
<sup>32</sup> If thou hast done foolishly in lifting up thyself,<br>Or if thou hast thought evil,<br>_Lay_ thy hand upon thy mouth.<br>
<sup>33</sup> For the <sup>p</sup>churning of milk bringeth forth butter,<br>And the <sup>q</sup>wringing of the nose bringeth forth blood;<br>So the <sup>r</sup>forcing of wrath bringeth forth strife.<br>

## Chapter 31

<sup>1</sup> <sup>a</sup>The words of king Lemuel; the <sup>b</sup>oracle which his mother taught him.<br>
<sup>2</sup> What, my son? and what, O son of my womb?<br>And what, O son of my vows?<br>
<sup>3</sup> Give not thy strength unto women,<br>Nor thy ways to <sup>c</sup>that which destroyeth kings.<br>
<sup>4</sup> It is not for kings, O Lemuel, it is not for kings to drink wine;<br>Nor for princes <sup>d</sup>_to say_, Where is strong drink?<br>
<sup>5</sup> Lest they drink, and forget <sup>e</sup>the law,<br>And pervert the justice _due_ to <sup>f</sup>any that is afflicted.<br>
<sup>6</sup> Give strong drink unto him that is ready to perish,<br>And wine unto the bitter in soul:<br>
<sup>7</sup> Let him drink, and forget his poverty,<br>And remember his misery no more.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Open thy mouth for the dumb,<br>In the cause of all such as are <sup>g</sup>left desolate.<br>
<sup>9</sup> Open thy mouth, judge righteously,<br>And minister justice to the poor and needy.<br>
<sup>10</sup> A worthy woman who can find?<br>For her price is far above rubies.<br>
<sup>11</sup> The heart of her husband trusteth in her,<br>And he shall have no lack of <sup>h</sup>gain.<br>
<sup>12</sup> She doeth him good and not evil<br>All the days of her life.<br>
<sup>13</sup> She seeketh wool and flax,<br>And worketh <sup>i</sup>willingly with her hands.<br>
<sup>14</sup> She is like the merchant-ships;<br>She bringeth her bread from afar.<br>
<sup>15</sup> She riseth also while it is yet night,<br>And giveth food to her household,<br>And their <sup>j</sup>task to her maidens.<br>
<sup>16</sup> She considereth a field, and buyeth it;<br>With the fruit of her hands she planteth a vineyard.<br>
<sup>17</sup> She girdeth her loins with strength,<br>And maketh strong her arms.<br>
<sup>18</sup> She perceiveth that her merchandise is profitable;<br>Her lamp goeth not out by night.<br>
<sup>19</sup> She layeth her hands to the distaff,<br>And her <sup>k</sup>hands hold the spindle.<br>
<sup>20</sup> She stretcheth out her <sup>l</sup>hand to the poor;<br>Yea, she reacheth forth her hands to the needy.<br>
<sup>21</sup> She is not afraid of the snow for her household;<br>For all her household are clothed with scarlet.<br>
<sup>22</sup> She maketh for herself <sup>m</sup>carpets of tapestry;<br>Her clothing is fine linen and purple.<br>
<sup>23</sup> Her husband is known in the gates,<br>When he sitteth among the elders of the land.<br>
<sup>24</sup> She maketh linen garments and selleth them,<br>And delivereth girdles unto the <sup>n</sup>merchant.<br>
<sup>25</sup> Strength and dignity are her clothing;<br>And she laugheth at the time to come.<br>
<sup>26</sup> She openeth her mouth with wisdom;<br>And the <sup>o</sup>law of kindness is on her tongue.<br>
<sup>27</sup> She looketh well to the ways of her household,<br>And eateth not the bread of idleness.<br>
<sup>28</sup> Her children rise up, and call her blessed;<br>Her husband _also_, and he praiseth her, _saying_:<br>
<sup>29</sup> Many daughters have done worthily,<br>But thou excellest them all.<br>
<sup>30</sup> Grace is deceitful, and beauty is vain;<br>_But_ a woman that feareth Jehovah, she shall be praised.<br>
<sup>31</sup> Give her of the fruit of her hands;<br>And let her works praise her in the gates.<br>
