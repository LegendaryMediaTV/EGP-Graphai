## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> How doth the city sit solitary, that was full of people!<br>She is become as a widow, that was great among the nations!<br>She that was a princess among the provinces is become tributary!<br>
<sup>2</sup> She weepeth sore in the night, and her tears are on her cheeks;<br>Among all her lovers she hath none to comfort her:<br>All her friends have dealt treacherously with her; they are become her enemies.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Judah is gone into <sup>a</sup>captivity because of affliction, and because of great servitude;<br>She dwelleth among the nations, she findeth no rest:<br>All her persecutors overtook her within the straits.<br>
<sup>4</sup> The ways of Zion do mourn, because none come to the <sup>b</sup>solemn assembly;<br>All her gates are desolate, her priests do sigh:<br>Her virgins are afflicted, and she herself is in bitterness.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Her adversaries are become the head, her enemies prosper;<br>For Jehovah hath afflicted her for the multitude of her transgressions:<br>Her young children are gone into captivity before the adversary.<br>
<sup>6</sup> And from the daughter of Zion all her <sup>c</sup>majesty is departed:<br>Her princes are become like harts that find no pasture,<br>And they are gone without strength before the pursuer.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Jerusalem remembereth in the days of her affliction and of her <sup>d</sup>miseries all her pleasant things that were from the days of old:<br>When her people fell into the hand of the adversary, and none did help her,<br>The adversaries saw her, they did mock at her <sup>e</sup>desolations.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Jerusalem hath grievously sinned; therefore she <sup>f</sup>is become as an unclean thing;<br>All that honored her despise her, because they have seen her nakedness:<br>Yea, she sigheth, and turneth backward.<br>
<sup>9</sup> Her filthiness was in her skirts; she remembered not her latter end;<br>Therefore is she come down wonderfully; she hath no comforter:<br>Behold, O Jehovah, my affliction; for the enemy hath magnified himself.<br>
<sup>10</sup> The adversary hath spread out his hand upon all her pleasant things:<br>For she hath seen that the nations are entered into her sanctuary,<br>Concerning whom thou didst command that they should not enter into thine assembly.<br>
<sup>11</sup> All her people sigh, they seek bread;<br>They have given their pleasant things for food to refresh the soul:<br>See, O Jehovah, and behold; for I am become abject.<br>
<sup>12</sup> Is it nothing to you, all ye that pass by?<br>Behold, and see if there be any sorrow like unto my sorrow, which is brought upon me,<sup>g</sup><br>Wherewith Jehovah hath afflicted _me_ in the day of his fierce anger.<br>
<sup>13</sup> From on high hath he sent fire into my bones, and it prevaileth against them;<br>He hath spread a net for my feet, he hath turned me back:<br>He hath made me desolate and faint all the day.<br>
<sup>14</sup> The yoke of my transgressions is bound by his hand;<br>They are knit together, they are come up upon my neck; he hath made my strength to <sup>h</sup>fail:<br>The Lord hath delivered me into their hands, <sup>i</sup>against whom I am not able to stand.<br>
<sup>15</sup> The Lord hath set at nought all my mighty men in the midst of me;<br>He hath called a solemn assembly against me to crush my young men:<br>The Lord hath trodden as in a winepress the virgin daughter of Judah.<br>
<sup>16</sup> For these things I weep; mine eye, mine eye runneth down with water;<br>Because the comforter that should refresh my soul is far from me:<br>My children are desolate, because the enemy hath prevailed.<br>
<sup>17</sup> Zion spreadeth forth her hands; there is none to comfort her;<br>Jehovah hath commanded concerning Jacob, that they that are round about him should be his adversaries:<br>Jerusalem is among them as an unclean thing.<br>
<sup>18</sup> Jehovah is righteous; for I have rebelled against his commandment:<br>Hear, I pray you, all ye peoples, and behold my sorrow:<br>My virgins and my young men are gone into captivity.<br>
<sup>19</sup> I called for my lovers, _but_ they deceived me:<br>My priests and mine elders gave up the ghost in the city,<br>While they sought them food to refresh their souls.<br>
<sup>20</sup> Behold, O Jehovah; for I am in distress; my heart is troubled;<br>My heart is turned within me; for I have grievously rebelled:<br>Abroad the sword bereaveth, at home there is as death.<br>
<sup>21</sup> They have heard that I sigh; there is none to comfort me;<br>All mine enemies have heard of my trouble; they are glad that thou hast done it:<br>Thou wilt bring the day that thou hast proclaimed, and they shall be like unto me.<br>
<sup>22</sup> Let all their wickedness come before thee;<br>And do unto them, as thou hast done unto me for all my transgressions:<br>For my sighs are many, and my heart is faint.<br>

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> How hath the Lord covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud in his anger!<br>He hath cast down from heaven unto the earth the beauty of Israel,<br>And hath not remembered his footstool in the day of his anger.<br>
<sup>2</sup> The Lord hath swallowed up all the habitations of Jacob, and hath not pitied:<br>He hath thrown down in his wrath the strongholds of the daughter of Judah;<br>He hath brought them down to the ground; he hath profaned the kingdom and the princes thereof.<br>
<sup>3</sup> He hath cut off in fierce anger <sup>a</sup>all the horn of Israel;<br>He hath drawn back his right hand from before the enemy:<br>And he hath burned up Jacob like a flaming fire, which devoureth round about.<br>
<sup>4</sup> He hath bent his bow like an enemy, he hath stood with his right hand as an adversary,<br>And hath slain all that were pleasant to the eye:<sup>b</sup><br>In the tent of the daughter of Zion he hath poured out his wrath like fire.<br>
<sup>5</sup> The Lord is become as an enemy, he hath swallowed up Israel;<br>He hath swallowed up all her palaces, he hath destroyed his strongholds;<br>And he hath multiplied in the daughter of Judah mourning and lamentation.<br>
<sup>6</sup> And he hath violently taken away his <sup>c</sup>tabernacle, as if it were of a garden; he hath destroyed his place of assembly:<br>Jehovah hath caused <sup>d</sup>solemn assembly and sabbath to be forgotten in Zion,<br>And hath despised in the indignation of his anger the king and the priest.<br>
<sup>7</sup> The Lord hath cast off his altar, he hath abhorred his sanctuary;<br>He hath given up into the hand of the enemy the walls of her palaces:<br>They have made a noise in the house of Jehovah, as in the day of a solemn assembly.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Jehovah hath purposed to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion;<br>He hath stretched out the line, he hath not withdrawn his hand from <sup>e</sup>destroying;<br>And he hath made the rampart and wall to lament; they languish together.<br>
<sup>9</sup> Her gates are sunk into the ground; he hath destroyed and broken her bars:<br>Her king and her princes are among the nations where the law is not;<br>Yea, her prophets find no vision from Jehovah.<br>
<sup>10</sup> The elders of the daughter of Zion sit upon the ground, they keep silence;<br>They have cast up dust upon their heads; they have girded themselves with sackcloth:<br>The virgins of Jerusalem hang down their heads to the ground.<br>
<sup>11</sup> Mine eyes do fail with tears, my heart is troubled;<br>My liver is poured upon the earth, because of the <sup>f</sup>destruction of the daughter of my people,<br>Because the young children and the sucklings swoon in the streets of the city.<br>
<sup>12</sup> They say to their mothers, Where is grain and wine?<br>When they swoon as the wounded in the streets of the city,<br>When their soul is poured out into their mothers’ bosom.<br>
<sup>13</sup> What shall I <sup>g</sup>testify unto thee? what shall I liken to thee, O daughter of Jerusalem?<br>What shall I compare to thee, that I may comfort thee, O virgin daughter of Zion?<br>For thy breach is great like the sea: who can heal thee?<br>
<sup>14</sup> Thy prophets have seen for thee false and foolish visions;<br>And they have not uncovered thine iniquity, to bring back thy captivity,<br>But have seen for thee false <sup>h</sup>oracles and <sup>i</sup>causes of banishment.<br>
<sup>15</sup> All that pass by clap their hands at thee;<br>They hiss and wag their head at the daughter of Jerusalem, _saying_,<br>Is this the city that men called The perfection of beauty, The joy of the whole earth?<br>
<sup>16</sup> All thine enemies have opened their mouth wide against thee;<br>They hiss and gnash the teeth; they say, We have swallowed her up;<br>Certainly this is the day that we looked for; we have found, we have seen it.<br>
<sup>17</sup> Jehovah hath done that which he purposed; he hath <sup>j</sup>fulfilled his word that he commanded in the days of old;<br>He hath thrown down, and hath not pitied:<br>And he hath caused the enemy to rejoice over thee; he hath exalted the horn of thine adversaries.<br>
<sup>18</sup> Their heart cried unto the Lord:<br>O wall of the daughter of Zion, let tears run down like a river day and night;<br>Give thyself no respite; let not the apple of thine eye cease.<br>
<sup>19</sup> Arise, cry out in the night, at the beginning of the watches;<br>Pour out thy heart like water before the face of the Lord:<br>Lift up thy hands toward him for the life of thy young children, that faint for hunger at the head of every street.<br>
<sup>20</sup> See, O Jehovah, and behold to whom thou hast done thus!<br>Shall the women eat their fruit, the children that are dandled in the hands?<br>Shall the priest and the prophet be slain in the sanctuary of the Lord?<br>
<sup>21</sup> The youth and the old man lie on the ground in the streets;<br>My virgins and my young men are fallen by the sword:<br>Thou hast slain them in the day of thine anger; thou hast slaughtered, _and_ not pitied.<br>
<sup>22</sup> Thou hast called, as in the day of a solemn assembly, my terrors on every side;<br>And there was none that escaped or remained in the day of Jehovah’s anger:<br>Those that I have dandled and brought up hath mine enemy consumed.<br>

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> I am the man that hath seen affliction by the rod of his wrath.<br>
<sup>2</sup> He hath led me and caused me to walk in darkness, <sup>a</sup>and not in light.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Surely against me he turneth his hand again and again all the day.<br>
<sup>4</sup> My flesh and my skin hath he <sup>b</sup>made old; he hath broken my bones.<br>
<sup>5</sup> He hath builded against me, and compassed me with gall and travail.<br>
<sup>6</sup> He hath made me to dwell in dark places, as those that have been long dead.<br>
<sup>7</sup> He hath walled me about, that I cannot go forth; he hath made my chain heavy.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Yea, when I cry, and call for help, he shutteth out my prayer.<br>
<sup>9</sup> He hath walled up my ways with hewn stone; he hath made my paths crooked.<br>
<sup>10</sup> He is unto me as a bear lying in wait, as a lion in secret places.<br>
<sup>11</sup> He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces; he hath made me desolate.<br>
<sup>12</sup> He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow.<br>
<sup>13</sup> He hath caused the <sup>c</sup>shafts of his quiver to enter into my reins.<br>
<sup>14</sup> I am become a derision to all my people, and their song all the day.<br>
<sup>15</sup> He hath filled me with bitterness, he hath sated me with wormwood.<br>
<sup>16</sup> He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones; he hath covered me with ashes.<br>
<sup>17</sup> And thou hast <sup>d</sup>removed my soul far off from peace; I forgat prosperity.<br>
<sup>18</sup> And I said, My strength is perished, and mine expectation from Jehovah.<br>
<sup>19</sup> Remember mine affliction and my <sup>e</sup>misery, the wormwood and the gall.<br>
<sup>20</sup> My soul hath them still in remembrance, and is bowed down within me.<br>
<sup>21</sup> This I recall to my mind; therefore have I hope.<br>
<sup>22</sup> _It is of_ Jehovah’s lovingkindnesses that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not.<br>
<sup>23</sup> They are new every morning; great is thy faithfulness.<br>
<sup>24</sup> Jehovah is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him.<br>
<sup>25</sup> Jehovah is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul that seeketh him.<br>
<sup>26</sup> It is good that a man should hope and quietly wait for the salvation of Jehovah.<br>
<sup>27</sup> It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth.<br>
<sup>28</sup> <sup>f</sup>Let him sit alone and keep silence, because he hath laid it upon him.<br>
<sup>29</sup> Let him put his mouth in the dust, if so be there may be hope.<br>
<sup>30</sup> Let him give his cheek to him that smiteth him; let him be filled full with reproach.<br>
<sup>31</sup> For the Lord will not cast off for ever.<br>
<sup>32</sup> For though he cause grief, yet will he have compassion according to the multitude of his lovingkindnesses.<br>
<sup>33</sup> For he doth not afflict <sup>g</sup>willingly, nor grieve the children of men.<br>
<sup>34</sup> To crush under foot all the prisoners of the earth,<br>
<sup>35</sup> To turn aside the right of a man before the face of the Most High,<br>
<sup>36</sup> To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord <sup>h</sup>approveth not.<br>
<sup>37</sup> Who is he that saith, and it cometh to pass, when the Lord commandeth it not?<br>
<sup>38</sup> Out of the mouth of the Most High cometh there not evil and good?<br>
<sup>39</sup> Wherefore doth a living man complain, <sup>i</sup>a man for the punishment of his sins?<br>
<sup>40</sup> Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to Jehovah.<br>
<sup>41</sup> Let us lift up our heart with our hands unto God in the heavens.<br>
<sup>42</sup> We have transgressed and have rebelled; thou hast not pardoned.<br>
<sup>43</sup> Thou hast <sup>j</sup>covered with anger and pursued us; thou hast slain, thou hast not pitied.<br>
<sup>44</sup> Thou hast covered thyself with a cloud, so that no prayer can pass through.<br>
<sup>45</sup> Thou hast made us an offscouring and refuse in the midst of the peoples.<br>
<sup>46</sup> All our enemies have opened their mouth wide against us.<br>
<sup>47</sup> Fear and the pit are come upon us, <sup>k</sup>devastation and destruction.<br>
<sup>48</sup> Mine eye runneth down with streams of water, for the destruction of the daughter of my people.<br>
<sup>49</sup> Mine eye poureth down, and ceaseth not, without any intermission,<br>
<sup>50</sup> Till Jehovah look down, and behold from heaven.<br>
<sup>51</sup> Mine eye affecteth my soul, because of all the daughters of my city.<br>
<sup>52</sup> They have chased me sore like a bird, they that are mine enemies without cause.<br>
<sup>53</sup> They have cut off my life in the dungeon, and have cast a stone upon me.<br>
<sup>54</sup> Waters flowed over my head; I said, I am cut off.<br>
<sup>55</sup> I called upon thy name, O Jehovah, out of the lowest dungeon.<br>
<sup>56</sup> Thou heardest my voice; hide not thine ear at my breathing, at my cry.<br>
<sup>57</sup> Thou drewest near in the day that I called upon thee; thou saidst, Fear not.<br>
<sup>58</sup> O Lord, thou hast pleaded the causes of my soul; thou hast redeemed my life.<br>
<sup>59</sup> O Jehovah, thou hast seen my wrong; judge thou my cause.<br>
<sup>60</sup> Thou hast seen all their vengeance and all their devices against me.<br>
<sup>61</sup> Thou hast heard their reproach, O Jehovah, and all their devices against me,<br>
<sup>62</sup> The lips of those that rose up against me, and their device against me all the day.<br>
<sup>63</sup> Behold thou their sitting down, and their rising up; I am their song.<br>
<sup>64</sup> Thou wilt render unto them a recompense, O Jehovah, according to the work of their hands.<br>
<sup>65</sup> Thou wilt give them <sup>l</sup>hardness of heart, thy curse unto them.<br>
<sup>66</sup> Thou wilt pursue them in anger, and destroy them from under the heavens of Jehovah.<br>

## Chapter 4

<sup>1</sup> How is the gold become dim! _how_ is the most pure gold changed!<br>The stones of the sanctuary are poured out at the head of every street.<br>
<sup>2</sup> The precious sons of Zion, <sup>a</sup>comparable to fine gold,<br>How are they esteemed as earthen pitchers, the work of the hands of the potter!<br>
<sup>3</sup> Even the jackals draw out the breast, they give suck to their young ones:<br>The daughter of my people is become cruel, like the ostriches in the wilderness.<br>
<sup>4</sup> The tongue of the sucking child cleaveth to the roof of his mouth for thirst:<br>The young children ask bread, and no man breaketh it unto them.<br>
<sup>5</sup> They that did feed delicately are desolate in the streets:<br>They that were brought up in scarlet embrace dunghills.<br>
<sup>6</sup> For <sup>b</sup>the iniquity of the daughter of my people is greater than <sup>c</sup>the sin of Sodom,<br>That was overthrown as in a moment, and no hands <sup>d</sup>were laid upon her.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Her <sup>e</sup>nobles were purer than snow, they were whiter than milk;<br>They were more ruddy in body than <sup>f</sup>rubies, their polishing was as of sapphire.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Their visage is <sup>g</sup>blacker than a coal; they are not known in the streets:<br>Their skin cleaveth to their bones; it is withered, it is become like a stick.<br>
<sup>9</sup> They that are slain with the sword are better than they that are slain with hunger;<br>For these <sup>h</sup>pine away, stricken through, for want of the fruits of the field.<br>
<sup>10</sup> The hands of the pitiful women have boiled their own children;<br>They were their food in the destruction of the daughter of my people.<br>
<sup>11</sup> Jehovah hath accomplished his wrath, he hath poured out his fierce anger;<br>And he hath kindled a fire in Zion, which hath devoured the foundations thereof.<br>
<sup>12</sup> The kings of the earth believed not, neither all the inhabitants of the world,<br>That the adversary and the enemy would enter into the gates of Jerusalem.<br>
<sup>13</sup> _It is_ because of the sins of her prophets, _and_ the iniquities of her priests,<br>That have shed the blood of the just in the midst of her.<br>
<sup>14</sup> They wander as blind men in the streets, they are polluted with blood,<br>So that men cannot touch their garments.<br>
<sup>15</sup> Depart ye, they cried unto them, Unclean! depart, depart, touch not!<sup>i</sup><br>When they fled away and wandered, men said among the nations, They shall no more sojourn _here_.<br>
<sup>16</sup> The <sup>j</sup>anger of Jehovah hath scattered them; he will no more regard them:<br>They respected not the persons of the priests, they favored not the elders.<br>
<sup>17</sup> Our eyes do yet fail _in looking_ for our vain help:<br>In our watching we have watched for a nation that could not save.<br>
<sup>18</sup> They hunt our steps, so that we cannot go in our streets:<br>Our end is near, our days are fulfilled; for our end is come.<br>
<sup>19</sup> Our pursuers were swifter than the eagles of the heavens:<br>They chased us upon the mountains, they laid wait for us in the wilderness.<br>
<sup>20</sup> The breath of our nostrils, the anointed of Jehovah, was taken in their pits;<br>Of whom we said, Under his shadow we shall live among the nations.<br>
<sup>21</sup> Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom, that dwellest in the land of Uz:<br>The cup shall pass through unto thee also; thou shalt be drunken, and shalt make thyself naked.<br>
<sup>22</sup> <sup>k</sup>The punishment of thine iniquity is accomplished, O daughter of Zion; he will no more carry thee away into captivity:<br>He will visit thine iniquity, O daughter of Edom; he will uncover thy sins.<br>

## Chapter 5

<sup>1</sup> Remember, O Jehovah, what is come upon us:<br>Behold, and see our reproach.<br>
<sup>2</sup> Our inheritance is turned unto strangers,<br>Our houses unto aliens.<br>
<sup>3</sup> We are orphans and fatherless;<br>Our mothers are as widows.<br>
<sup>4</sup> We have drunken our water for money;<br>Our wood <sup>a</sup>is sold unto us.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Our pursuers are upon our necks:<br>We are weary, and have no rest.<br>
<sup>6</sup> We have given the hand to the Egyptians,<br>And to the Assyrians, to be satisfied with bread.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Our fathers sinned, and are not;<br>And we have borne their iniquities.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Servants rule over us:<br>There is none to deliver us out of their hand.<br>
<sup>9</sup> We get our bread at the peril of our lives,<br>Because of the sword of the wilderness.<br>
<sup>10</sup> Our skin is <sup>b</sup>black like an oven,<br>Because of the burning heat of famine.<br>
<sup>11</sup> They ravished the women in Zion,<br>The virgins in the cities of Judah.<br>
<sup>12</sup> Princes were hanged up by their hand:<br>The faces of elders were not honored.<br>
<sup>13</sup> The young men bare the mill;<br>And the children stumbled under the wood.<br>
<sup>14</sup> The elders have ceased from the gate,<br>The young men from their music.<br>
<sup>15</sup> The joy of our heart is ceased;<br>Our dance is turned into mourning.<br>
<sup>16</sup> The crown is fallen from our head:<br>Woe unto us! for we have sinned.<br>
<sup>17</sup> For this our heart is faint;<br>For these things our eyes are dim;<br>
<sup>18</sup> For the mountain of Zion, which is desolate:<br>The <sup>c</sup>foxes walk upon it.<br>
<sup>19</sup> Thou, O Jehovah, <sup>d</sup>abidest for ever;<br>Thy throne is from generation to generation.<br>
<sup>20</sup> Wherefore dost thou forget us for ever,<br>_And_ forsake us so long time?<br>
<sup>21</sup> Turn thou us unto thee, O Jehovah, and we shall be turned;<br>Renew our days as of old.<br>
<sup>22</sup> <sup>e</sup>But thou hast utterly rejected us;<br>Thou art very wroth against us.<br>

> - <sup>d</sup> 19. Or, _sittest_ as King
