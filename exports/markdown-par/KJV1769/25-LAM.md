## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> How doth the city sit solitary,<br>_That was_ full of people!<br>_How_ is she become as a widow!<br>She _that was_ great among the nations,<br>_And_ princess among the provinces,<br>_How_ is she become tributary!<br>
<sup>2</sup> She weepeth sore in the night,<br>And her tears _are_ on her cheeks:<br>Among all her lovers<br>She hath none to comfort _her:_<br>All her friends have dealt treacherously with her,<br>They are become her enemies.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Judah is gone into captivity because of affliction,<br>And because of great servitude:<sup>a</sup><br>She dwelleth among the heathen,<br>She findeth no rest:<br>All her persecutors overtook her<br>Between the straits.<br>
<sup>4</sup> The ways of Zion do mourn,<br>Because none come to the solemn feasts:<br>All her gates are desolate:<br>Her priests sigh,<br>Her virgins are afflicted,<br>And she _is_ in bitterness.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Her adversaries are the chief,<br>Her enemies prosper;<br>For the LORD hath afflicted her<br>For the multitude of her transgressions:<br>Her children are gone<br>Into captivity before the enemy.<br>
<sup>6</sup> And from the daughter of Zion<br>All her beauty is departed:<br>Her princes are become like harts<br>_That_ find no pasture,<br>And they are gone without strength<br>Before the pursuer.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Jerusalem remembered<br>In the days of her affliction and of her miseries<br>All her pleasant<sup>b</sup> things<br>That she had in the days of old,<br>When her people fell into the hand of the enemy,<br>And none did help her:<br>The adversaries saw her,<br>_And_ did mock at her sabbaths.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Jerusalem hath grievously sinned;<br>Therefore she is removed:<sup>c</sup><br>All that honoured her despise her,<br>Because they have seen her nakedness:<br>Yea, she sigheth,<br>And turneth backward.<br>
<sup>9</sup> Her filthiness _is_ in her skirts;<br>She remembereth not her last end;<br>Therefore she came down wonderfully:<br>She had no comforter.<br>O LORD, behold my affliction:<br>For the enemy hath magnified _himself_.<br>
<sup>10</sup> The adversary hath spread out his hand<br>Upon all her pleasant<sup>d</sup> things:<br>For she hath seen _that_ the heathen<br>Entered into her sanctuary,<br>Whom thou didst command<br>_That_ they should not enter into thy congregation.<br>
<sup>11</sup> All her people sigh,<br>They seek bread;<br>They have given their pleasant things<br>For meat to relieve the soul:<sup>e</sup><br>See, O LORD, and consider;<br>For I am become vile.<br>
<sup>12</sup> _Is it_ nothing to you,<sup>f</sup><br>All ye that pass by?<sup>g</sup><br>Behold, and see if there be any sorrow<br>Like unto my sorrow, which is done unto me,<br>Wherewith the LORD hath afflicted<br>_Me_ in the day of his fierce anger.<br>
<sup>13</sup> From above hath he sent fire into my bones,<br>And it prevaileth against them:<br>He hath spread a net for my feet,<br>He hath turned me back:<br>He hath made me desolate<br>_And_ faint all the day.<br>
<sup>14</sup> The yoke of my transgressions is bound<br>By his hand:<br>They are wreathed, _and_ come up upon my neck:<br>He hath made my strength to fall,<br>The Lord hath delivered me into _their_ hands,<br>_From whom_ I am not able to rise up.<br>
<sup>15</sup> The Lord hath trodden under foot all my mighty _men_<br>In the midst of me:<br>He hath called an assembly against me<br>To crush my young men:<br>The Lord hath trodden the virgin,<br>The daughter of Judah, _as_ in a winepress.<br>
<sup>16</sup> For these _things_ I weep;<br>Mine eye, mine eye runneth down with water,<br>Because the comforter that should relieve<sup>h</sup> my<br>Soul is far from me:<br>My children are desolate,<br>Because the enemy prevailed.<br>
<sup>17</sup> Zion spreadeth forth her hands,<br>_And there is_ none to comfort her:<br>The LORD hath commanded concerning Jacob,<br>_That_ his adversaries _should be_ round about him:<br>Jerusalem is as<br>A menstruous woman among them.<br>
<sup>18</sup> The LORD is righteous;<br>For I have rebelled against his commandment:<sup>i</sup><br>Hear, I pray you, all people,<br>And behold my sorrow:<br>My virgins and my young men<br>Are gone into captivity.<br>
<sup>19</sup> I called for my lovers,<br>_But_ they deceived me:<br>My priests and mine elders<br>Gave up the ghost in the city,<br>While they sought their meat<br>To relieve their souls.<br>
<sup>20</sup> Behold, O LORD; for I _am_ in distress:<br>My bowels are troubled;<br>Mine heart is turned within me;<br>For I have grievously rebelled:<br>Abroad the sword bereaveth,<br>At home _there is_ as death.<br>
<sup>21</sup> They have heard that I sigh:<br>_There is_ none to comfort me:<br>All mine enemies have heard of my trouble;<br>They are glad that thou hast done _it:_<br>Thou wilt bring the day _that_ thou hast called,<sup>j</sup><br>And they shall be like unto me.<br>
<sup>22</sup> Let all their wickedness come before thee;<br>And do unto them,<br>As thou hast done unto me<br>For all my transgressions:<br>For my sighs _are_ many,<br>And my heart _is_ faint.<br>

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> How hath the Lord covered<br>The daughter of Zion with a cloud in his anger,<br>_And_ cast down from heaven unto the earth<br>The beauty of Israel,<br>And remembered not his footstool<br>In the day of his anger!<br>
<sup>2</sup> The Lord hath swallowed up<br>All the habitations of Jacob, and hath not pitied:<br>He hath thrown down in his wrath<br>The strong holds of the daughter of Judah;<br>He hath brought _them_ down<sup>a</sup> to the ground:<br>He hath polluted the kingdom and the princes thereof.<br>
<sup>3</sup> He hath cut off in _his_ fierce anger<br>All the horn of Israel:<br>He hath drawn back his right hand<br>From before the enemy,<br>And he burned against Jacob like a flaming fire,<br>_Which_ devoureth round about.<br>
<sup>4</sup> He hath bent his bow like an enemy:<br>He stood with his right hand as an adversary,<br>And slew all _that were_ pleasant to the eye<sup>b</sup><br>In the tabernacle of the daughter of Zion:<br>He poured out his fury like fire.<br>
<sup>5</sup> The Lord was as an enemy:<br>He hath swallowed up Israel,<br>He hath swallowed up all her palaces:<br>He hath destroyed his strong holds,<br>And hath increased in the daughter of Judah<br>Mourning and lamentation.<br>
<sup>6</sup> And he hath violently taken away his tabernacle,<sup>c</sup> as _if it were of_ a garden:<br>He hath destroyed his places of the assembly:<br>The LORD hath caused the solemn feasts<br>And sabbaths to be forgotten in Zion,<br>And hath despised in the indignation of his anger<br>The king and the priest.<br>
<sup>7</sup> The Lord hath cast off his altar,<br>He hath abhorred his sanctuary,<br>He hath given up<sup>d</sup> into the hand of the enemy<br>The walls of her palaces;<br>They have made a noise in the house of the LORD,<br>As in the day of a solemn feast.<br>
<sup>8</sup> The LORD hath purposed to destroy<br>The wall of the daughter of Zion:<br>He hath stretched out a line,<br>He hath not withdrawn his hand from destroying:<sup>e</sup><br>Therefore he made the rampart and the wall to lament;<br>They languished together.<br>
<sup>9</sup> Her gates are sunk into the ground;<br>He hath destroyed and broken her bars:<br>Her king and her princes _are_ among the Gentiles:<br>The law _is_ no _more_;<br>Her prophets also find<br>No vision from the LORD.<br>
<sup>10</sup> The elders of the daughter of Zion<br>Sit upon the ground, _and_ keep silence:<br>They have cast up dust upon their heads;<br>They have girded themselves with sackcloth:<br>The virgins of Jerusalem<br>Hang down their heads to the ground.<br>
<sup>11</sup> Mine eyes do fail with tears,<br>My bowels are troubled,<br>My liver is poured upon the earth,<br>For the destruction of the daughter of my people;<br>Because the children and the sucklings swoon<sup>f</sup><br>In the streets of the city.<br>
<sup>12</sup> They say to their mothers,<br>Where _is_ corn and wine?<br>When they swooned as the wounded<br>In the streets of the city,<br>When their soul was poured out<br>Into their mothers’ bosom.<br>
<sup>13</sup> What thing shall I take to witness for thee? what thing shall I liken to thee,<br>O daughter of Jerusalem?<br>What shall I equal to thee, that I may comfort thee,<br>O virgin daughter of Zion?<br>For thy breach _is_ great like the sea:<br>Who can heal thee?<br>
<sup>14</sup> Thy prophets have seen vain<br>And foolish things for thee:<br>And they have not discovered thine iniquity,<br>To turn away thy captivity;<br>But have seen for thee false burdens<br>And causes of banishment.<br>
<sup>15</sup> All that pass by<sup>g</sup> clap<br>_Their_ hands at thee;<br>They hiss and wag their head<br>At the daughter of Jerusalem, _saying_,<br>_Is_ this the city that _men_ call<br>The perfection of beauty,<br>The joy of the whole earth?<br>
<sup>16</sup> All thine enemies<br>Have opened their mouth against thee:<br>They hiss and gnash the teeth:<br>They say, We have swallowed _her_ up:<br>Certainly this _is_ the day that we looked for;<br>We have found, we have seen _it_.<br>
<sup>17</sup> The LORD hath done _that_ which he had devised;<br>He hath fulfilled his word<br>That he had commanded in the days of old:<br>He hath thrown down, and hath not pitied:<br>And he hath caused _thine_ enemy to rejoice over thee,<br>He hath set up the horn of thine adversaries.<br>
<sup>18</sup> Their heart cried unto the Lord,<br>O wall of the daughter of Zion,<br>Let tears run down like a river<br>Day and night:<br>Give thyself no rest;<br>Let not the apple of thine eye cease.<br>
<sup>19</sup> Arise, cry out in the night:<br>In the beginning of the watches<br>Pour out thine heart like water<br>Before the face of the Lord:<br>Lift up thy hands toward him for the life<br>Of thy young children,<br>That faint for hunger<br>In the top of every street.<br>
<sup>20</sup> Behold, O LORD,<br>And consider to whom thou hast done this.<br>Shall the women eat their fruit,<br>_And_ children of a span long?<sup>h</sup><br>Shall the priest and the prophet be slain<br>In the sanctuary of the Lord?<br>
<sup>21</sup> The young and the old lie<br>On the ground in the streets:<br>My virgins and my young men<br>Are fallen by the sword;<br>Thou hast slain _them_ in the day of thine anger;<br>Thou hast killed, _and_ not pitied.<br>
<sup>22</sup> Thou hast called as in a solemn day<br>My terrors round about,<br>So that in the day of the LORD’s anger<br>None escaped nor remained:<br>Those that I have swaddled and brought up hath<br>Mine enemy consumed.<br>

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> I _am_ the man _that_ hath seen affliction<br>By the rod of his wrath.<br>
<sup>2</sup> He hath led me, and brought _me_<br>_Into_ darkness, but not _into_ light.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Surely against me is he turned; he turneth his hand<br>_Against me_ all the day.<br>
<sup>4</sup> My flesh and my skin hath he made old;<br>He hath broken my bones.<br>
<sup>5</sup> He hath builded against me,<br>And compassed _me_ with gall and travail.<br>
<sup>6</sup> He hath set me in dark places,<br>As _they that be_ dead of old.<br>
<sup>7</sup> He hath hedged me about, that I cannot get out:<br>He hath made my chain heavy.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Also when I cry and shout,<br>He shutteth out my prayer.<br>
<sup>9</sup> He hath inclosed my ways with hewn stone,<br>He hath made my paths crooked.<br>
<sup>10</sup> He _was_ unto me _as_ a bear lying in wait,<br>_And as_ a lion in secret places.<br>
<sup>11</sup> He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces:<br>He hath made me desolate.<br>
<sup>12</sup> He hath bent his bow, and set me<br>As a mark for the arrow.<br>
<sup>13</sup> He hath caused the arrows<sup>a</sup> of his quiver<br>To enter into my reins.<br>
<sup>14</sup> I was a derision to all my people;<br>_And_ their song all the day.<br>
<sup>15</sup> He hath filled me with bitterness,<sup>b</sup><br>He hath made me drunken with wormwood.<br>
<sup>16</sup> He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones,<br>He hath covered me with ashes.<sup>c</sup><br>
<sup>17</sup> And thou hast removed my soul far off from peace:<br>I forgat prosperity.<sup>d</sup><br>
<sup>18</sup> And I said, My strength and my hope<br>Is perished from the LORD:<br>
<sup>19</sup> Remembering<sup>e</sup> mine affliction and my misery,<br>The wormwood and the gall.<br>
<sup>20</sup> My soul hath _them_ still in remembrance,<br>And is humbled<sup>f</sup> in me.<br>
<sup>21</sup> This I recall to my mind,<sup>g</sup><br>Therefore have I hope.<br>
<sup>22</sup> _It is of_ the LORD’s mercies that we are not consumed,<br>Because his compassions fail not.<br>
<sup>23</sup> _They are_ new every morning:<br>Great _is_ thy faithfulness.<br>
<sup>24</sup> The LORD _is_ my portion, saith my soul;<br>Therefore will I hope in him.<br>
<sup>25</sup> The LORD _is_ good unto them that wait for him,<br>To the soul _that_ seeketh him.<br>
<sup>26</sup> _It is_ good that _a man_ should both hope and quietly<br>Wait for the salvation of the LORD.<br>
<sup>27</sup> _It is_ good for a man that he bear<br>The yoke in his youth.<br>
<sup>28</sup> He sitteth alone and keepeth silence,<br>Because he hath borne _it_ upon him.<br>
<sup>29</sup> He putteth his mouth in the dust;<br>If so be there may be hope.<br>
<sup>30</sup> He giveth _his_ cheek to him that smiteth him:<br>He is filled full with reproach.<br>
<sup>31</sup> For the Lord will not<br>Cast off for ever:<br>
<sup>32</sup> But though he cause grief, yet will he have compassion<br>According to the multitude of his mercies.<br>
<sup>33</sup> For he doth not afflict willingly<sup>h</sup><br>Nor grieve the children of men.<br>
<sup>34</sup> To crush under his feet<br>All the prisoners of the earth,<br>
<sup>35</sup> To turn aside the right of a man<br>Before the face of the Most High,<sup>i</sup><br>
<sup>36</sup> To subvert a man in his cause,<br>The Lord approveth not.<sup>j</sup><br>
<sup>37</sup> Who _is_ he _that_ saith, and it cometh to pass,<br>_When_ the Lord commandeth _it_ not?<br>
<sup>38</sup> Out of the mouth of the Most High<br>Proceedeth not evil and good?<br>
<sup>39</sup> Wherefore doth a living man complain,<sup>k</sup><br>A man for the punishment of his sins?<br>
<sup>40</sup> Let us search and try our ways,<br>And turn again to the LORD.<br>
<sup>41</sup> Let us lift up our heart with _our_ hands<br>Unto God in the heavens.<br>
<sup>42</sup> We have transgressed and have rebelled:<br>Thou hast not pardoned.<br>
<sup>43</sup> Thou hast covered with anger, and persecuted us:<br>Thou hast slain, thou hast not pitied.<br>
<sup>44</sup> Thou hast covered thyself with a cloud,<br>That _our_ prayer should not pass through.<br>
<sup>45</sup> Thou hast made us _as_ the offscouring and refuse<br>In the midst of the people.<br>
<sup>46</sup> All our enemies<br>Have opened their mouths against us.<br>
<sup>47</sup> Fear and a snare is come upon us,<br>Desolation and destruction.<br>
<sup>48</sup> Mine eye runneth down with rivers of water<br>For the destruction of the daughter of my people.<br>
<sup>49</sup> Mine eye trickleth down, and ceaseth not,<br>Without any intermission,<br>
<sup>50</sup> Till the LORD look down,<br>And behold from heaven.<br>
<sup>51</sup> Mine eye affecteth mine heart<sup>l</sup><br>Because of all<sup>m</sup> the daughters of my city.<br>
<sup>52</sup> Mine enemies chased me sore, like a bird,<br>Without cause.<br>
<sup>53</sup> They have cut off my life in the dungeon,<br>And cast a stone upon me.<br>
<sup>54</sup> Waters flowed over mine head;<br>_Then_ I said, I am cut off.<br>
<sup>55</sup> I called upon thy name, O LORD,<br>Out of the low dungeon.<br>
<sup>56</sup> Thou hast heard my voice:<br>Hide not thine ear at my breathing, at my cry.<br>
<sup>57</sup> Thou drewest near in the day _that_ I called upon thee:<br>Thou saidst, Fear not.<br>
<sup>58</sup> O Lord, thou hast pleaded the causes of my soul;<br>Thou hast redeemed my life.<br>
<sup>59</sup> O LORD, thou hast seen my wrong:<br>Judge thou my cause.<br>
<sup>60</sup> Thou hast seen all their vengeance<br>_And_ all their imaginations against me.<br>
<sup>61</sup> Thou hast heard their reproach, O LORD,<br>_And_ all their imaginations against me;<br>
<sup>62</sup> The lips of those that rose up against me,<br>And their device against me all the day.<br>
<sup>63</sup> Behold their sitting down, and their rising up;<br>I _am_ their musick.<br>
<sup>64</sup> Render unto them a recompence, O LORD,<br>According to the work of their hands.<br>
<sup>65</sup> Give them sorrow of heart,<br>Thy curse unto them.<br>
<sup>66</sup> Persecute and destroy them in anger<br>From under the heavens of the LORD.<br>

## Chapter 4

<sup>1</sup> How is the gold become dim!<br>_How_ is the most fine gold changed!<br>The stones of the sanctuary are poured out<br>In the top of every street.<br>
<sup>2</sup> The precious sons of Zion,<br>Comparable to fine gold,<br>How are they esteemed as earthen pitchers,<br>The work of the hands of the potter!<br>
<sup>3</sup> Even the sea monsters<sup>a</sup> draw out the breast,<br>They give suck to their young ones:<br>The daughter of my people _is become_ cruel,<br>Like the ostriches in the wilderness.<br>
<sup>4</sup> The tongue of the sucking child cleaveth<br>To the roof of his mouth for thirst:<br>The young children ask bread,<br>_And_ no man breaketh _it_ unto them.<br>
<sup>5</sup> They that did feed delicately<br>Are desolate in the streets:<br>They that were brought up<br>In scarlet embrace dunghills.<br>
<sup>6</sup> For the punishment of the iniquity<sup>b</sup> of the daughter of my people is greater<br>Than the punishment of the sin of Sodom,<br>That was overthrown as in a moment,<br>And no hands stayed on her.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Her Nazarites were purer than snow,<br>They were whiter than milk,<br>They were more ruddy in body than rubies,<br>Their polishing _was_ of sapphire:<br>
<sup>8</sup> Their visage is blacker than a coal;<br>They are not known in the streets:<br>Their skin cleaveth to their bones;<br>It is withered, it is become like a stick.<br>
<sup>9</sup> _They that be_ slain with the sword<br>Are better than _they that be_ slain with hunger:<br>For these pine away,<sup>c</sup> stricken<br>Through for _want of_ the fruits of the field.<br>
<sup>10</sup> The hands of the pitiful women<br>Have sodden their own children:<br>They were their meat<br>In the destruction of the daughter of my people.<br>
<sup>11</sup> The LORD hath accomplished his fury;<br>He hath poured out his fierce anger,<br>And hath kindled a fire in Zion,<br>And it hath devoured the foundations thereof.<br>
<sup>12</sup> The kings of the earth,<br>And all the inhabitants of the world, would not have believed<br>That the adversary and the enemy should have entered<br>Into the gates of Jerusalem.<br>
<sup>13</sup> For the sins of her prophets,<br>_And_ the iniquities of her priests,<br>That have shed the blood<br>Of the just in the midst of her,<br>
<sup>14</sup> They have wandered _as_ blind _men_ in the streets,<br>They have polluted themselves with blood,<br>So that men could not touch<sup>d</sup><br>Their garments.<br>
<sup>15</sup> They cried unto them, Depart ye; _it is_ unclean;<sup>e</sup><br>Depart, depart, touch not:<br>When they fled away and wandered,<br>They said among the heathen,<br>They shall no more sojourn _there_.<br>
<sup>16</sup> The anger<sup>f</sup> of the LORD hath divided them;<br>He will no more regard them:<br>They respected not the persons of the priests,<br>They favoured not the elders.<br>
<sup>17</sup> As for us, our eyes as yet failed<br>For our vain help:<br>In our watching we have watched<br>For a nation _that_ could not save _us_.<br>
<sup>18</sup> They hunt our steps,<br>That we cannot go in our streets:<br>Our end is near, our days are fulfilled;<br>For our end is come.<br>
<sup>19</sup> Our persecutors are swifter<br>Than the eagles of the heaven:<br>They pursued us upon the mountains,<br>They laid wait for us in the wilderness.<br>
<sup>20</sup> The breath of our nostrils, the anointed of the LORD,<br>Was taken in their pits,<br>Of whom we said, Under his shadow<br>We shall live among the heathen.<br>
<sup>21</sup> Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom,<br>That dwellest in the land of Uz;<br>The cup also shall pass through unto thee:<br>Thou shalt be drunken, and shalt make thyself naked.<br>
<sup>22</sup> The punishment of thine iniquity<sup>g</sup> is accomplished, O daughter of Zion;<br>He will no more carry thee away into captivity:<br>He will visit thine iniquity, O daughter of Edom;<br>He will discover thy sins.<sup>h</sup><br>

## Chapter 5

<sup>1</sup> Remember, O LORD, what is come upon us:<br>Consider, and behold our reproach.<br>
<sup>2</sup> Our inheritance is turned to strangers,<br>Our houses to aliens.<br>
<sup>3</sup> We are orphans and fatherless,<br>Our mothers _are_ as widows.<br>
<sup>4</sup> We have drunken our water for money;<br>Our wood is sold unto us.<sup>a</sup><br>
<sup>5</sup> Our necks _are_ under persecution:<sup>b</sup><br>We labour, _and_ have no rest.<br>
<sup>6</sup> We have given the hand _to_ the Egyptians,<br>_And to_ the Assyrians, to be satisfied with bread.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Our fathers have sinned, and _are_ not;<br>And we have borne their iniquities.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Servants have ruled over us:<br>_There is_ none that doth deliver _us_ out of their hand.<br>
<sup>9</sup> We gat our bread with _the peril of_ our lives<br>Because of the sword of the wilderness.<br>
<sup>10</sup> Our skin was black like an oven<br>Because of the terrible<sup>c</sup> famine.<br>
<sup>11</sup> They ravished the women in Zion,<br>_And_ the maids in the cities of Judah.<br>
<sup>12</sup> Princes are hanged up by their hand:<br>The faces of elders were not honoured.<br>
<sup>13</sup> They took the young men to grind,<br>And the children fell under the wood.<br>
<sup>14</sup> The elders have ceased from the gate,<br>The young men from their musick.<br>
<sup>15</sup> The joy of our heart is ceased;<br>Our dance is turned into mourning.<br>
<sup>16</sup> The crown is fallen _from_ our head:<sup>d</sup><br>Woe unto us, that we have sinned!<br>
<sup>17</sup> For this our heart is faint;<br>For these _things_ our eyes are dim.<br>
<sup>18</sup> Because of the mountain of Zion, which is desolate,<br>The foxes walk upon it.<br>
<sup>19</sup> Thou, O LORD, remainest for ever;<br>Thy throne from generation to generation.<br>
<sup>20</sup> Wherefore dost thou forget us for ever,<br>_And_ forsake us so long time?<sup>e</sup><br>
<sup>21</sup> Turn thou us unto thee, O LORD, and we shall be turned;<br>Renew our days as of old.<br>
<sup>22</sup> But thou hast utterly rejected us;<sup>f</sup><br>Thou art very wroth against us.<br>

> - <sup>d</sup> 16. Heb. _the crown of our head is fallen [† symbol is missing in 1611 text]_
> - <sup>e</sup> 20. Heb. _for length of days_
> - <sup>f</sup> 22. Or, _for wilt thou utterly reject us?_
