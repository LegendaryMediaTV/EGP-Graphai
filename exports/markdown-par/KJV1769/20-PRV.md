## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel;<br>
<sup>2</sup> To know wisdom and instruction;<br>To perceive the words of understanding;<br>
<sup>3</sup> To receive the instruction of wisdom,<br>Justice, and judgment, and equity;<sup>a</sup><br>
<sup>4</sup> To give subtilty to the simple,<br>To the young man knowledge and discretion.<sup>b</sup><br>
<sup>5</sup> A wise _man_ will hear, and will increase learning;<br>And a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels:<br>
<sup>6</sup> To understand a proverb, and the interpretation;<sup>c</sup><br>The words of the wise, and their dark sayings.<br>
<sup>7</sup> The fear of the LORD _is_ the beginning<sup>d</sup> of knowledge:<br>_But_ fools despise wisdom and instruction.<br>
<sup>8</sup> My son, hear the instruction of thy father,<br>And forsake not the law of thy mother:<br>
<sup>9</sup> For they _shall be_ an ornament<sup>e</sup> of grace unto thy head,<br>And chains about thy neck.<br>
<sup>10</sup> My son, if sinners entice thee,<br>Consent thou not.<br>
<sup>11</sup> If they say, Come with us, let us lay wait for blood,<br>Let us lurk privily for the innocent without cause:<br>
<sup>12</sup> Let us swallow them up alive as the grave;<br>And whole, as those that go down into the pit:<br>
<sup>13</sup> We shall find all precious substance,<br>We shall fill our houses with spoil:<br>
<sup>14</sup> Cast in thy lot among us;<br>Let us all have one purse:<br>
<sup>15</sup> My son, walk not thou in the way with them;<br>Refrain thy foot from their path:<br>
<sup>16</sup> For their feet run to evil,<br>And make haste to shed blood.<br>
<sup>17</sup> Surely in vain the net is spread<br>In the sight of any bird.<sup>f</sup><br>
<sup>18</sup> And they lay wait for their _own_ blood;<br>They lurk privily for their _own_ lives.<br>
<sup>19</sup> So _are_ the ways of every one that is greedy of gain;<br>_Which_ taketh away the life of the owners thereof.<br>

### The Cry of Wisdom

<sup>20</sup> Wisdom<sup>g</sup> crieth without;<br>She uttereth her voice in the streets:<br>
<sup>21</sup> She crieth in the chief place of concourse, in the openings of the gates:<br>In the city she uttereth her words, _saying_,<br>
<sup>22</sup> How long, ye simple ones, will ye love simplicity?<br>And the scorners delight in their scorning,<br>And fools hate knowledge?<br>
<sup>23</sup> Turn you at my reproof:<br>Behold, I will pour out my spirit unto you,<br>I will make known my words unto you.<br>
<sup>24</sup> Because I have called, and ye refused;<br>I have stretched out my hand, and no man regarded;<br>
<sup>25</sup> But ye have set at nought all my counsel,<br>And would none of my reproof:<br>
<sup>26</sup> I also will laugh at your calamity;<br>I will mock when your fear cometh;<br>
<sup>27</sup> When your fear cometh as desolation,<br>And your destruction cometh as a whirlwind;<br>When distress and anguish cometh upon you.<br>
<sup>28</sup> Then shall they call upon me, but I will not answer;<br>They shall seek me early, but they shall not find me:<br>
<sup>29</sup> For that they hated knowledge,<br>And did not choose the fear of the LORD:<br>
<sup>30</sup> They would none of my counsel:<br>They despised all my reproof.<br>
<sup>31</sup> Therefore shall they eat of the fruit of their own way,<br>And be filled with their own devices.<br>
<sup>32</sup> For the turning away of the simple<sup>h</sup> shall slay them,<br>And the prosperity of fools shall destroy them.<br>
<sup>33</sup> But whoso hearkeneth unto me shall dwell safely,<br>And shall be quiet from fear of evil.<br>

> - <sup>g</sup> 20. Heb. _Wisdoms_, that is, _excellent wisdom_

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> My son, if thou wilt receive my words,<br>And hide my commandments with thee;<br>
<sup>2</sup> So that thou incline thine ear unto wisdom,<br>_And_ apply thine heart to understanding;<br>
<sup>3</sup> Yea, if thou criest after knowledge,<br>_And_ liftest up thy voice<sup>a</sup> for understanding;<br>
<sup>4</sup> If thou seekest her as silver,<br>And searchest for her as _for_ hid treasures;<br>
<sup>5</sup> Then shalt thou understand the fear of the LORD,<br>And find the knowledge of God.<br>
<sup>6</sup> For the LORD giveth wisdom:<br>Out of his mouth _cometh_ knowledge and understanding.<br>
<sup>7</sup> He layeth up sound wisdom for the righteous:<br>_He is_ a buckler to them that walk uprightly.<br>
<sup>8</sup> He keepeth the paths of judgment,<br>And preserveth the way of his saints.<br>
<sup>9</sup> Then shalt thou understand righteousness, and judgment,<br>And equity; _yea_, every good path.<br>
<sup>10</sup> When wisdom entereth into thine heart,<br>And knowledge is pleasant unto thy soul;<br>
<sup>11</sup> Discretion shall preserve thee,<br>Understanding shall keep thee:<br>
<sup>12</sup> To deliver thee from the way of the evil _man_,<br>From the man that speaketh froward things;<br>
<sup>13</sup> Who leave the paths of uprightness,<br>To walk in the ways of darkness;<br>
<sup>14</sup> Who rejoice to do evil,<br>_And_ delight in the frowardness of the wicked;<br>
<sup>15</sup> Whose ways _are_ crooked,<br>And _they_ froward in their paths:<br>
<sup>16</sup> To deliver thee from the strange woman,<br>_Even_ from the stranger _which_ flattereth with her words;<br>
<sup>17</sup> Which forsaketh the guide of her youth,<br>And forgetteth the covenant of her God.<br>
<sup>18</sup> For her house inclineth unto death,<br>And her paths unto the dead.<br>
<sup>19</sup> None that go unto her return again,<br>Neither take they hold of the paths of life.<br>
<sup>20</sup> That thou mayest walk in the way of good _men_,<br>And keep the paths of the righteous.<br>
<sup>21</sup> For the upright shall dwell in the land,<br>And the perfect shall remain in it.<br>
<sup>22</sup> But the wicked shall be cut off from the earth,<br>And the transgressors shall be rooted out<sup>b</sup> of it.<br>

> - <sup>a</sup> 3. Heb. _givest thy voice_
> - <sup>b</sup> 22. Or, _plucked up_

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> My son, forget not my law;<br>But let thine heart keep my commandments:<br>
<sup>2</sup> For length of days, and long life,<sup>a</sup><br>And peace, shall they add to thee.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Let not mercy and truth forsake thee:<br>Bind them about thy neck;<br>Write them upon the table of thine heart:<br>
<sup>4</sup> So shalt thou find favour and good understanding<sup>b</sup><br>In the sight of God and man.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Trust in the LORD with all thine heart;<br>And lean not unto thine own understanding.<br>
<sup>6</sup> In all thy ways acknowledge him,<br>And he shall direct thy paths.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Be not wise in thine own eyes:<br>Fear the LORD, and depart from evil.<br>
<sup>8</sup> It shall be health<sup>c</sup> to thy navel,<br>And marrow<sup>d</sup> to thy bones.<br>
<sup>9</sup> Honour the LORD with thy substance,<br>And with the firstfruits of all thine increase:<br>
<sup>10</sup> So shall thy barns be filled with plenty,<br>And thy presses shall burst out with new wine.<br>
<sup>11</sup> My son, despise not the chastening of the LORD;<br>Neither be weary of his correction:<br>
<sup>12</sup> For whom the LORD loveth he correcteth;<br>Even as a father the son _in whom_ he delighteth.<br>
<sup>13</sup> Happy _is_ the man _that_ findeth wisdom,<br>And the man _that_ getteth understanding.<sup>e</sup><br>
<sup>14</sup> For the merchandise of it _is_ better than the merchandise of silver,<br>And the gain thereof than fine gold.<br>
<sup>15</sup> She _is_ more precious than rubies:<br>And all the things thou canst desire are not to be compared unto her.<br>
<sup>16</sup> Length of days _is_ in her right hand;<br>_And_ in her left hand riches and honour.<br>
<sup>17</sup> Her ways _are_ ways of pleasantness,<br>And all her paths _are_ peace.<br>
<sup>18</sup> She _is_ a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her:<br>And happy _is every one_ that retaineth her.<br>
<sup>19</sup> The LORD by wisdom hath founded the earth;<br>By understanding hath he established<sup>f</sup> the heavens.<br>
<sup>20</sup> By his knowledge the depths are broken up,<br>And the clouds drop down the dew.<br>
<sup>21</sup> My son, let not them depart from thine eyes:<br>Keep sound wisdom and discretion:<br>
<sup>22</sup> So shall they be life unto thy soul,<br>And grace to thy neck.<br>
<sup>23</sup> Then shalt thou walk in thy way safely,<br>And thy foot shall not stumble.<br>
<sup>24</sup> When thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid:<br>Yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy sleep shall be sweet.<br>
<sup>25</sup> Be not afraid of sudden fear,<br>Neither of the desolation of the wicked, when it cometh.<br>
<sup>26</sup> For the LORD shall be thy confidence,<br>And shall keep thy foot from being taken.<br>
<sup>27</sup> Withhold not good from them to whom it is due,<sup>g</sup><br>When it is in the power of thine hand to do _it_.<br>
<sup>28</sup> Say not unto thy neighbour, Go, and come again,<br>And to morrow I will give; when thou hast it by thee.<br>
<sup>29</sup> Devise not evil<sup>h</sup> against thy neighbour,<br>Seeing he dwelleth securely by thee.<br>
<sup>30</sup> Strive not with a man without cause,<br>If he have done thee no harm.<br>
<sup>31</sup> Envy thou not the oppressor,<sup>i</sup><br>And choose none of his ways.<br>
<sup>32</sup> For the froward _is_ abomination to the LORD:<br>But his secret _is_ with the righteous.<br>
<sup>33</sup> The curse of the LORD _is_ in the house of the wicked:<br>But he blesseth the habitation of the just.<br>
<sup>34</sup> Surely he scorneth the scorners:<br>But he giveth grace unto the lowly.<br>
<sup>35</sup> The wise shall inherit glory:<br>But shame shall be the promotion of fools.<sup>j</sup><br>

> - <sup>b</sup> 4. Or, _good success_
> - <sup>c</sup> 8. Heb. _medicine_
> - <sup>d</sup> 8. Heb. _watering_, or _moistening_
> - <sup>e</sup> 13. Heb. _the man that draweth out understanding_
> - <sup>f</sup> 19. Or, _prepared_
> - <sup>g</sup> 27. Heb. _the owners thereof_
> - <sup>h</sup> 29. Or, _practise no evil_
> - <sup>i</sup> 31. Heb. _a man of violence_

## Chapter 4

### A Father’s Instruction

<sup>1</sup> Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father,<br>And attend to know understanding.<br>
<sup>2</sup> For I give you good doctrine,<br>Forsake ye not my law.<br>
<sup>3</sup> For I was my father’s son,<br>Tender and only _beloved_ in the sight of my mother.<br>
<sup>4</sup> He taught me also, and said unto me,<br>Let thine heart retain my words:<br>Keep my commandments, and live.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Get wisdom, get understanding:<br>Forget _it_ not; neither decline from the words of my mouth.<br>
<sup>6</sup> Forsake her not, and she shall preserve thee:<br>Love her, and she shall keep thee.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Wisdom _is_ the principal thing; _therefore_ get wisdom:<br>And with all thy getting get understanding.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Exalt her, and she shall promote thee:<br>She shall bring thee to honour, when thou dost embrace her.<br>
<sup>9</sup> She shall give to thine head an ornament of grace:<br>A crown of glory shall she deliver to thee.<sup>a</sup><br>
<sup>10</sup> Hear, O my son, and receive my sayings;<br>And the years of thy life shall be many.<br>
<sup>11</sup> I have taught thee in the way of wisdom;<br>I have led thee in right paths.<br>
<sup>12</sup> When thou goest, thy steps shall not be straitened;<br>And when thou runnest, thou shalt not stumble.<br>
<sup>13</sup> Take fast hold of instruction; let _her_ not go:<br>Keep her; for she _is_ thy life.<br>
<sup>14</sup> Enter not into the path of the wicked,<br>And go not in the way of evil _men_.<br>
<sup>15</sup> Avoid it, pass not by it,<br>Turn from it, and pass away.<br>
<sup>16</sup> For they sleep not, except they have done mischief;<br>And their sleep is taken away, unless they cause _some_ to fall.<br>
<sup>17</sup> For they eat the bread of wickedness,<br>And drink the wine of violence.<br>
<sup>18</sup> But the path of the just _is_ as the shining light,<br>That shineth more and more unto the perfect day.<br>
<sup>19</sup> The way of the wicked _is_ as darkness:<br>They know not at what they stumble.<br>
<sup>20</sup> My son, attend to my words;<br>Incline thine ear unto my sayings.<br>
<sup>21</sup> Let them not depart from thine eyes;<br>Keep them in the midst of thine heart.<br>
<sup>22</sup> For they _are_ life unto those that find them,<br>And health<sup>b</sup> to all their flesh.<br>
<sup>23</sup> Keep thy heart with all diligence;<sup>c</sup><br>For out of it _are_ the issues of life.<br>
<sup>24</sup> Put away from thee a froward mouth,<br>And perverse lips<sup>d</sup> put far from thee.<br>
<sup>25</sup> Let thine eyes look right on,<br>And let thine eyelids look straight before thee.<br>
<sup>26</sup> Ponder the path of thy feet,<br>And let all thy ways be established.<sup>e</sup><br>
<sup>27</sup> Turn not to the right hand nor to the left:<br>Remove thy foot from evil.<br>

> - <sup>b</sup> 22. Heb. _medicine_
> - <sup>c</sup> 23. Heb. _above all keeping_
> - <sup>d</sup> 24. Heb. _frowardness of mouth and perverseness of lips_
> - <sup>e</sup> 26. Or, _all thy ways shall be ordered aright_

## Chapter 5

<sup>1</sup> My son, attend unto my wisdom,<br>_And_ bow thine ear to my understanding:<br>
<sup>2</sup> That thou mayest regard discretion,<br>And _that_ thy lips may keep knowledge.<br>
<sup>3</sup> For the lips of a strange woman drop _as_ an honeycomb,<br>And her mouth<sup>a</sup> _is_ smoother than oil:<br>
<sup>4</sup> But her end is bitter as wormwood,<br>Sharp as a twoedged sword.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Her feet go down to death;<br>Her steps take hold on hell.<br>
<sup>6</sup> Lest thou shouldest ponder the path of life,<br>Her ways are moveable, _that_ thou canst not know _them_.<br>
<sup>7</sup> Hear me now therefore, O ye children,<br>And depart not from the words of my mouth.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Remove thy way far from her,<br>And come not nigh the door of her house:<br>
<sup>9</sup> Lest thou give thine honour unto others,<br>And thy years unto the cruel:<br>
<sup>10</sup> Lest strangers be filled with thy wealth;<sup>b</sup><br>And thy labours _be_ in the house of a stranger;<br>
<sup>11</sup> And thou mourn at the last,<br>When thy flesh and thy body are consumed,<br>
<sup>12</sup> And say, How have I hated instruction,<br>And my heart despised reproof;<br>
<sup>13</sup> And have not obeyed the voice of my teachers,<br>Nor inclined mine ear to them that instructed me!<br>
<sup>14</sup> I was almost in all evil<br>In the midst of the congregation and assembly.<br>
<sup>15</sup> Drink waters out of thine own cistern,<br>And running waters out of thine own well.<br>
<sup>16</sup> Let thy fountains be dispersed abroad,<br>_And_ rivers of waters in the streets.<br>
<sup>17</sup> Let them be only thine own,<br>And not strangers’ with thee.<br>
<sup>18</sup> Let thy fountain be blessed:<br>And rejoice with the wife of thy youth.<br>
<sup>19</sup> _Let her be as_ the loving hind and pleasant roe;<br>Let her breasts satisfy thee<sup>c</sup> at all times;<br>And be thou ravished always with her love.<sup>d</sup><br>
<sup>20</sup> And why wilt thou, my son, be ravished with a strange woman,<br>And embrace the bosom of a stranger?<br>
<sup>21</sup> For the ways of man _are_ before the eyes of the LORD,<br>And he pondereth all his goings.<br>
<sup>22</sup> His own iniquities shall take the wicked himself,<br>And he shall be holden with the cords of his sins.<sup>e</sup><br>
<sup>23</sup> He shall die without instruction;<br>And in the greatness of his folly he shall go astray.<br>

> - <sup>b</sup> 10. Heb. _thy strength_
> - <sup>c</sup> 19. Heb. _water thee_
> - <sup>d</sup> 19. Heb. _err thou always in her love_
> - <sup>e</sup> 22. Heb. _sin_

## Chapter 6

<sup>1</sup> My son, if thou be surety for thy friend,<br>_If_ thou hast stricken thy hand with a stranger,<br>
<sup>2</sup> Thou art snared with the words of thy mouth,<br>Thou art taken with the words of thy mouth.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Do this now, my son, and deliver thyself,<br>When thou art come into the hand of thy friend;<br>Go, humble thyself, and make sure thy friend.<sup>a</sup><br>
<sup>4</sup> Give not sleep to thine eyes,<br>Nor slumber to thine eyelids.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Deliver thyself as a roe from the hand _of the hunter_,<br>And as a bird from the hand of the fowler.<br>
<sup>6</sup> Go to the ant, thou sluggard;<br>Consider her ways, and be wise:<br>
<sup>7</sup> Which having no guide,<br>Overseer, or ruler,<br>
<sup>8</sup> Provideth her meat in the summer,<br>_And_ gathereth her food in the harvest.<br>
<sup>9</sup> How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard?<br>When wilt thou arise out of thy sleep?<br>
<sup>10</sup> _Yet_ a little sleep, a little slumber,<br>A little folding of the hands to sleep:<br>
<sup>11</sup> So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth,<br>And thy want as an armed man.<br>
<sup>12</sup> A naughty person, a wicked man,<br>Walketh with a froward mouth.<br>
<sup>13</sup> He winketh with his eyes, he speaketh with his feet,<br>He teacheth with his fingers;<br>
<sup>14</sup> Frowardness _is_ in his heart, he deviseth mischief continually;<br>He soweth<sup>b</sup> discord.<br>
<sup>15</sup> Therefore shall his calamity come suddenly;<br>Suddenly shall he be broken without remedy.<br>
<sup>16</sup> These six _things_ doth the LORD hate:<br>Yea, seven _are_ an abomination unto him:<sup>c</sup><br>
<sup>17</sup> A proud look,<sup>d</sup> a lying tongue,<br>And hands that shed innocent blood,<br>
<sup>18</sup> An heart that deviseth wicked imaginations,<br>Feet that be swift in running to mischief,<br>
<sup>19</sup> A false witness _that_ speaketh lies,<br>And he that soweth discord among brethren.<br>
<sup>20</sup> My son, keep thy father’s commandment,<br>And forsake not the law of thy mother:<br>
<sup>21</sup> Bind them continually upon thine heart,<br>_And_ tie them about thy neck.<br>
<sup>22</sup> When thou goest, it shall lead thee;<br>When thou sleepest, it shall keep thee;<br>And _when_ thou awakest, it shall talk with thee.<br>
<sup>23</sup> For the commandment _is_ a lamp;<sup>e</sup> and the law _is_ light;<br>And reproofs of instruction _are_ the way of life:<br>
<sup>24</sup> To keep thee from the evil woman,<br>From the flattery of the tongue of a strange woman.<sup>f</sup><br>
<sup>25</sup> Lust not after her beauty in thine heart;<br>Neither let her take thee with her eyelids.<br>
<sup>26</sup> For by means of a whorish woman _a man is brought_ to a piece of bread:<br>And the adulteress<sup>g</sup> will hunt for the precious life.<br>
<sup>27</sup> Can a man take fire in his bosom,<br>And his clothes not be burned?<br>
<sup>28</sup> Can one go upon hot coals,<br>And his feet not be burned?<br>
<sup>29</sup> So he that goeth in to his neighbour’s wife;<br>Whosoever toucheth her shall not be innocent.<br>
<sup>30</sup> _Men_ do not despise a thief, if he steal<br>To satisfy his soul when he is hungry;<br>
<sup>31</sup> But _if_ he be found, he shall restore sevenfold;<br>He shall give all the substance of his house.<br>
<sup>32</sup> _But_ whoso committeth adultery with a woman lacketh understanding:<sup>h</sup><br>He _that_ doeth it destroyeth his own soul.<br>
<sup>33</sup> A wound and dishonour shall he get;<br>And his reproach shall not be wiped away.<br>
<sup>34</sup> For jealousy _is_ the rage of a man:<br>Therefore he will not spare in the day of vengeance.<br>
<sup>35</sup> He will not regard any ransom;<sup>i</sup><br>Neither will he rest content, though thou givest many gifts.<br>

## Chapter 7

<sup>1</sup> My son, keep my words,<br>And lay up my commandments with thee.<br>
<sup>2</sup> Keep my commandments, and live;<br>And my law as the apple of thine eye.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Bind them upon thy fingers,<br>Write them upon the table of thine heart.<br>
<sup>4</sup> Say unto wisdom, Thou _art_ my sister;<br>And call understanding _thy_ kinswoman:<br>
<sup>5</sup> That they may keep thee from the strange woman,<br>From the stranger _which_ flattereth with her words.<br>
<sup>6</sup> For at the window of my house<br>I looked through my casement,<br>
<sup>7</sup> And beheld among the simple ones,<br>I discerned among the youths,<sup>a</sup><br>A young man void of understanding,<br>
<sup>8</sup> Passing through the street near her corner;<br>And he went the way to her house,<br>
<sup>9</sup> In the twilight, in the evening,<sup>b</sup><br>In the black and dark night:<br>
<sup>10</sup> And, behold, there met him a woman<br>_With_ the attire of an harlot, and subtil of heart.<br>
<sup>11</sup> (She _is_ loud and stubborn;<br>Her feet abide not in her house:<br>
<sup>12</sup> Now _is she_ without, now in the streets,<br>And lieth in wait at every corner.)<br>
<sup>13</sup> So she caught him, and kissed him,<br>_And_ with an impudent face said unto him,<br>
<sup>14</sup> _I have_ peace offerings with me;<sup>c</sup><br>This day have I payed my vows.<br>
<sup>15</sup> Therefore came I forth to meet thee,<br>Diligently to seek thy face, and I have found thee.<br>
<sup>16</sup> I have decked my bed with coverings of tapestry,<br>With carved _works_, with fine linen of Egypt.<br>
<sup>17</sup> I have perfumed my bed<br>With myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.<br>
<sup>18</sup> Come, let us take our fill of love until the morning:<br>Let us solace ourselves with loves.<br>
<sup>19</sup> For the goodman _is_ not at home,<br>He is gone a long journey:<br>
<sup>20</sup> He hath taken a bag of money with him,<sup>d</sup><br>_And_ will come home at the day appointed.<sup>e</sup><br>
<sup>21</sup> With her much fair speech she caused him to yield,<br>With the flattering of her lips she forced him.<br>
<sup>22</sup> He goeth after her straightway,<sup>f</sup><br>As an ox goeth to the slaughter,<br>Or as a fool to the correction of the stocks;<br>
<sup>23</sup> Till a dart strike through his liver;<br>As a bird hasteth to the snare,<br>And knoweth not that it _is_ for his life.<br>
<sup>24</sup> Hearken unto me now therefore, O ye children,<br>And attend to the words of my mouth.<br>
<sup>25</sup> Let not thine heart decline to her ways,<br>Go not astray in her paths.<br>

## Chapter 25

### Proverbs of Solomon

<sup>1</sup> These _are_ also proverbs of Solomon, which the men of Hezekiah king of Judah copied out.<br>
<sup>2</sup> _It is_ the glory of God to conceal a thing:<br>But the honour of kings _is_ to search out a matter.<br>
<sup>3</sup> The heaven for height, and the earth for depth,<br>And the heart of kings _is_ unsearchable.<sup>a</sup><br>
<sup>4</sup> Take away the dross from the silver,<br>And there shall come forth a vessel for the finer.<br>
<sup>5</sup> Take away the wicked _from_ before the king,<br>And his throne shall be established in righteousness.<br>
<sup>6</sup> Put not forth thyself<sup>b</sup> in the presence of the king,<br>And stand not in the place of great _men:_<br>
<sup>7</sup> For better _it is_ that it be said unto thee, Come up hither;<br>Than that thou shouldest be put lower in the presence of the prince<br>Whom thine eyes have seen.<br>
<sup>8</sup> Go not forth hastily to strive,<br>Lest _thou know not_ what to do in the end thereof,<br>When thy neighbour hath put thee to shame.<br>
<sup>9</sup> Debate thy cause with thy neighbour _himself_;<br>And discover not a secret to another:<sup>c</sup><br>
<sup>10</sup> Lest he that heareth _it_ put thee to shame,<br>And thine infamy turn not away.<br>
<sup>11</sup> A word fitly spoken<sup>d</sup><br>_Is like_ apples of gold in pictures of silver.<br>
<sup>12</sup> _As_ an earring of gold, and an ornament of fine gold,<br>_So is_ a wise reprover upon an obedient ear.<br>
<sup>13</sup> As the cold of snow in the time of harvest,<br>_So is_ a faithful messenger to them that send him:<br>For he refresheth the soul of his masters.<br>
<sup>14</sup> Whoso boasteth himself of a false gift<sup>e</sup><br>_Is like_ clouds and wind without rain.<br>
<sup>15</sup> By long forbearing is a prince persuaded,<br>And a soft tongue breaketh the bone.<br>
<sup>16</sup> Hast thou found honey? eat so much as is sufficient for thee,<br>Lest thou be filled therewith, and vomit it.<br>
<sup>17</sup> Withdraw thy foot from thy neighbour’s house;<br>Lest he be weary of thee,<sup>f</sup> and _so_ hate thee.<br>
<sup>18</sup> A man that beareth false witness against his neighbour<br>_Is_ a maul, and a sword, and a sharp arrow.<br>
<sup>19</sup> Confidence in an unfaithful man in time of trouble<br>_Is like_ a broken tooth, and a foot out of joint.<br>
<sup>20</sup> _As_ he that taketh away a garment in cold weather, _and as_ vinegar upon nitre,<br>So _is_ he that singeth songs to an heavy heart.<br>
<sup>21</sup> If thine enemy be hungry, give him bread to eat;<br>And if he be thirsty, give him water to drink:<br>

<sup>14</sup> She is like the merchants’ ships;<br>She bringeth her food from afar.<br>
<sup>15</sup> She riseth also while it is yet night,<br>And giveth meat to her household,<br>And a portion to her maidens.<br>
<sup>16</sup> She considereth a field, and buyeth<sup>e</sup> it:<br>With the fruit of her hands she planteth a vineyard.<br>
<sup>17</sup> She girdeth her loins with strength,<br>And strengtheneth her arms.<br>
<sup>18</sup> She perceiveth<sup>f</sup> that her merchandise _is_ good:<br>Her candle goeth not out by night.<br>
<sup>19</sup> She layeth her hands to the spindle,<br>And her hands hold the distaff.<br>
<sup>20</sup> She stretcheth out her hand to the poor;<br>Yea, she reacheth forth her hands to the needy.<br>
<sup>21</sup> She is not afraid of the snow for her household:<br>For all her household _are_ clothed with scarlet.<sup>g</sup><br>
<sup>22</sup> She maketh herself coverings of tapestry;<br>Her clothing _is_ silk and purple.<br>
<sup>23</sup> Her husband is known in the gates,<br>When he sitteth among the elders of the land.<br>
<sup>24</sup> She maketh fine linen, and selleth _it_;<br>And delivereth girdles unto the merchant.<br>
<sup>25</sup> Strength and honour _are_ her clothing;<br>And she shall rejoice in time to come.<br>
<sup>26</sup> She openeth her mouth with wisdom;<br>And in her tongue _is_ the law of kindness.<br>
<sup>27</sup> She looketh well to the ways of her household,<br>And eateth not the bread of idleness.<br>
<sup>28</sup> Her children arise up, and call her blessed;<br>Her husband _also_, and he praiseth her.<br>
<sup>29</sup> Many daughters have done virtuously,<sup>h</sup><br>But thou excellest them all.<br>
<sup>30</sup> Favour _is_ deceitful, and beauty _is_ vain:<br>_But_ a woman _that_ feareth the LORD, she shall be praised.<br>
<sup>31</sup> Give her of the fruit of her hands;<br>And let her own works praise her in the gates.<br>
