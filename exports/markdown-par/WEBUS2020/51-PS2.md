## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> <sup>a</sup>> _This Psalm is a genuine one of David, though extra,<sup>b</sup> composed when he fought in single combat with Goliath._

I was small among my brothers,<br>and youngest in my father’s house.<br>I tended my father’s sheep.<br>
<sup>2</sup> My hands formed a musical instrument,<br>and my fingers tuned a lyre.<br>
<sup>3</sup> Who shall tell my Lord?<br>The Lord himself, he himself hears.<br>
<sup>4</sup> He sent forth his angel and took me from my father’s sheep,<br>and he anointed me with his anointing oil.<br>
<sup>5</sup> My brothers were handsome and tall;<br>but the Lord didn’t take pleasure in them.<br>
<sup>6</sup> I went out to meet the Philistine,<br>and he cursed me by his idols.<br>
<sup>7</sup> But I drew his own sword and beheaded him,<br>and removed reproach from the children of Israel.

> - <sup>a</sup> 1. _Psalm 151_ is recognized as Deuterocanonical Scripture by the Greek Orthodox and Russian Orthodox Churches.
> - <sup>b</sup> Subtitle. or, supernumerary
